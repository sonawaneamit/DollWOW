# Irontech customization audit — 2026-08-13

## Scope and evidence

- 248 current Rosemary-linked Irontech source pages audited for pricing and option evidence.
- 421 live Irontech-labeled DollWOW catalog records swept through the normalized runtime configuration; 419 expose a compatible head selector and paid extra-head selector. The standalone IronAI head keeps its five-model manufacturer list, and the Drake Von male build remains on its product-specific male head rather than receiving a female standard library.
- Current retail guidance reviewed against RosemaryDoll's Irontech TPE and silicone collections and YourDoll's Irontech collection.
- Product-specific supplier/dealer option sets remain the compatibility boundary; this work does not assume every Irontech body supports every option.
- Rosemary's live Irontech selector was used as the master standard-head source because it currently exposes the broadest accessible image-backed library: 65 TPE head identities and 37 silicone head identities. Legacy standard TPE forms are expanded through head 102, and compatible standard female silicone builds whose older form omitted the selector receive the current silicone library. Male, Oriental, robotic, ROS-family, and other special heads remain isolated to products whose source forms support them.
- No official Irontech factory price list was available for this pass. Dealer prices are therefore recorded as current guidance, not permanent factory truth.

## Checkout normalization

- `Choose a Head` is single-select. Switching the compatible head identity is included; a silicone-material or special-construction surcharge remains visible where the source distinguishes it.
- `Add Extra Head` is multi-select and always paid. Current dealer guidance used is $375 for a standard TPE extra head and $299 for a standard silicone extra head, with higher product-specific source prices preserved where present.
- Dealer `An Extra Free Head` promotional language is removed from the DollWOW checkout model.
- Head-specific follow-up fields from dealer forms are not repeated as unrelated configurator steps.
- Existing approved DollWOW prices remain authoritative: ULW $195 and IronAI $119.
- Dealer-confirmed included appearance and base-construction selectors use a verified $0 delta rather than disappearing from checkout: skin, eyes, nails, areola/nipple color and size, vagina color/texture, hair color, included breast/standing/version choices, and the supported male size choices.
- Surface options with real image references are Visualizer-ready; head switching remains deliberately non-visualizable until the separate head-swap workflow is validated.
- The post-normalization runtime sweep found zero ordinary known Irontech choices left in an unpriced checkout-interruption state.
