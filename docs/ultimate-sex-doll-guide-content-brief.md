## Search Intent Architecture

- The flagship guide owns the complete selection and ownership journey.
- Collection pages own high-volume transactional terms such as realistic, silicone, mini, male, torso, custom, and ready-to-ship dolls.
- Supporting Learning Center pages own narrow questions about cleaning, storage, materials, skeletons, cost, scams, privacy, and customization.
- Brand hubs own manufacturer-name searches and current catalog facts.
- The guide summarizes each specialist topic, gives a direct answer, then links to its deeper page using descriptive anchor text.
- Do not duplicate full supporting articles inside the flagship guide.

## Match And Exceed Requirements

- A direct 40-80 word answer within the first screen.
- A linked table of contents with stable chapter anchors.
- HTML comparison tables, decision grids, checklists, and concise definitions.
- At least 24 verified product examples using current Shopify images and links.
- Product facts rendered as text, not trapped inside images.
- Measurements shown in `ft/in + cm`, `lb + kg`, and `F + C` where relevant.
- Material and care caveats tied to the exact manufacturer or product when formulations differ.
- No unsupported psychological, medical, safety, hypoallergenic, odor-free, delivery, or durability claims.
- No fake reviews, invented testing, or invented buyer personas.
- No competitor backlinks from commercial comparison copy.
- Clear Alex authorship, Jesse review attribution, last-reviewed date, sources, and revision history.
- Article, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, ItemList, ImageObject, and Person/Organization schema where applicable.
- A canonical web URL; the PDF links to it and is marked as the downloadable edition.
