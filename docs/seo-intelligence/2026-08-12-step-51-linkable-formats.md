## Baseline And Noise Control

- DollWow currently has 4 backlinks from 4 referring domains. That is a normal pre-authority baseline for a new domain, not a content-quality verdict.

- A format was retained only when the most-linked-page data showed a plausible reason for independent sites to reference it. A large backlink count alone did not qualify a page.
- This audit identifies assets worth producing. It does not approve outreach targets, competitor links on DollWow, or copied editorial structures.

## Immediate Content Decisions

1. Do not create another broad beginner guide, material guide, cleaning guide, warehouse guide, or legal guide. Improve the existing canonical owner instead.
2. Keep collection pages as the canonical owners for commercial category intent. Add original buying help and Learning Center links without turning them into repetitive essays.
3. Validate an original `2026 Sex Doll Size, Weight & Price Index` using current U.S. search demand and SERP composition. Candidate sections include weight distributions, height bands, price ranges by material, product-form differences, and brand-level ranges where the catalog evidence is complete enough.
4. Build the index only from current first-party catalog data, publish its methodology and review date, use both U.S. and metric units, and avoid popularity or quality claims that the data cannot support.
5. Treat factory, review, unboxing, and creator media as a later proof program. Do not manufacture social proof for launch.
6. Turn Care 365 and related approved guarantees into stable, linkable proof pages after the frontend implementation confirms the exact operational terms.
