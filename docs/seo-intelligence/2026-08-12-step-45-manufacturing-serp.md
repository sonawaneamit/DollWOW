## Decision

- Three US desktop queries were checked at a recorded cost of `$0.0105`.
- Results consistently reward explanatory guides, factory tours, and video. The DollWow page should therefore answer the manufacturing question directly and explain what each stage means for a buyer, supported by a useful process visual.
- The page owns informational intent around how dolls are made. Commercial searches such as `silicone doll factory` should support `/shop/silicone` rather than be forced into this guide.
- Official Irontech and Tantaly material provides the primary-source boundary. Their documented methods apply to their products and must not be presented as universal factory practice.
- The manuscript is prepared but hidden pending approval of one mobile-first process visual. This avoids launching another text-only guide against image- and video-rich results.
