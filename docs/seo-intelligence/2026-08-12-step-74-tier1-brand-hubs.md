## Findings

1. All six hubs returned `200`, used self-referencing canonicals, had one H1, contained 803 to 925 readable words, linked to relevant guides and buying paths, and rendered substantial real-product imagery.
2. DollWow did not appear in the sampled Bing top 20 or sampled brand answers. Google and ChatGPT LLM Mentions also returned a zero baseline. This is the post-release citation-growth baseline, not evidence that the pages need indiscriminate length.
3. Ambiguous short brand names caused severe search pollution. Bing interpreted `wm dolls` as Waste Management, `irontech dolls` as software/package intent, `se doll` as unrelated brands and products, and `tantaly dolls` as unrelated results. Titles should state the adult product category and defining material or form.
4. AI answers organized brand evaluation around material, product form, body/head compatibility, measurements and handling weight, authenticity or seller proof, model-specific options, arrival evidence, care, and repair support. Existing DollWow hubs already cover these decisions.
5. Merchant results were useful only as a discovery diagnostic. Broad brand phrases returned toys, collectibles, reborn dolls, and unrelated products. They do not justify unsupported bestseller, review, or popularity claims.
6. Strict all-competitor page intersections returned no shared linking pages. No public copy was created from that absence and no backlink URL was removed from the existing uncensored outreach exports.
7. The live product grids are the correct visual layer for these commercial hubs because they show actual carried products, current prices, and brand-specific imagery.
