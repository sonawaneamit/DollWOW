# Dolls Castle Brand Hub Match And Exceed

Date: 2026-08-11

## Decision

- Keep `/brands/dolls-castle` as the sole commercial owner for adult Dolls Castle brand intent.
- Do not create a separate brand guide yet. The live hub can satisfy the current commercial query set without splitting authority or repeating the same catalog and buyer guidance.
- Treat the bare query `dolls castle` carefully. Its result page mixes adult products with children's toys, castles, and dollhouses, so its apparent demand cannot be counted as wholly relevant adult traffic.

## Data basis

- Refreshed `dolls castle`, `dolls castle sex doll`, and `dolls castle dolls` through DataForSEO for US desktop at depth 20 for $0.0105.
- The adult-qualified searches show clear brand and retailer-collection intent. Repeated result themes include the official manufacturer, realistic and fantasy products, TPE, full silicone, hybrid construction, compact and full-size formats, customization, and authenticity checks.
- Ran focused Backlinks and OnPage calls for $0.0489. Total recorded Step 18 DataForSEO cost: $0.0594.
- The manufacturer domain returned 20,609 backlinks from 171 referring domains at collection time. Much of the profile is noisy or low quality, so raw backlink count is not treated as evidence of authority by itself.
- The most useful official link patterns were the authorized-seller directory, anti-counterfeit verification system, custom-build information, and distinctive product pages. These informed trust and buyer-help sections without copying competitor prose.
- DataForSEO OnPage confirmed the pre-upgrade DollWow URL was public with HTTP 200, one H1, a self-referencing canonical, 159 products, relevant metadata, and 758 words of crawlable content.

## Claims gates

- DollWow has a Dolls Castle authorization certificate in the repository, so approved-seller language is supported and the certificate remains visible on the hub.
- No founding year is used. Retailer pages repeat a date, but an official dated source was not retained for this release.
- Material, fantasy, compact-format, and customization statements remain range-level guidance. Each product page must confirm the exact construction and supported options.
- No popularity, quality, bestseller, shipping-time, or universal feature claims were inferred from result order or catalog breadth.

## Implemented

- Replaced generic brand text with Dolls Castle-specific realistic-versus-fantasy, TPE, full-silicone, hybrid, body-and-head pairing, dimensions, listed weight, care, authenticity, and customization guidance.
- Added three buyer notes, three comparison rows, six customer-facing FAQs with schema, relevant Learning Center paths, live catalog examples, and proactive missing-model sourcing through live chat and `hello@dollwow.com`.
- Extended the reusable brand-intelligence collector and SERP competitor mapper so future Dolls Castle refreshes retain the correct query ownership and report labels.

## Validation

- TypeScript, the customer-copy audit, and all 163 tests passed.
- Desktop visual QA at 1280 x 720 showed the full copy and authorization certificate without overflow.
- Mobile visual QA at 390 x 844 found no horizontal overflow but showed that the complete introduction delayed the first products. The frontend task received a scoped handoff to reuse the SSR-accessible mobile disclosure pattern while preserving the text and desktop layout.
