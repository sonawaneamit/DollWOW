## DollWow Decision

- Keep `/shop/custom` as the sole commercial owner for the measured 3,600-volume `custom sex dolls` cluster.
- Expand `/learn/custom-sex-dolls` as the supporting educational owner for customization process, option, compatibility, and pre-production questions.
- Do not imitate the polluted `sex doll customization` result set or create synonym pages. The useful commercial and guide results support one collection and one guide.
- Organize the guide around base model, body and head, material, appearance, skeleton and feet, electronics, conflicts, included versus paid options, final price, Human Build Check, production, eligible factory media, and Doll Passport records.
- Keep the relevant live custom-product module because base-model selection is the first actionable step. The shared classifier now excludes partial-body products from this full-doll path.
- Require product-specific manufacturer confirmation and avoid implying every brand option applies to every SKU.
