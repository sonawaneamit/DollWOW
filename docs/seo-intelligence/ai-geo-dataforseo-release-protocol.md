# AI/GEO DataForSEO Release Protocol

Updated: 2026-08-14

## Rule

AI/GEO optimization is mandatory for every new or materially refreshed indexable page. Cost is not a reason to omit relevant intelligence. Every API call must still answer a documented decision question, and weak or polluted outputs must be rejected.

## Required Relevant Evidence

1. Google and Bing keyword demand, variants, trends, and conversational questions.
2. DataForSEO Labs competitor keywords, relevant pages, intersections, gaps, and canonical ownership evidence.
3. U.S. desktop and mobile organic results, page types, PAA, related searches, snippets, discussions, video, image, shopping, and AI Overview presence.
4. Google AI Mode and AI Overview results for principal buyer questions.
5. AI Keyword Data demand and trend estimates.
6. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity LLM Responses with web search and citations where supported.
7. LLM Scraper and LLM Mentions evidence for brand, domain, page, and source visibility.
8. Content Analysis for useful semantic, quality, and freshness gaps. Reject off-topic or polluted results.
9. Focused OnPage checks for the canonical URL; full-site crawls only when justified.
10. Backlinks and Domain Analytics for flagship, research, tools, datasets, and major commercial pages.
11. Merchant evidence for shopping-led intent.
12. DollWow Shopify, policies, GSC, GA4, Bing Webmaster, customer questions, and original datasets.

Business Data, App Data, Amazon Labs, and other specialized endpoints are conditional. Use them when they answer the page's actual question rather than to claim every API was called.

## Page Acceptance Criteria

- One unambiguous canonical owner and winning page type.
- Direct answer near the top and natural conversational question coverage.
- Clear entities, definitions, dates, units, product identity, and source ownership.
- Important facts in server-rendered HTML, not only images or interactive controls.
- Standards-based discovery of `/llms.txt` and `/agent-index.json`, plus a Markdown alternate when the page family has an approved representation.
- A source-HTML check confirming that the direct answer, identity, principal facts, and canonical next step remain usable without JavaScript.
- Privacy-safe search and AI crawler observability on public content routes, with a post-release baseline where available.
- Extractable headings, lists, comparisons, FAQs, and next steps where useful.
- Primary or controlling citations for factual claims and matching Article citations when applicable.
- Current catalog facts and live handling of price, availability, quantity, and delivery claims.
- Appropriate visible-content-backed schema only.
- Original information gain through catalog analysis, real examples, expert interpretation, maintained data, policies, or tools.
- Descriptive image alt text/captions and equivalent important facts in HTML.
- Customer-forward copy with no internal SEO, schema, prompt, PDP, SERP, or crawlability language.

`llms.txt`, query endpoints, sitewide Markdown alternates, and content negotiation are retrieval aids, not ranking claims. Keep canonical HTML authoritative, generate Markdown from the same public server-rendered main content, make alternate representations factually equivalent, and mark noncanonical Markdown `noindex, follow`.

## Evidence Record

Store target search and prompt clusters, demand, winning pages, ranking and cited sources, AI answers, mentions, citation gaps, rejected evidence, catalog proof, canonical action, endpoints, dates, success/failure, costs, and artifact locations.

## Statuses

- `research pending`
- `content ready; AI/GEO validation pending`
- `AI/GEO validated; visual package pending`
- `released; monitoring pending`
- `complete; recurring monitoring`

## Backfill And Monitoring

Backfill flagship assets, datasets and tools first; then Tier 1 collections and brand hubs; then high-demand editorial content; then Tier 2 pages. Record publication baselines. Monitor GSC/Bing weekly and AI keyword demand, mentions, citations, model answers, AI Overview/AI Mode, competitor gaps, and refresh needs monthly.

AI responses are competitive intelligence, not factual authority. Verify adopted claims against current DollWow data, policies, primary manufacturer evidence, law or regulation where relevant, or a disclosed original methodology.
