## Adopted content decisions

- Lead with product form, construction, dimensions, weight, availability, and model-specific options.
- Explain conventional full dolls, torso formats, and UniSeries without inventing specs or applying one model's feature set to the entire brand.
- Provide six extractable buyer questions, a three-row comparison, handling guidance, and a prominent official-store CTA.
- Keep prices dynamic in Shopify and omit COGS, copied official-store pricing, promotions, founding claims, sales claims, and universal compatibility claims.
- Suppress authorization messaging because no Fanreal certificate or approval evidence is stored in the repository.

## Rejected evidence

- Official-store prices and promotions: volatile and not authoritative for DollWow.
- Official-store audience counts, geographic reach, experience claims, production timing, delivery timing, and warranty language: unnecessary for this hub and not safe to generalize to DollWow orders.
- Retailer authorization, review, popularity, and quality claims: not primary evidence.
- Any suggestion that Fanreal is Avant or shares Avant authorization: false canonical grouping.

- Store must record current seller approval or certificate evidence before any authorization card or authorized-vendor listing is enabled.
- Run the full AI/GEO evidence pass and focused OnPage validation against the deployed candidate.
