## DollWow Decision

- Expand `/learn/implanted-hair-vs-wig` as the single educational owner for implanted hair, wig comparison, care, and compatibility questions.
- Keep `/shop/custom` as the commercial owner. Do not create a separate wig collection unless live catalog inventory and qualified demand support it.
- Cover flexibility, hairline, replacement, cleaning, storage, styling risk, price, production, exact-head compatibility, electronics, product-photo interpretation, and repair rather than repeating a generic realism claim.
- Preserve manufacturer-confirmation gates. Do not imply that every head supports implanted hair, every doll includes a wig, or a universal care routine is safe.
- Remove the generic custom-product carousel because unrelated base models do not prove that a hair option is supported. Use the approved comparison visual, custom collection, support, and configuration links instead.
- Use the customer question as the conversion path: send the exact product and desired look through live chat or email so DollWow can verify the head-specific option before production.
