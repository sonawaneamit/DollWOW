### Reference guide review

The RosemaryDoll reference PDF is 120 pages and approximately 15.2 MB. Its strongest feature is visual density: most pages use one or more product or brand images, short blocks of text, strong chapter changes, and magazine-like pacing. It covers materials, price, customization, brands, scam avoidance, unboxing, use, cleaning, storage, FAQs, and future technology.

Its weaknesses create the Exceed opportunity:

- Unsupported absolute claims about safety, allergies, odor, durability, heat, lifespan, and material behavior.
- Psychological and relationship claims presented without sources or appropriate qualification.
- No transparent recommendation methodology, evidence hierarchy, author expertise, review process, or citation system.
- Numerous grammar and accuracy problems, duplicated copy, inconsistent units, and promotional claims presented as universal facts.
- A very long brand catalog containing brands DollWOW does not carry.
- Useful information is often trapped in images or thin page spreads rather than available as structured HTML.
- Limited canonical ownership and internal-link architecture across commercial and educational intents.

DollWOW should match the visual richness and breadth, then exceed it through current catalog facts, clearer decisions, cited limitations, practical tools, stronger authorship, dual units, accessibility, and an intentionally connected web architecture.
