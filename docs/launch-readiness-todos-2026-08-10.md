# DollWow launch-readiness decisions

These items were intentionally not guessed during the August 10 storefront audit. They need a business, supplier, legal, or Shopify decision before implementation.

## P0 — settle before taking orders

- [ ] Approve one canonical order timeline for ready-to-ship and custom products: stock confirmation, payment capture, production, factory photos, balance/final approval, dispatch, and delivery.
- [ ] Approve cancellation, change-order, return, damage-claim, and warranty rules. Reconcile the current 24-hour claim language with the complete process and remedies.
- [ ] Confirm the legal company name, business/contact address, support email, and any phone or scheduled-call option that should appear in policies and checkout.
- [ ] Confirm the exact neutral card statement descriptor, supported payment methods, plain-box promise, carriers, and signature requirements.
- [ ] Review every authorization claim against evidence shown on the authorization page. Remove or qualify brands without a public certificate or equivalent supplier proof.
- [ ] Audit duplicate catalog records, especially identical titles with different prices or configurations. Preserve genuinely different material, head, body, and technology versions with explicit differentiating titles.
- [ ] Standardize custom-option pricing and production-time language. A product must not promise a checkout price while its choices still say “price confirmation required.”
- [ ] Disable checkout tipping in Shopify unless it is an intentional business decision.

## P1 — high-value trust and comparison work

- [ ] Design a real product-to-product comparison workspace for saved dolls. Keep competitor price matching as a separate task.
- [ ] Replace technical custom-charge line items at checkout with a buyer-friendly configuration summary while retaining complete order data for operations.
- [ ] Decide which PDP facts are authoritative when source data conflicts: warehouse location, dispatch estimate, delivery estimate, weight, and included options.
- [ ] Add a complete privacy notice covering analytics, retention, processors, customer rights, contact details, and how a visitor changes consent.
- [ ] Define a support response-time promise and escalation path suitable for first-time buyers.
- [ ] Add verified customer reviews only after genuine orders exist; do not seed invented testimonials.

## P2 — next conversion pass

- [ ] Add an in-page PDP section navigator with active-section state and a compact purchase dock after the hero.
- [ ] Further compress included/default options into an expandable “included” group and show price/compatibility changes accessibly.
- [ ] Move the competitor price-match form immediately below its heading and prefill the referred product.
- [ ] Add pause controls to autoplay media and confirm every animation respects reduced-motion preferences.
- [ ] Finish keyboard/focus testing for search and the mobile menu, including focus trapping, Escape, inert background, and focus restoration.
- [ ] Revisit the 36-product page size after real performance and engagement data is available; add incremental loading only if it improves discovery without recreating the unbounded catalog.
