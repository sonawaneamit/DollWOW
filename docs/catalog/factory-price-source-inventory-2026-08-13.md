## Pricing rule

1. Current manufacturer retail/minimum-advertised price, when explicitly supplied.
2. Current product-specific Rosemary retail option price.
3. Current product-specific YourDoll retail option price when Rosemary lacks the choice.
4. Factory/wholesale price only as compatibility and cost-floor evidence—not as an automatic customer price.
5. No numeric evidence means the choice stays in the factory record but is not offered in online checkout.

This preserves margin and prevents an ambiguous option from replacing Add to Cart with an “ask our team” conversion stop.
