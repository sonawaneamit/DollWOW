# Existing-brand product listing checklist

Use one copy per sellable SKU. Record evidence and approved exceptions in Linear. Send the exact private headless preview only after all applicable pre-review checks are complete.

Product / SKU · Linear issue · Prepared by · Date · Verified Vercel deployment · Approval reference

## Source, identity and pricing

Confirm the exact sellable configuration before writing or importing.

- [ ] **Check the queue and duplicates.** Read the latest Linear decisions, the brand playbook and active work. Match existing records by supplier SKU, body, head, market and stock type. Reuse the correct draft.
- [ ] **Verify the exact build.** Record supplier, model and SKU, body and head codes, material, dimensions, net and packed weight, and factory inclusions. Reconcile disagreements and label measurements that vary by head.
- [ ] **Verify sourcing and media rights.** Save dated factory evidence and media authorization. Confirm that gallery images, videos, swatches and charts show the exact model or identify the applicable reference.
- [ ] **Record the commercial basis.** Verify dealer cost, freight or approved estimate, retail floor, customer shipping and delivery terms. Show gross margin at base and contribution after applicable promotions and fees as directed.
- [ ] **Verify availability and eligibility.** Check made-to-order or warehouse stock, applicable destinations and lead times. Confirm campaign eligibility and exclusions before publication.
- [ ] **Save the draft identity and evidence.** Record Shopify product and variant IDs, exact price and currency, source links, and the issue checkpoint. Keep the draft unpublished through review.

## Options, tiers and storefront QA

Run checks on the actual DollWOW product route in the protected Vercel deployment.

- [ ] **Reconcile the whole option menu.** Start with the existing menu for the same brand, body type, material, gender and series. Reuse it across photographed head pairings, keeping the product’s own default. Check supplier-documented exceptions and map each choice to SKU, availability, reference, included/paid status, amount and valid combinations.
- [ ] **Validate head swaps and extra heads.** Keep the photographed head as default. Verify replacement compatibility, skin, head-specific features and price. Treat a second head as a separate purchase and verify its named charge.
- [ ] **Build three meaningful setups where applicable.** Show the key factory inclusions on Starter. Add supported build changes to Enthusiast and Collector. Use relevant care items when useful. Document fixed builds or source limits in Linear.
- [ ] **Test selections and totals.** Select each tier on desktop and mobile. Check its listed choices, resolved total, default state, manual edits and conditional branches. Verify hidden or incompatible choices cannot reach checkout.
- [ ] **Verify checkout and shipment handling.** Use the exact parent and named add-on variants. Check currency, amounts, quantities, availability, order properties and promo allocation. Use non-mutating tests during private review. Record any separately authorized checkout run.
- [ ] **Inspect the actual page and all assets.** Check desktop and phone layouts, keyboard access, price and purchase controls, gallery order, playable video, swatches and editorial. Verify all expected media render in the deployed preview. Check DollVue eligibility where supported.

## SEO and AI search readiness

Keep evidence per canonical product URL. Use the existing DataForSEO release protocol.

- [ ] **Choose the canonical and target queries.** Check GSC for the brand and related products. Use DataForSEO demand, Labs and U.S. desktop/mobile SERPs to map model, body and buyer queries to the right PDP, collection or guide.
- [ ] **Record AI search evidence.** Check relevant AI Keyword Data, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, LLM answers and citations, and mentions or scraper data. Record endpoint, market, date, cost, result and what changed.
- [ ] **Write model-specific, verifiable content.** Use a distinct title, H1 and description. Put the product identity, price basis, availability, specifications, factory inclusions and meaningful choices in initial HTML. Use manufacturer-backed buyer answers and descriptive alt text.
- [ ] **Validate HTML and structured data.** Inspect the rendered headless HTML. Verify self-canonical production URL and matching Product, Offer and breadcrumb data where applicable. Compare price, currency, availability and identifiers with the actual page.
- [ ] **Check discovery and internal links.** Verify contextual links from the right brand or collection and useful supporting pages. Confirm production sitemap inclusion is expected, the draft preview is noindex, and approved AI discovery formats use equivalent public facts.
- [ ] **Record what is still unverified.** Log source gaps, failed provider calls and any unresolved canonicals or schema discrepancies. Record the starting GSC/Bing and AI search baseline. A private preview cannot establish public indexing or visibility.

## Review, publish and monitor

Complete all applicable pre-review checks before sending Martin the exact preview.

- [ ] **Close or document every pre-review item.** Mark each item complete with evidence in Linear. For a genuine exception, record why the item does not apply or get the specific decision needed. Open failures stay in the internal checkpoint.
- [ ] **Send a review-ready package.** Post one protected headless PDP link per model, exact tier totals and selections, shipping basis, media count, and SEO/GEO evidence. Include the tested deployment and any approved exceptions.
- [ ] **Record approval and refresh the checks.** Record the approved deployment, scope and price in Linear. Refresh product, menu, stock, campaign and charge data immediately before release.
- [ ] **Verify production after publishing.** Confirm the exact public URL and assets, collection links, prices, checkout routing, canonical, crawl directives, sitemap membership and structured data. Save the release receipt in Linear.
- [ ] **Recheck search visibility.** Inspect the public URL in GSC after Google reads it. Track indexing and enhancements, then measure impressions, clicks and AI citations in the recurring SEO/GEO review.

Use the [AI/GEO DataForSEO release protocol](../seo-intelligence/ai-geo-dataforseo-release-protocol.md) for provider selection and evidence retention.
