  generic full-body upsell configuration.

  and prices. Existing registry entries must remain intact.

creation-date ordering, model deduplication, discovery diversity and TDF brand
eligibility remain authoritative. Eileen's gallery refresh does not make her a
new arrival. Climax remains excluded from homepage product promotions under

## Operations

Temporary `dollwow-test` tags hide staged products from public catalog/PDP
queries during real Shopify checkout testing. Remove them only for the nine
explicitly approved products after the compatible release is deployed. Restore
the tag or DRAFT status for an affected product if a release check fails.
No orders, payments, supplier emails or unrelated discounts are changed.
