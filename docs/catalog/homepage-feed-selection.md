# Homepage feed selection

Homepage selection is automatic; do not hand-maintain product lists for these feeds.

- Preserve homepage brand and public-product eligibility filters.
- New Arrivals uses the newest-first Shopify feed. A known custom counterpart suppresses a new warehouse copy; regional copies use their earliest occurrence in the fetched window. This is bounded catalog evidence, not a complete historical model-launch date.
- Bestsellers preserves Shopify ranking, showing the highest-ranked listing for each model. Do not invent sales counts or randomize ranking.
- Discovery feeds favor models with fewer appearances across the page, then balance brands and available look/material metadata. Ties preserve catalog order.
- Reserve specialty and ready-stock candidates before broad female/male discovery. Sparse feeds may overlap; never pad a feed with duplicate warehouse variants.
- Rare Finds requires explicit rare/limited/specialty/elf/anime/hybrid/torso classification or height of at least 180cm. A generic special offer or 170cm height alone is not a specialty signal.
- Compare models across warehouse locations; preserve different model names and build specifications. Do not merge inventory records or change checkout availability.
- Test future warehouse imports, brand-heavy batches, sparse pools, ranked feed ordering, and cross-feed exposure. Verify the rendered desktop/mobile page as well as card counts.

No draft publication, merchandising claims, or visual redesign is authorized by this selection policy.
