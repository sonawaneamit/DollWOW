## Read Height And Weight Together

Height describes scale. Weight describes much of the daily ownership work.

Convert both units when comparing:

- 75 cm is about 2 ft 6 in
- 150 cm is about 4 ft 11 in
- 155 cm is about 5 ft 1 in
- 160 cm is about 5 ft 3 in
- 55 lb is about 25 kg
- 66 lb is about 30 kg
- 88 lb is about 40 kg
- 95 lb is about 43 kg

These conversions are reference points, not claims about every Piper body at that height. Use the listed weight on the exact product page.

Before ordering, rehearse the route from the delivery point to the private room. Consider stairs, narrow corners, door width, the cleaning surface, drying clearance, and a relaxed storage position. If you would not comfortably lift the listed weight from a low surface, plan a second person or choose a lighter verified build.

## How To Read A Piper Sex Doll Review

- Combines product, seller, and delivery complaints into one score
- Claims a feature applies to every Piper doll
- Gives no order date, ownership period, or original evidence

A negative report can reveal a useful question even when it cannot establish a universal brand problem. A positive report can show one successful order without proving every model or seller performs the same way.

## Care, Cleaning, And Storage

The exact Piper material controls the care routine. Do not use a TPE solvent, silicone adhesive, powder, oil, or cleaner simply because it worked on another doll.

For every build:

1. Confirm the body and head materials separately.
2. Use only compatible, mild cleaning products.
3. Support the weight during cleaning and turning.
4. Remove moisture completely before dressing or storage.
5. Keep dark or untested dyes away from long contact.
6. Avoid sharp hardware, tight straps, concentrated pressure, and heat.
7. Store in a relaxed position using light, clean fabric and product-specific support.

Electronics, implanted hair, painted finishes, removable inserts, seams, and special foot systems can change the routine. Ask before wetting, heating, powdering, or repairing an unfamiliar component.
