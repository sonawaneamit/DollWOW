# Full-Size TPE or Silicone Torso? Comparing Space, Weight and Cost

A silicone torso can be a better fit for a smaller home, while a full-size TPE doll offers the complete silhouette and clothing possibilities of a full body. Neither choice wins on material alone. Compare the physical form first, then the exact weight, storage position and complete price.

This is a decision about ownership footprint, not another material ranking. Our [TPE versus silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) covers the broader material question.

## Begin with what the product actually includes

"Torso" is not one fixed shape. Some designs include a head and shoulders; others concentrate on the central body, with different amounts of the hips or legs retained. The [DollWOW torso collection](/shop/torsos) separates that product form from hips products and full-body dolls.

Read the photographs alongside the dimensions. Establish whether the listed height includes a removable head and whether the product is measured upright or in another position. A short height figure does not tell you the width of the base or how far the chest projects when stored.

A full body offers arms and legs for dressing and display, but also needs room to support those limbs. If your main interest is a complete dressed presentation, a torso may not provide the shape you want even when it is easier to store.

## Use exact weights, not material assumptions

Two factory examples show why the distinction matters:

| --- | --- | --- |
| Dolls Castle Nyla 183CM E | 42.5 kg net | Full-size TPE listing |
| SEDOLL ST8 | 19.5 kg | Silicone torso body in the SET Series 2 table |

Sources: [Dolls Castle Nyla specifications](https://dolls-castle.com/product/curvy-slim-tpe-sex-doll-183cm-nyla/) and [SEDOLL SET Series 2](https://www.sedoll.com/sedoll-set-series-2-is-here-four-new-silicone-torso-designs-four-different-directions/), checked 16 October 2026. These are different forms, not matched products or a controlled material test. Confirm whether a chosen torso's figure includes the head and accessories before treating it as the assembled weight.

The smaller number can still represent a substantial object to move. A compact body may be awkward to grip, particularly when you need to reach across a cleaning surface. Plan support and assistance around the actual task, not just the weight on a page.

## Measure the whole storage envelope

Measure usable length, width and depth inside the intended storage space. Then allow for the manufacturer's resting position, padding, the head if included, and room to take the product out without scraping its finish.

A torso is promising when length is your limiting dimension. It is less helpful if the cupboard is narrow or the only available shelf is difficult to reach. A full body may fit a long supported surface better than a short, deep torso fits a shallow cabinet.

Also plan the space between cleaning and storage. A product that fits perfectly when dry still needs somewhere supported to dry after care. Keep the [storage guide](/learn/sex-doll-storage) beside your shortlist.

## Compare the complete cost of each choice

Use two current configurations in the same currency and for the same destination. Record the included head, required options, delivery charges and applicable taxes or duties. Leave an uncertain cost marked for confirmation rather than treating it as free.

Then add the ownership items you would genuinely need: an appropriate support surface, storage arrangement and compatible care supplies. A less expensive listing is not automatically the lower-cost choice if it needs a storage solution your home does not have.

Avoid comparing a promotional starting price with a fully configured total. You do not need a large accessory bundle to make the comparison fair; you need equivalent information about what each purchase includes.

## Think through one ordinary care session

Imagine taking the product out, placing it on its support, cleaning it, drying it and returning it to storage. Where do you need to bend, reach or change your grip? Which steps require another person's help?

Follow the exact material and finish instructions for each candidate. A silicone torso is not automatically suitable for immersion, and a removable head does not make every opening easy to dry. Electronics and painted finishes add their own restrictions. See the [cleaning guide](/learn/how-to-clean-a-sex-doll) for the wider routine.

## Choose the form you will enjoy owning

Choose a torso when its specific shape solves your space problem and provides the presentation you want. Choose a full body when complete proportions and dressing flexibility justify the extra space and handling arrangements.
