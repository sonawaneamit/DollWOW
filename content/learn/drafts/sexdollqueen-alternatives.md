## What In Stock Should Tell You

An in-stock or ready-to-ship listing should identify an existing product in a confirmed configuration. Before checkout, ask for:

1. current warehouse and stock confirmation
2. manufacturer, body, and head
3. material and skin tone
4. height, body measurements, and listed weight
5. installed skeleton, standing, hair, eyes, makeup, and other options
6. included accessories and care items
7. images of the exact unit where available
8. expected dispatch stage after order confirmation

The core configuration of an existing unit is usually fixed. A wig, clothing, or accessory may be changeable, but the body, head, material, tone, skeleton, or installed functions generally cannot be treated like a new custom build.

## Ready to Ship Is Not the Same as Delivered Tomorrow

Ready to ship removes or shortens the production stage. The complete journey can still include:

- order and stock confirmation
- warehouse handling and dispatch
- carrier transit
- customs where applicable
- appointment, signature, or final handoff

Weather, carrier operations, customs, address issues, and delivery access can affect timing. Ask for the current route to your destination rather than relying on one broad estimate.

## How to Read Reviews Without Being Misled

The strongest review evidence identifies the exact order and explains useful ownership details. Look for:

- brand, body, head, and material
- selected options
- whether the unit was ready stock or made to order
- order and delivery context
- original photographs connected to the product
- handling, cleaning, storage, and long-term observations
- balanced strengths and tradeoffs

A star rating without product identity has limited value. A review of one body does not prove that every product from a store or manufacturer performs the same way.

Use the [review evidence ladder](/learn/sex-doll-reviews) to rank exact-order evidence above broad praise, seller-controlled testimonials, or context-free ratings.

## Turn Review Concerns Into Questions

Reviews are most useful when they help you ask better questions before ordering.

If a review mentions weight, ask for the current manufacturer value in pounds and kilograms for the exact body and head. If it mentions late delivery, separate production from carrier transit. If it mentions a visual mismatch, ask which photographed options were standard. If it mentions a repair, confirm the material and repair method.

Send any claim that affects your decision to DollWow. The team will check what applies to the exact listing, request supplier clarification where needed, and avoid treating an unrelated experience as product fact.
