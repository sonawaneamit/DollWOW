# FANREAL Buying Guide: Real Skin, Bodies and Included Features

Choose a FANREAL doll by matching the body, head construction and finish you want. A face name alone is not a complete specification, and "Real Skin" does not tell you which mechanical options are included.

The easiest approach is to make three decisions in order: proportions and handling, the head you prefer, then appearance details and extras.

## What Real Skin describes

FANREAL describes Real Skin Texture as surface detail developed through scanning and sculpting, including pores and wrinkles around joints. It is a texture designation, not a promise that every photographed feature or optional mechanism comes with the doll. [FANREAL's official feature overview](https://www.fanreal.com/)

Separate texture from body painting in your comparison. Ask which finish is included in the selected listing, then compare photographs of that finish. A smooth-looking gallery image and a close-up texture photograph are not enough to establish whether two offers use the same build.

If you mainly want a particular face and hairstyle, keep that preference separate from the texture decision. It helps avoid paying for an upgrade merely because it appeared beside the image you liked.

## Choose the body before comparing styling

The factory's body introduction identifies multiple body shapes, including 168 cm F-cup, 170 cm D-cup, 170 cm G-cup and 175 cm D-cup, alongside UniSeries bodies. It directs buyers to product-level measurements. [Official body introduction](https://www.fanreal.com/fanreal-body-intro/)

The practical distinction is not height alone. Compare waist and hip measurements, shoulder width, listed weight and the space available for dressing and storage. Two bodies of similar height may suit very different clothing and handling arrangements.

Start with two body choices, not ten photographs. Write down what attracts you to each and which everyday task could be difficult. The [size and weight guide](/learn/sex-doll-size-weight-guide) can help with that wider decision.

## Recognize separate-head and integrated construction

FANREAL describes UniSeries as an integrated head-and-body design. That is a construction difference, not simply a makeup package. [Factory UniSeries explanation](https://www.fanreal.com/fanreal-uniseries-intro/)

If changing heads later matters to you, establish the construction before ordering. Do not apply a removable-head assumption to an integrated product. Likewise, do not assume a separately sold head fits a body because the face appears elsewhere in the brand's gallery.

Consider the ownership tradeoff: are you choosing one complete appearance, or do you value the ability to change supported components? Ask how the specific design affects storage and aftercare rather than guessing from the neck area in a photograph.

## Read the default specification and option menu separately

FANREAL's official Vanessa 170 cm D-cup page distinguishes a default hard silicone head from a soft silicone movable-jaw option. The same page lists moving eyes in its default configuration and presents other upgrades separately. Those details belong to that factory listing, not every FANREAL doll. [Vanessa factory configuration](https://www.fanreal.com/product/vanessa-ae/)

Use this short comparison when reading a DollWOW product:

| Detail | Record before comparing totals |
| --- | --- |
| Body | Full code, measurements and weight basis |
| Head | Face plus construction or version |
| Finish | Texture, painting and skin selection |
| Hair | Wig or supported implanted choice |
| Mechanical features | Included specification versus selected upgrades |
| Fulfillment | Custom build or exact stocked unit |

A menu item is a choice, not proof that it is already included. Save the selected configuration rather than relying on the starting-price screenshot.

On October 16, DollWOW's FANREAL collection includes Diana 168 cm F-cup and Melissa 175 cm D-cup listings. For a stocked unit, confirm what is already installed. For a custom build, confirm the final combination before production. An optional hand or foot change should remain a separate decision from your preferred face or skin finish.

## A sensible final shortlist

Keep one line for each complete build: body, head version, finish, hair, selected extras and current total. Add the practical reason you prefer it, such as the proportions, handling weight or appearance.

Choose the configuration that answers those priorities clearly. A longer list of feature names is not automatically a better doll for your home or routine.
