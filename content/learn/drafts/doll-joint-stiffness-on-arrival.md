# Stiff Joints on Arrival: When to Stop and Ask for Help

If a new doll's joint will not move as expected, stop before increasing the force. Resistance alone does not tell you whether the joint is behaving normally, being moved in the wrong direction or needs support attention.

Your first task is to keep the doll supported and record the concern. You do not need to diagnose the internal skeleton or prove the problem by repeatedly reproducing it.

## Start with the exact body and skeleton

Find the brand, full body code and selected skeleton in your order. Include any hand, foot or shoulder option relevant to the concern. Similar-looking dolls can have different supported movement.

Irontech provides an English manual through its [official download page](https://www.irontechdoll.com/download/). In the downloaded 18-page edition checked on October 16, 2026, PDF page 5 describes stabilizing the body during joint movement and using soft support. Page 6 makes its shoulder-shrugging example conditional on the doll having that joint. These are reasons to identify the installed mechanism, not a diagnosis of a stiff joint or permission to reproduce every illustrated pose.

Its [factory FAQ](https://www.irontechdoll.com/help-faq/) directs owners to maintain the doll according to its manual and contact the vendor or factory about problems. The general manual does not establish which shoulder, hand or other joint is installed in your body.

Use the instructions supplied for your exact build. A general manual or another model's demonstration is not a model-specific diagnosis of stiffness.

## Make the immediate situation stable

Stop the attempted movement and leave the doll safely supported. Do not lift the whole body through the affected limb, pull from delicate fingers or feet, or use your body weight to push through resistance.

If clothing prevents you from seeing the area, do not force the joint just to remove it. Tell support what is obstructing the view. A clear description of the current position is preferable to causing a second problem while seeking a better photograph.

Do not try to match the most flexible pose in a gallery. The [skeleton options guide](/learn/sex-doll-skeleton-options) explains the buying differences; it is not permission to test an unfamiliar joint's limits.

## Choose the next step without testing harder

| Arrival observation | Immediate next step | Record for support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expected movement meets unfamiliar resistance | Pause; do not add force to discover the range | Joint, attempted direction and present position |
| A manual shows a shoulder movement but the order does not identify that joint | Ask which mechanism is installed before attempting it | Body code, skeleton selection and manual page |
| A sound, visible distortion or sudden looseness has already occurred | Do not repeat the movement for a recording | What happened once, with available still photos |
| Carton or surface damage is also visible | Keep packaging and report the arrival concern promptly | Packaging, affected area, delivery date and order reference |

This is a conservative arrival-triage table, not a factory-approved diagnostic decision tree. It produces a useful support record without deciding whether resistance is normal or authorizing an adjustment.

## Describe the observation, not the presumed fault

Write down which joint is involved, the direction you attempted and what happened. "The left elbow resisted the first attempted bend" is useful. "The skeleton is broken" is a conclusion that may not yet be supported.

Record any sound, visible distortion or unexpected looseness that already occurred, without repeating the movement to capture it. Mention whether the concern was present at unpacking and whether there is visible damage to the carton or surrounding material.

Photograph the overall position and the affected area where possible. Keep the original photographs and packaging. Avoid editing images in a way that hides the context support needs.

## A hypothetical support worksheet

This example is hypothetical, not a customer case or a diagnosis:

| Field | Example of a useful observation |
| --- | --- |
| Build | Exact body code and skeleton copied from the order |
| Area | Left shoulder |
| First observation | Resistance during the first attempted movement |
| Current state | Movement stopped; body remains supported |
| Evidence | Overall position and close photograph available |
| Question | Which instructions apply, and should movement remain paused? |

The worksheet deliberately does not assign a cause or a repair. It gives the manufacturer or support team the information needed to decide the next step for the actual product.

## Avoid improvised fixes

Do not apply oil, solvent or lubricant to the outside of a joint in the hope of reaching the mechanism. Do not heat the area, cut the material, access screws or follow another owner's internal adjustment procedure without applicable authorization and instructions.

There is no universal break-in exercise, safe force figure or joint angle in this guide. Repeating a movement until it becomes easier is not an appropriate test when the cause of resistance is unknown.

If you already tried something, include it in the support request. An accurate record helps more than trying to return the doll to an undocumented starting state.

## Report an arrival concern promptly

DollWOW's [Care for Life page](/care-for-life) separates arrival defects and transit damage from the first-year accidental-damage rescue. It asks for arrival issues to be reported within 24 hours under the relevant returns and shipping-protection process. Do not wait through a self-imposed break-in period before raising an arrival concern.

Send the order reference, build details, observations and photographs. Ask whether the current position should be maintained and request the exact applicable instructions. Coverage and resolution depend on the published terms and review of the issue, not a diagnosis made from a general article.

Keep movement paused until the applicable instructions or support response resolve the concern. This checklist records an observation; it does not establish a fault or authorize a repair.
