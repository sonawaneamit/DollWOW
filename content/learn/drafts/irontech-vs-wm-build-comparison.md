# Irontech vs WM: Head and Material Labels Compared

Irontech or WM? A good place to start is what you want from your doll, then which complete build gives you that at a price you're comfortable with.

Below, we'll compare two listings and work through common head and material labels. The builds aren't identical, and this isn't a hands-on review or a pair of matching quotes. Keep your favorite [Irontech](/brands/irontech-dolls) and [WM](/brands/wm-dolls) options handy as you read.

## Two similar-height listings

Two DollWOW offers checked on 23 October 2026 show why the complete identity matters:

| Listed material | Silicone | Silicone |

Molly's 37.5 kg is body only. The WM listing just says "Weight," without saying what's included. Ask for weights that cover the same parts before comparing how the dolls would handle.

Sources: [Molly's exact offer](/products/irontech-molly-166cm-2-0-s44-ros-max-silicone-doll) and [the WM head 216 offer](/products/wm-head-216-165cm-d-cup-silicone-companion-doll-62ttx). These dated listings aren't new factory measurements or quotes for matching builds. Prices are before selected extras, shipping, tax and checkout discounts; check the current offers when you're ready to order.

The $899 price gap covers different bodies, heads and included features, rather than the ROS MAX head alone. Subtracting 23 from 37.5 won't tell you which finished doll is easier to move, either. Adding Molly's separate head-weight range would still be an estimate, not a confirmed complete weight.

Molly's listed head is ROS MAX; other menu choices aren't automatically interchangeable. For the WM doll, ask which head construction is supplied rather than looking for a generic equivalent to ROS MAX. Neither record establishes that this WM build uses S-TPE.

## Start with your everyday preferences

Imagine a buyer who wants an adult-presenting full-body doll, removable hair for different looks, manageable handling weight and no powered functions. Those are illustrative preferences, not a description of either brand's standard package.

Our example buyer starts with a body material and a weight they're comfortable handling, then picks a suitably proportioned doll from each brand. An exact match might not exist. That's fine, as long as the remaining differences are clear.

The [Irontech buying guide](/learn/irontech-dolls-buying-guide) and [WM buying guide](/learn/wm-dolls-buying-guide) remain the broader brand references. This worksheet helps make the narrower purchase decision between two candidates.

## Get the materials genuinely aligned

A silicone head with a TPE body is different from a full-silicone doll, even if both cards mention "silicone." Give the head and body separate lines on your shortlist.

WM's official store separates TPE, hybrid and silicone product categories. Irontech's official materials likewise describe model-specific choices and compatibility. Those categories are a starting point, not proof that any two shortlisted dolls have equivalent construction. [WM official store](https://wmdollshop.com/) and [Irontech factory FAQ](https://www.irontechdoll.com/help-faq/)

If your preferred bodies require different materials, keep the comparison but name the difference. You are then choosing between two offers with a material tradeoff, not isolating the effect of the brand.

## ROS MAX and S-TPE answer different questions

A named head system and a named body material are not equivalent upgrades. For example, Irontech's ROS MAX identifies a head construction, while WM's S-TPE announcement concerns a material offering. Comparing the two labels as competing versions of one feature would not answer the buyer's question. [Irontech ROS MAX](https://www.irontechdoll.com/product/sex-doll-head-ros-max/) and [WM S-TPE announcement](https://wmdollshop.com/news/new-material-stpe/)

| Factory example | What the maker says | What still needs checking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Irontech ROS MAX silicone head, including selectable S10 ROS MAX | A silicone head version described as upgraded ROS, with specific head references | Body material, complete-doll weight or inclusion in a particular DollWOW price |
| WM New Material S-TPE announcement, dated January 6, 2024 | A named material announcement, separate from WM's head-feature categories | A head function, a matched body weight or a measured advantage over ROS MAX |

One important care note from Irontech's [ROS MAX eye warning](https://www.irontechdoll.com/help-faq/): the head has no dynamic eye movement, and the factory warns owners not to pull the eyelids to imitate blinking. "Upgraded" doesn't mean every facial function is included. WM's short S-TPE announcement isn't a laboratory comparison, either.

For the hypothetical buyer, enter ROS MAX in the **head version** field and S-TPE in the **material** field only if the chosen offer supports it. Those entries cannot fill the same comparison cell. Start with the actual head they prefer and the required removable-hair arrangement; then establish the body material independently.

A lighter-sounding material name is worth checking against the complete doll's weight. The model's figures will help more than a general impression that one brand should be easier to move.

## Compare the total you would actually pay

First list included items. Then add only the extras you want and that the selected model supports. Keep optional accessories separate so you can see whether the apparent price gap comes from the main build or your shopping choices.

Request the same currency and delivery destination for both quotes. Separate the configured product amount from shipping, tax and any current discount. A temporary promotion should not become a permanent claim that one brand always costs less.

If the totals are close, think about what you'll enjoy day to day: proportions, handling, changing hairstyles or a particular construction. When only one doll meets your must-have, you have a good reason to choose it without declaring the whole brand better.

## Keep the final difference visible

Write one sentence explaining why you prefer the chosen build and one sentence naming what you are giving up. That makes the decision easier to revisit without restarting the brand debate.
