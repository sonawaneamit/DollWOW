## Start With the Exact Doll

Retailers often use similar manufacturer photography, shortened model names, or different styling for the same body. Two listings can look alike while offering different heads, materials, skeletons, standing options, accessories, or fulfillment paths.

Before comparing prices, record:

1. manufacturer and product family
2. exact body and head
3. TPE, full silicone, silicone head, or hybrid construction
4. height, body measurements, and listed weight in pounds and kilograms
5. full doll, torso, hips product, head, or another product form
6. included appearance and structural options
7. ready-to-ship, made-to-order, or pre-order status

If the listing does not make those details clear, send the URL or a screenshot through [Compare a listing](/compare). DollWow will identify what can be verified, what still needs supplier confirmation, and whether the same or closest supported product can be offered.

## Decide What Realistic Means to You

Realism is personal, but it can still be evaluated carefully. Separate the features that matter instead of relying on one polished hero image.

### Face and expression

Compare the sculpt from the front, profile, and three-quarter view. Look at eye alignment, mouth shape, makeup transitions, brows, hairline, and whether several photographs show the same head.

### Body proportions

Height alone is not enough. Compare shoulder width, torso length, bust or chest, waist, hips, limb proportions, and the relationship between the selected head and body.

### Surface finish

Material affects the starting point, but finish quality also depends on the exact manufacturer, product family, makeup, pigmentation, seams, and photography. Full silicone is not automatically more realistic for every buyer, and TPE is not automatically less realistic.

### Hands, feet, and joints

Inspect fingers, nails, feet, standing hardware, elbows, knees, and visible joint behavior. These details often reveal more than a distant lifestyle photograph.

Use the [realism guide](/learn/most-realistic-sex-dolls) for a complete inspection method. When one visual detail controls the purchase, ask DollWow to confirm whether it belongs to the standard build or a paid option.

## Choose the Right Material Path

### TPE

TPE is often selected for softness and a lower starting price. Formulation, firmness, weight, care, and stain sensitivity vary by product, so compare the exact body rather than treating all TPE as one material.

### Full silicone

Full-silicone dolls are often considered for fine sculpt detail, controlled surface finish, and simpler surface cleaning. They can carry a higher price and substantial handling weight. Verify construction and listed weight before deciding.

### Silicone head or hybrid build

A silicone head on a TPE body or another hybrid path can combine priorities. Confirm the neck connection, tone match, head-body pairing, care routine, and exact materials used in the listing.

Read [TPE vs silicone sex dolls](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls), then browse current [TPE](/shop/tpe) and [silicone](/shop/silicone) products side by side.

## Ready to Ship or Made to Order?

A ready-to-ship doll is useful when the confirmed existing configuration fits your needs and timing matters. Ask which warehouse holds the unit, whether the photographs show that exact unit or a manufacturer reference, what is included, and what cannot be changed.

A made-to-order doll is useful when supported appearance or structural choices matter more than speed. Confirm the body, head, material, tone, skeleton, standing system, hair, eyes, makeup, functions, conflicts, configured price, production estimate, and factory-media process before the order becomes final.

## Plan for Handling, Delivery, and Storage

Measure the route before ordering. Include the entrance, hallway, stairs, elevator, corners, cleaning space, storage opening, and the position in which the doll will be supported.

Read height and weight together. A flexible doll can be harder to move than a rigid object with the same weight because the limbs and center of mass shift. If you cannot comfortably lift the listed weight, use the [lighter dolls collection](/shop/lighter), consider a more compact body, or ask the team to narrow the catalog around a firm handling limit.

## Common Mistakes

### Comparing photographs instead of products

Shared imagery does not prove that the body, head, options, stock path, or included configuration is identical.

### Choosing by height alone

Height does not reveal handling weight, shoulder and hip width, clothing fit, or the space needed for cleaning and storage.

### Treating every silicone or TPE doll as equivalent

Material is a family. Formulation, construction, finish, firmness, weight, and care can vary by brand and body.

### Trusting the largest discount

Compare the configured and delivered order. A percentage is not useful when the reference price, included options, or shipping differs.

### Waiting until after payment to ask a critical question

Use live chat or email before checkout. DollWow can confirm the detail, request supplier information, or add an approved missing product while the decision is still flexible.
