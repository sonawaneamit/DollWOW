# Dolls Castle Buying Guide: Bodies, Materials and Included Features

The most useful way to compare Dolls Castle dolls is to identify the body first, then confirm the head and included features. A familiar face or similar height does not make two listings the same build. Material, proportions, skeleton and the order configuration all deserve their own line on your shortlist.

Start with the [Dolls Castle collection](/brands/dolls-castle), using the checks below to turn the photographs into a practical buying decision.

## Separate the material families from the styling

The [official Dolls Castle site](https://dolls-castle.com/) has separate TPE and silicone collections. Its catalog also includes listings described as hybrid. Those terms concern construction; a realistic or stylized appearance is a separate choice.

For each candidate, write down the head material and body material independently. A silicone head on a TPE body is not a full-silicone doll. Similarly, an upgraded TPE option should not be interpreted as silicone just because the product has a premium finish.

If the description and specification table use different wording, resolve that before comparing prices. The broader [TPE versus silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) can help with material preferences, but the exact listing must identify what you are buying.

## A factory example: the 183 cm Nyla body

The manufacturer's [183 cm Nyla page](https://dolls-castle.com/product/curvy-slim-tpe-sex-doll-183cm-nyla/) identifies body code 183CM E with head JS061. Its specification table lists a TPE body, 83 cm bust, 57 cm waist, 97 cm hips and 42.5 kg net weight. The same table lists 56 kg package weight. These are factory figures, not DollWOW measurements.

The page's included-feature list names an EVO skeleton, shrugging shoulders, articulated fingers, standing feet and gel breasts. Its option menu separately offers Normal TPE and S-TPE. That is a useful example of why you should distinguish an included feature from an upgrade rather than treating every selectable item as an extra charge.

These details describe that factory listing, not a universal Dolls Castle specification. The manufacturer's prose also rounds the weight differently from its table; use the written order confirmation to settle the weight applicable to your selected build.

## Choose proportions that work at home

Height gives you a starting point, but shoulders, hips and weight decide much of the everyday experience. A tall, slim body still needs somewhere long enough to rest, and a shorter, fuller body may need more width than expected.

Measure the intended storage area and the route to your cleaning space. Include door turns, stairs and the room needed to move around the supported doll. Avoid choosing a body on the assumption that you can simply fold it into a smaller space.

For clothing, compare actual circumferences with the garment's size chart. A lettered cup label is not a complete clothing specification. Loose openings and accessible fastenings can make dressing more comfortable than tight garments that need force to pull into place.

## Compare what is included before adding options

Use a short record for each candidate:

| Decision | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Complete body code, head code and both materials |
| Standard build | Features explicitly included in the selected listing |
| Changes | Each supported upgrade and its additional charge |
| Appearance | Skin choice, makeup, hair and reference photograph |
| Ownership | Confirmed weight, care instructions and support method |

Do not assume a three-tier package exists for every body. If the product uses individual manual selections, compare those selections directly. A package name is useful only when its contents are stated for that product.

Ask about compatibility before prioritizing powered features or special construction. An option shown elsewhere in the brand's range is not evidence that it belongs on your body and head combination.

## Keep stock and custom builds separate

A warehouse listing represents an existing configuration. A custom listing concerns a build that still needs production. A factory warehouse label does not by itself establish that the same unit is available through DollWOW.

If timing matters, ask for the exact available configuration and dispatch location. If appearance matters more, establish which changes are supported before production. Use [how ordering works](/how-ordering-works) to understand the order path rather than borrowing a delivery promise from the factory's direct-sale page.

## Make the final choice on the complete build

Choose the candidate that fits your space and handling arrangements, then confirm its appearance and options together. Save the final configuration, relevant specification table and any clarification of conflicting figures.

A clear body-and-head record will remain useful long after the purchase, especially for care questions or a later replacement part. Bring that record to [DollWOW support](/support) when you need a decision-critical detail confirmed.
