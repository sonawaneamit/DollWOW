# Sex Doll Size and Weight Guide: What 2,615 Listings Show

By Alex, Doll Collector and Educator with 20+ Years of Experience

## Quick Answer

In a dated review of `2,615` current full-size DollWow listings with usable height, weight, and price data, the median listed doll was `5 ft 4 in / 163 cm` tall and weighed `81.5 lb / 37 kg`. The middle half of listings weighed between `73.9 and 90.4 lb / 33.5 and 41 kg`.

Those figures describe the DollWow catalog on August 12, 2026. They are not a universal industry average, and they do not mean a doll near the median will be easy for you to move. Shape, rigidity, grip, stairs, doorways, storage position, and your own lifting ability matter alongside the number on the product page.

## How To Read Sex Doll Height

Height helps you estimate visual scale and required length. It does not tell you the doll's width, shape, clothing fit, or handling difficulty by itself.

A doll listed at `5 ft 3 in / 160 cm` can have a slim build, a fuller build, broad shoulders, a large bust, wide hips, or a deliberately stylized shape. Two dolls with the same height can also use different internal structures and have very different listed weights.

Before choosing by height, check:

- Shoulder width and torso depth
- Bust or chest, waist, and hip measurements
- Arm and leg length where supplied
- Foot size if clothing or shoes matter
- Listed weight in pounds and kilograms
- Box dimensions and packaged weight when available
- The narrowest doorway, turn, staircase, or elevator on the delivery route

For compact full-body formats, see the [mini sex doll guide](/learn/mini-sex-dolls). Torsos and hips are separate product forms and should not be compared with full-size dolls using height alone.

## How Much Do Sex Dolls Weigh?

Current full-size DollWow listings cover a broad range. In this snapshot, one quarter of analyzed listings weighed under `75 lb / 34 kg`, almost half fell between `75 and 89 lb / 34 and 40.4 kg`, and about one quarter weighed `90 lb / 40.8 kg` or more.

The listed weight is only the first handling test. A rigid object without convenient handles can feel harder to move than a familiar object of the same weight. A doll can also shift at the hips, knees, shoulders, or neck while being repositioned.

Do not test your limit for the first time after delivery. Use a safely contained object near the listed weight, keep it close to your body, and rehearse only movements you can perform without strain. If stairs, tight turns, overhead lifting, or storage access are involved, choose a lower weight or plan for two-person assistance.

## Height Does Not Predict Weight Reliably

The median listed weight rises across some height bands, but the relationship is not strong enough to use height as a substitute for weight. In the catalog snapshot, the `165-169 cm / 5 ft 5 in-5 ft 7 in` band had nearly the same median weight as the shorter `160-164 cm / 5 ft 3 in-5 ft 5 in` band.

Body volume, material, internal foam, skeleton design, wall thickness, and manufacturer construction all affect the result. A tall slim body can weigh less than a shorter fuller body. A silicone torso can also weigh more than its compact dimensions suggest.

The practical rule is simple: filter by a weight you can manage, then compare height and proportions within that range.

## Does Material Decide The Weight?

No. Material alone does not reliably tell you how heavy the finished doll will be.

In this dated DollWow snapshot, full-silicone listings had a lower median listed weight than TPE listings. That does not prove silicone is generally lighter. Catalog mix, body geometry, weight-reduction construction, head pairings, and the number of listings per body all affect the medians.

## Methodology And Limitations

This analysis used the public DollWow product feed reviewed on August 12, 2026. It included listings at least `121 cm / 4 ft` tall with a positive listed weight and price. The height boundary removed compact partial-body products from the full-size distribution.

The unit of analysis is a catalog listing, not a unique body mold or the entire sex doll market. One body can appear with several heads or configurations. Supplier measurements may be rounded. Product data, prices, materials, and availability can change after the review date.

The index reports medians and distribution bands because a single average can be distorted by unusually light or heavy products. It does not measure quality, popularity, sales, realism, durability, or independent hands-on testing.

## FAQs

### What is the average size of a sex doll?

There is no single industry-standard size. In DollWow's August 12, 2026 snapshot of 2,615 current full-size listings, the median height was 5 ft 4 in / 163 cm. The middle half of listings were 5 ft 2 in-5 ft 6 in / 158-167 cm tall.

### How much does a full-size sex doll weigh?

Weight varies widely. The median listed weight in the dated DollWow snapshot was 81.5 lb / 37 kg, and the middle half ranged from 73.9 to 90.4 lb / 33.5 to 41 kg. Always check the exact listing.

### Is a taller sex doll always heavier?

No. Body volume, material, internal structure, skeleton, and weight-reduction construction all affect weight. Compare the listed height and weight together.

### Is TPE lighter than silicone?

Not reliably. The finished weight depends on the complete build. In this DollWow snapshot, the median full-silicone listing was lighter than the median TPE listing, but catalog mix prevents that from becoming a universal material rule.

### What weight is easiest to handle?

That depends on the buyer, route, grip, storage position, and required movements. Choose a listed weight below your safe lifting limit and rehearse the relevant movement with a safely contained object before ordering.

### Are mini dolls always lightweight?

No. A compact doll can still be dense, and some listings described as mini may be torsos or hips rather than full dolls. Confirm product form, complete dimensions, and listed weight.

### Which measurements matter for clothing?

Bust or chest, waist, hips, shoulders, inseam, arm length, and foot size are more useful than height alone. Supplier measurements and clothing labels can vary, so allow adjustment room.

### How should I measure my delivery route?

Check the narrowest door, hallway turn, stair landing, elevator opening, and storage entrance. Include clearance for packaging and the person moving it.

### Does a listed weight include packaging?

Not necessarily. Product weight and packaged shipping weight are different figures. Ask DollWow to confirm the relevant number when it affects delivery or handling.
