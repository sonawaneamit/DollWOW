# Matching a Replacement Head to the Body Skin Tone

The best starting point for a replacement head is the body's original material and factory skin-tone identifier. A colour name such as "tan" is not enough to establish a match, especially across brands or between silicone and TPE. Confirm the mechanical connection separately, then compare the actual finish under useful lighting.

You are looking for a coherent pairing, not trying to make two unrelated screen photographs identical.

## Recover the body's original identity

Find the original order confirmation, body code, material, skin-tone selection and approximate production date. Keep the brand's spelling or number exactly as recorded. Do not replace a factory identifier with a colour you have inferred from the current appearance.

Add any body painting or finish selection. The underlying material colour and painted appearance answer different questions, and a head chosen only against a shaded product photograph may disappoint when placed on the actual body.

If the original record is incomplete, send the available documents and neutral photographs to support. Treat the colour as unidentified until it can be connected to a supplier record; a confident guess is still a guess.

## Why the same colour name may look different

SEDOLL provides a useful specific example in its [LSTPE upgrade explanation](https://www.sedoll.com/stpe-series-introduces-a-new-custom-option-lstpe-lightweight-upgrade/). The manufacturer documents a Light Tan LSTPE body paired with selected Light Tan silicone heads, while explaining that the same named tone may look different across those materials. It describes additional adjustments for the cross-material combination and says other tones remain under matching tests.

That is evidence for the documented SE pairing, not a universal colour chart. It illustrates why "both are Light Tan" is a starting point rather than a guarantee.

Likewise, a colour code from one manufacturer has no automatic equivalent at another. Ask whether the factory supports the exact head/body/material combination, and whether its reference concerns a current production colour rather than an older sample.

## Check the connector before judging colour

A visually promising head still needs the correct neck connection. Establish the connector type, dimensions where specified, fitting direction and any approved adapter. Do not force a fitting because the thread looks close.

Also ask about the head's weight and its compatibility with the body's neck assembly. A successful colour comparison does not establish structural suitability, and a supported mechanical fit does not establish a colour match.

For a custom pairing, record these as two separate confirmations. It makes later questions far easier to resolve than a single note saying "compatible."

## Ask for photographs that answer the right question

When a comparison is available, ask for the proposed head and body in the same frame under the same neutral light. A wider view should show the relationship; a closer view should show the neck transition without deep shadow.

Avoid using a warmly lit room photograph on one side and a cool studio image on the other. Ask that decorative colour lighting and image filters be left out. A neutral reference in the frame can help discuss lighting, but it does not turn an ordinary photograph into a calibrated colour measurement.

Our [factory-photo archive](/factory-photos) explains the limits of visual review. Photographs can reveal a visible contrast; they cannot promise exactly how every screen, room light or viewing angle will render the finished pairing.

## Use a short matching record

| Item | Record before ordering |
| --- | --- |
| Existing body | Brand, body code, material and production period |
| Original tone | Exact factory name or number from the order |
| Surface finish | Painting or finish selection, if documented |
| Proposed head | Head code, material and tone identifier |
| Mechanical fit | Confirmed connector and any approved adapter |
| Visual check | Same-frame comparison and any visible difference |

This is a blank purchasing record, not a tested cross-brand compatibility table. Keep unanswered fields open until someone can provide the relevant information.

## Decide what level of difference you can accept

If you want an unobtrusive neck transition, say so before ordering. If a deliberate contrast is acceptable, record that preference just as clearly. Appearance is personal, and "close enough" means different things to different owners.

Do not use household paint, solvent or an unapproved coating to correct a mismatch. Ask for the supported solution rather than risking the existing finish. A material-compatible replacement is a better starting point than planning a repair to a new purchase.

Send the completed record through [DollWOW support](/support), or use it when considering a [custom build](/shop/custom). Keep the resulting confirmation with the body documents so the next replacement begins with a reliable record.
