# One Doll, Two Looks: Dark's Original Photo and DollVue Preview

A different hair and eye combination can change the overall impression of a doll without requiring a different body. The useful question is whether you prefer that appearance on the model you already like.

Here is a historical, illustrative DollWOW demonstration using [Irontech Dark, 164 cm F-cup silicone](/products/irontech-dark-164cm-f-cup-silicone-companion-doll-1k1t7), documented on 16 October 2026. It pairs the original product photograph with one generated DollVue alternative. It is not a customer case study, a new generation, a photograph of a second completed doll or a record of currently orderable selections.

## Start with the original photograph

![Original product photograph of adult-presenting Irontech Dark with dark hair, wearing a green outfit and holding a football.](/images/dollvue/landing/02-irontech-dark-164cm-f-cup-silicone-companion-doll-1k1t7-original.jpg)

The original product photograph is the starting reference, not a generated preview. It shows Dark with dark hair in a specific outfit, pose and lighting setup. Use it to identify the doll and the photographed look before considering changes.

A styled photograph is useful for seeing the sculpt and overall presentation. It does not establish that the outfit and props are supplied, or identify every installed construction feature. Keep those questions with the written product specification.

## What the pair helps you decide

Look first at whether the lighter hair direction suits your preference for this face. Then consider the selected eye colour. Keeping the product identity fixed makes that decision more useful than comparing two unrelated dolls with different proportions and photography.

If one change interests you and the other does not, separate those preferences in your notes. This combined preview cannot show exactly how a hair-only or eyes-only change would look. Nor does its existence establish that either change is currently available to purchase for this head.

The preview also changes some fine rendering details. Do not treat every visible facial difference, highlight or strand as an orderable feature. The selected labels describe the intended changes; generated detail is not a new factory specification.

For example, the arm and the ball's placement differ between the images. Neither is a selected appearance option. This is why the pair should guide a colour preference rather than serve as a controlled before-and-after photograph of an otherwise unchanged product.

## What the preview cannot establish

DollVue is an approximate visual aid, not a finished-doll guarantee. Lighting, colour rendering and generated details limit a side-by-side comparison. Your screen is also not a physical colour sample.

Neither image measures softness, handling weight, joint movement or durability. A preserved pose does not test the skeleton, and a convincing hairstyle does not establish its attachment method. Confirm whether the actual order uses a wig or supported implanted hair separately; the [hair-options guide](/learn/implanted-hair-vs-wig) explains that distinction.

This historical example records demonstration labels only. It has not been reconciled here with the current product's option codes or supported combinations. The current product menu and a confirmed configuration govern what is available to order now; do not infer availability for Dark or another Irontech doll from this illustration.

## Keep visual preferences separate from an order

If the lighter-hair direction interests you, keep that as a preference rather than copying the old labels into an order. Ask which current hair and eye choices the exact head supports, how they are identified, and whether a relevant factory reference is available. If the answer is unknown, the preference remains unconfirmed; the preview does not fill the gap.

The [published ordering process](/how-ordering-works) explains the next steps. For eligible custom builds, available factory media provides a separate opportunity to compare the completed configuration with the agreed order before shipment. A DollVue preview is not that production-stage check.

Use this pair to describe a visual direction. Any purchase must use a separately confirmed current configuration, which may differ from the demonstration. Neither the historical labels nor incidental generated details are a promise of what can be supplied.
