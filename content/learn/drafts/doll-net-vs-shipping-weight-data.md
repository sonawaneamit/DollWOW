# Doll Weight vs Boxed Shipping Weight: A Comparison Worksheet

Use the finished doll's weight to plan everyday handling, and the complete packed weight to plan delivery. They answer different questions. A figure labelled simply "weight" needs one more detail: what does it include?

This worksheet uses one published factory net/packed example and a separate hypothetical packing ledger. Neither is a DollWOW-measured shipment or a customer case study.

## Start with the exact configuration

Record the brand, body code, head code, material and weight-reduction selection before copying any number. A different head or internal option can make an otherwise similar listing a poor comparison.

SE's LSTPE announcement, for example, names particular supported bodies and gives body-specific weight comparisons. Those are manufacturer-listed body weights, not evidence of the weight of a delivered box. [SE's official LSTPE explanation](https://www.sedoll.com/stpe-series-introduces-a-new-custom-option-lstpe-lightweight-upgrade/)

Do not subtract two numbers from different configurations and call the difference "packaging." First establish that they describe the same build.

## Keep five weight fields separate

| Field | What to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Body weight | Is the head excluded, and which body options apply? |
| Complete doll weight | Does this include the selected head? |
| Accessories | Are extra heads, clothing and care items counted? |
| Gross packed weight | Does this include every shipped carton and its packaging? |
| Carrier billing weight | Is the charge based on physical weight or a size-based calculation? |

Treat a missing field as unknown, not zero. "Net" is useful only when the seller explains the contents of that net figure. An extra head in another carton should not quietly disappear from the delivery plan.

Keep the original units alongside any conversion. Use kilograms or pounds consistently when doing arithmetic; a number without its unit is not ready to compare.

## One published net-and-packed comparison

Dolls Castle's [183 cm Nyla specification](https://dolls-castle.com/product/curvy-slim-tpe-sex-doll-183cm-nyla/) lists 42.5 kg net weight and 56 kg package weight, with a 165 x 41 x 31 cm box. Those are factory-listed figures checked on 16 October 2026, not a DollWOW-weighed shipment. The prose rounds the net weight to 42 kg, so retain the table's label and ask which figure applies to the selected order.

The table's net-to-package difference is 13.5 kg. It does not break that difference into accessories and packaging, or separately identify head weight. Do not relabel the whole difference as cardboard weight. One published example does not establish a typical packaging allowance.

## A hypothetical worked example

Imagine a written packing specification lists a 30 kg body, a 4 kg head, 2 kg of accessories and 7 kg of packaging. These invented figures illustrate the method only; they do not describe a DollWOW product.

| Hypothetical ledger entry | Mass | Counted in complete doll? | Counted in packed shipment? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Body | 30 kg | Yes | Yes |
| Selected head | 4 kg | Yes | Yes |
| Accessories | 2 kg | No | Yes |
| Packaging | 7 kg | No | Yes |
| Total | 43 kg packed | 34 kg | 43 kg |

The complete doll would be 34 kg, about 75.0 lb. The packed shipment would total 43 kg, about 94.8 lb. The difference is 9 kg, about 19.8 lb, because the accessories and packaging travel too. Conversions are rounded. These ledger entries are invented teaching values, not a breakdown of Nyla's 13.5 kg difference.

That distinction changes the practical question. Moving the doll between a dressing area and storage is a 34 kg task in this example. Receiving the unopened parcel involves a larger, heavier object. Neither figure establishes whether one person can safely manage it.

Now imagine that the label says only "net weight: 30 kg." You cannot know whether to add the head. The correct next step is a clarification, not a more elaborate calculation.

## Ask for the box dimensions as well

A delivery route needs length, width and height, not just weight. Measure the narrowest doorway, turning space and intended unpacking area against the actual packed dimensions.

Ask whether the consignment uses one carton or several, and whether the supplied dimensions include protective packing. Do not infer carton size from standing height or a photograph of another delivery.

For a building with stairs, confirm the delivery service's handoff point before arranging help. A shipping estimate alone does not establish upstairs placement, unpacking or removal of packaging. Keep the [current shipping guide](/shipping) beside the product specification.

## Save a comparison you can revisit

For each shortlisted build, keep the product link, configuration, date and five weight fields together. Add the source of each number: product specification, written packing confirmation or carrier quote. This prevents a later option change from being compared against an older weight.

If a lighter version is available, compare complete-doll figures on the same basis. A body-only saving is still useful information, but it should not be presented as the percentage reduction of the entire shipment.

## Use the result to choose, not just calculate

An attractive build that cannot be moved comfortably is not a good fit for your routine. Consider the route to cleaning, dressing and storage as carefully as the route from the front door.
