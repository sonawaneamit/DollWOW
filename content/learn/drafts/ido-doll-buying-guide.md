# IDO Doll Buying Guide: Body Options and Included Construction

An IDO selection starts with a fixed factory construction package, then the body and head identity. The adult silicone manufacturer IDODOLL lists substantial construction features as standard, not as a menu of extras you must add individually. Choosing a model is different from designing a new body specification.

The [IDO collection](/brands/ido-dolls) holds the current shopping choices. This guide explains how to interpret the factory's body designations and compare the complete offer rather than a long menu of feature names.

## Identify the body, not just the face

The official [IDO Advantage page](https://www.idodoll.com/ido-advantage/), checked on 16 October 2026, lists five body designations: 165 cm D, 167 cm F, 169 cm C, 170 cm F and 175 cm D. It describes platinum silicone as standard. These are the bodies covered by that page, not an exhaustive inventory of every DollWOW listing or a promise that every combination is available.

Keep the height and body designation together. Then add the full head code. A display name can appear with more than one body, so the name alone is not a complete configuration.

Ask for the actual bust, waist, hips and shoulder dimensions when narrowing the choice. Body designations help identify a model; they do not replace a measurement sheet or determine whether clothing will fit.

## Understand the standard package

The factory's current page lists EVO skeleton, articulated fingers, hard hands, toe bones, bolt-free standing feet, body makeup, skin texture, gel-filled breasts and buttocks, weight reduction, implanted eyebrows and lashes, and a wig among its standard features. These are manufacturer statements about its current package, not DollWOW test results. [Factory inclusions](https://www.idodoll.com/ido-advantage/)

Use the package as a baseline, then keep the choices and identification fields separate:

| Factory package or selection | How to record it |
| --- | --- |
| Platinum silicone; texture and multi-layer makeup | Standard construction/finish, not three assumed paid extras |
| EVO skeleton; articulated fingers; hard hands; toe bones; bolt-free standing feet | Included structural package; still confirm the supplied generation |
| Weight Reduction 4.0; gel-filled breasts and buttocks | Included construction, not a second weight saving to subtract |
| Implanted eyebrows/lashes and wig | Included head styling items; record the actual supplied wig |
| Body designation and head code | Identity of the chosen combination, not permission to redesign its mechanics |
| Skin and available wig reference | Appearance selection only where the actual offer supports it |

This table interprets the [factory's standard configuration](https://www.idodoll.com/ido-advantage/). For a particular warehouse unit, confirm its generation and installed specification rather than assuming a current range page updates an already completed doll.

## Compare weight on the same basis

Do not subtract a weight-reduction claim from a listing's weight unless the listing explicitly gives a before-reduction figure. Otherwise you may count the reduction twice.

Ask whether each figure represents the body, complete doll or packed shipment. Use the same basis for your shortlist, and request clarification when a label simply says "Weight." A precise-looking number is not useful when its scope is unknown.

Plan storage and the route through your home around the chosen build. Reduced weight does not mean the doll is effortless to move, and standing-foot construction is not permission for unsupported long-term storage.

## Check the head and appearance separately

Record the head code, supported functions, skin tone, wig and makeup direction. A hairstyle can change the overall impression enough that two photographs of the same sculpt look like different faces.

Ask whether a detail is part of the supplied head, an appearance selection or a photographed accessory. Do not transfer a function from a similarly named head or infer moving parts from an expressive photograph.

The [customization guide](/learn/custom-sex-dolls) helps separate permanent decisions from replaceable styling. A well-defined standard build can still leave meaningful appearance choices without making every construction feature a paid extra.

## Keep stock and factory offers distinct

The factory describes its construction as a unified standard package rather than a custom-build menu. DollWOW's ship-from-warehouse and custom-made labels describe the order route; neither label alone establishes which IDO appearance selections can be changed. Use the exact offer to confirm the body, head and permitted appearance choices. Do not treat factory fulfillment as an open-ended internal-upgrade service.

For a made-to-order offer, save the selected body and head, appearance choices, included items and current total. For stock, save the existing configuration and warehouse location. Read [ready-to-ship versus custom dolls](/learn/ready-to-ship-vs-custom-sex-dolls) before comparing delivery expectations.

Finish with a build you can describe clearly, not simply the longest feature list. [DollWOW support](/support) can help confirm the exact version and resolve any inclusion or handling question before you choose.
