# Top Fire Buying Guide: Body Codes and Factory Feature Checks

With Top Fire, start by separating the body code from the head code. Then compare the exact measurements, listed weight and included configuration. A feature on a factory menu is a useful question to investigate, not a promise that every Top Fire listing includes it.

Use the [Top Fire collection](/brands/top-fire-dolls) as your shopping starting point, and keep the factory information below separate from the current DollWOW offer.

## Read the code before comparing the photographs

The factory's [158D-T06-Bid page](https://www.topfiredoll.com/product/silicone-doll-158cm-bid/) gives a concrete example. The listing identifies a 158 cm, D-cup silicone build with T06 in the product code. This is more useful for identifying the configuration than the name Bid on its own.

Write down the complete identifier alongside your preferred photograph. When comparing another face on the same apparent body, confirm that the body code really is unchanged. When comparing another body with the same face, do not carry its measurements over.

Height and cup labels are a convenient index, but neither describes the whole shape. Shoulder dimensions, waist, hips and actual body weight deserve separate checks before deciding which body family suits your home.

## A factory weight example: net is not packed

For that 158D-T06-Bid listing, the factory table gives 28 kg net weight and 41.5 kg shipping weight. It lists an 87 cm bust, 58 cm waist and 92 cm hips, with package dimensions of 146 x 42 x 31 cm. These are manufacturer figures, not DollWOW measurements or a catalog-wide weight range.

Use net weight to begin the handling discussion, then confirm whether it includes the selected head and options. Use the packed figures for delivery planning. The box dimensions do not define the recommended storage space once the doll is assembled.

The manufacturer's table includes a manual-measurement variation note. Avoid treating a rounded size conversion as a precision guarantee for a tight case or fitted garment.

## Distinguish selectable features from included features

The same factory page displays choices for hard standing feet or feet with bolts, and hardened or normal hands. These are examples of configuration categories to clarify, not a statement that both constructions are supplied together.

Ask which selection is standard in the DollWOW product you are considering and what changes the total. Keep these groups separate:

| Group | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Body and skeleton | Exact construction and supported movement |
| Hands and feet | Selected type and its care or support requirements |
| Head and appearance | Head code, material, skin, eyes, hair and finish |
| Powered functions | Exact system, compatibility and supplied manual |
| Accessories | What is included versus separately purchased |

A product photograph may show styling or accessories outside the base offer. Match the written order to what you actually want, not to everything visible in a gallery.

## Treat head technology as a specific selection

Top Fire discusses ROS head technology in its [official brand introduction](https://www.topfiredoll.com/about-us/). That establishes the manufacturer's terminology, not the detailed specification of every head or a universal set of powered functions.

Ask for the capabilities of the selected head code and whether they are mechanical or powered. A head feature name does not establish body heating, simulated breathing, electronic movement or compatibility with implanted hair.

If an electrical feature matters to you, ask for the matching instructions and component list before ordering. Do not apply settings or installation advice from a different manufacturer.

## Choose the body around ordinary handling

Plan where the doll will rest, be dressed and be cleaned. Include the support arrangement and space needed to move around it. Standing-related terminology needs a documented support method; it should not be interpreted as permission to leave a heavy doll unattended upright.

For clothing, compare the body measurements with the garment chart and fastening design. Avoid relying on a cup letter or height alone. For storage, use the [storage guide](/learn/sex-doll-storage) together with the selected build's instructions.

## Compare the order path and the complete offer

Ship from warehouse means an existing ready-to-ship configuration in the warehouse region shown. Custom-made means production for your confirmed build, followed by shipment from the factory. A factory's warehouse or direct-sale delivery claim is not automatically a DollWOW commitment.

Compare the configured total in the same currency and destination, with delivery and any applicable taxes or duties clarified. Keep any uncertain cost or feature open until it is answered.

Bring your body code, head code and selected options to [DollWOW support](/support). The strongest shortlist is the one you can explain clearly: a body you can accommodate, a head you like, and features documented for that exact combination.
