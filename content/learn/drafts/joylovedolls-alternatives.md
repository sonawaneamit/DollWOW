## Male-Doll Comparisons Need Adult-Specific Detail

General searches for male dolls can include unrelated toys, mannequins, collectibles, and non-adult products. Use adult-specific pages and check details that matter to ownership.

### Product form

Separate full-body male dolls, compact bodies, torsos, and other formats. A low price or weight may reflect a different product form rather than a better deal on an equivalent doll.

### Height and weight

Compare feet and inches with centimeters, and pounds with kilograms. Height does not predict weight. Material, body volume, internal foam, and skeleton design can create major differences between models of similar height.

### Material and construction

Confirm whether the body and head are TPE, silicone, or a hybrid. A silicone head with a TPE body is not the same as full silicone.

### Anatomy and options

Check the exact configuration, fixed or removable components, body proportions, skeleton, standing support, hair, facial hair, and any supported functions. Do not assume one male model’s options apply to every body.

## Compare TPE, Silicone, and Hybrid Correctly

### Full TPE

TPE is often chosen for softness and value. Formulations and finished bodies vary in firmness, weight, surface behavior, and care. Compare the exact product rather than applying a general material reputation to every model.

### Full silicone

Full silicone can support detailed sculpting and a controlled finish. Silicone formulas and body designs vary in firmness, stretch, weight, and maintenance. A higher price does not automatically mean better fit or easier ownership.

### Hybrid

A hybrid may combine a silicone head with a TPE body or use another mixed construction. It can balance appearance, feel, and price priorities, but it should not be priced or described as though it were full silicone.

Use the [TPE vs silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) and compare current [TPE](/shop/tpe), [silicone](/shop/silicone), and [hybrid](/shop/hybrid) products. DollWow can confirm unclear construction through live chat or email before checkout.

## Ready-to-Ship Versus Custom

Ready-to-ship is usually the better route when a confirmed existing unit fits and timing matters. Custom is usually the better route when a supported head, body, material, tone, skeleton, or function matters more than speed.

For a ready unit, confirm the exact configuration, warehouse, current stock, images, allowed changes, and expected dispatch. For a custom build, confirm option compatibility, price changes, production timing, approval steps, and when selections become final.

## Common Comparison Mistakes

### Comparing two homepages

A homepage cannot show whether the exact male, silicone, TPE, ready, or custom product you want is a better fit. Compare matching categories and products.

### Mixing adult and unrelated male dolls

Use adult-specific pages and verify anatomy, proportions, material, measurements, and ownership requirements.

### Comparing different product forms

A torso or compact product is not a lower-priced equivalent of a full-body doll. Check what is actually included.

### Letting a coupon determine fit

Confirm the product and complete total before applying a promotion.

### Assuming shared photos mean identical offers

Manufacturer images can appear on several stores. Verify body, head, material, options, and stock.

Availability varies by product and brand. Resolve conflicts before production.
