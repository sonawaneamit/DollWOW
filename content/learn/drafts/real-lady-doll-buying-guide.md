# Real Lady Buying Guide: Body and Head Families Explained

A Real Lady build is easier to compare when you keep its body code, head code and installed options separate. Choose the proportions first, identify the exact head version next, then review appearance and mechanical choices together.

A face name is a useful way to remember a look. It is not enough to identify everything being supplied.

## Read the complete body code

Real Lady's official ULW white paper identifies RL150, RL159, RL168 and RL168-3 among its documented body examples. The suffix matters: RL168 and RL168-3 are presented as separate entries, so they should not be collapsed into a single specification because their codes share a number. [Real Lady's official 2026 ULW white paper](https://www.real-lady.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Real_Lady_ULW_White_Paper_2026_Branded.pdf)

For a buying comparison, record the code exactly as listed, alongside the material, dimensions and selected weight configuration. A photograph may show the proportions you like, but measurements answer whether the body will suit your clothing, display space and storage arrangement.

Do not choose by the height number alone. Compare the shoulder, waist and hip measurements that matter to your preferred clothing and supported storage position. Keep the [size and weight guide](/learn/sex-doll-size-weight-guide) open for the broader handling questions.

## Identify the head version, not just the face

The factory's ROS MAX head listing includes named head references such as R1 Adela and R2 Ruby. ROS MAX identifies a particular head version; the face code identifies the selected sculpt within that listing. [Real Lady's factory ROS MAX listing](https://www.real-lady.com/product/ros-max-silicone-oral-sex-doll-head/)

Write both down. If a listing says only "Adela," ask which construction is included rather than importing the specification from a separate ROS MAX page. A head sold on its own is also a different offer from a complete doll supplied with that head.

When comparing photographs, check the selected makeup, hair and skin reference. Styling can make a familiar face look different without establishing a different underlying head. Conversely, a familiar face name does not prove the construction has stayed the same.

## Treat lightweight construction as its own choice

ULW is the manufacturer's weight-reduction designation. It should be tied to the supported body and configuration, not treated as a universal property of Real Lady dolls. The white paper distinguishes its "up to 35%" marketing claim from the approximately 20.7% highest reduction in the Real Lady-only production records it summarizes. Those records are manufacturer-reported, not independent DollWOW testing. Actual results vary with body size, internal structure and options.

Ask for the finished weight of the exact build you are considering and what that figure includes. If you are comparing standard and lightweight versions, use figures with the same head-inclusion basis. Do not apply a headline percentage to every body.

The practical decision is whether the confirmed difference helps with your routine. Consider moving between dressing, cleaning and storage areas, not just the initial delivery. Lighter does not automatically mean manageable without suitable support or help.

## Build a short, useful comparison

| Choice | What belongs in your notes |
| --- | --- |
| Body | Exact code and suffix, material and measurements |
| Weight | Selected construction and what the figure includes |
| Head | Face code plus construction version |
| Appearance | Supported skin, makeup and hair selections |
| Other features | Included equipment versus separately chosen options |
| Fulfillment | Actual stocked configuration or custom production |

Start with two builds and fill in the differences. A blank field is a question to resolve, not permission to assume the better specification.

Keep head-to-body fit and colour matching separate. A head can have an appealing appearance without being a confirmed pairing for your selected body. Ask for the supported combination in writing when the product information leaves it unclear.

## Compare the total after selecting options

Use the [Real Lady collection](/brands/real-lady-dolls) to find current DollWOW listings, then work from the actual product's choices. Factory-direct menus and offers are not proof of what a DollWOW starting price includes.

For a custom order, save the configuration with the full codes. For a warehouse doll, confirm the installed head, finish and internal options; do not assume they can be changed before dispatch.

An extra feature is worthwhile when it serves a preference you actually have. If the face, proportions and handling are already right, a longer upgrade list is not a requirement.

## Finish with the ownership questions

Before deciding, establish the supported storage method, care guidance for the finish and the instructions applicable to the head. Keep those references with the build record so aftercare starts from the doll you own.

For support terms, see [Care for Life](/care-for-life). For the purchase itself, choose the complete Real Lady configuration that makes sense in your home, not simply the newest label in a gallery.
