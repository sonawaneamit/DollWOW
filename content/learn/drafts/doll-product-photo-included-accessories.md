# Styled Product Photos: Which Hair, Clothes and Accessories Are Included?

A beautiful product photograph helps you choose a look. The written configuration tells you what you are buying. Read them together, especially when the hairstyle, outfit or accessories are part of what attracted you to the doll.

The easiest way to avoid disappointment is to separate three things: the doll itself, the selected appearance, and the items packed with it. A hairstyle may be selectable, an outfit may be styling, and a care accessory may be a separate purchase.

## Start with the exact photographed build

Save the product link and the particular gallery image you like. A gallery can show different wigs, makeup or clothing on the same body. "Like the pictures" becomes much clearer when everyone knows which picture you mean.

Record the body code, head code and materials alongside that image. A silicone head on a TPE body is a hybrid build, not a full-silicone doll. The face name alone may not identify the complete combination.

Use this simple reading sheet:

| Photographed detail | Written confirmation to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Hairstyle | Wig or implanted hair, colour, length and included quantity |
| Clothing | Exact garments listed as included, if any |
| Shoes and jewellery | Named included items rather than visual presence |
| Makeup and skin finish | Selected finish and its price status |
| Extra head or care tools | Quantity, compatibility and whether separately shipped |

Leave an uncertain item marked "ask," not "included." That keeps your comparison useful without turning every photograph into a packing list.

## Read "same as image" one option at a time

FunWest's [official FAQ](https://funwestdoll.com/faq/) explains "Same as Image" as applying to the particular option selected. Its example is about matching an appearance choice to promotional photography. It is not a statement that every object in the scene ships with the doll.

For a hypothetical listing, imagine that the hairstyle says "as shown" while accessories say "no add-on." The hairstyle instruction does not override the accessory selection. Ask separately about the outfit if it is important to your decision.

There is also a concrete exclusion on [FunWest's Bea FWD041 factory listing](https://funwestdoll.com/product/fwd041-006-european-bea/): the photographed outfit is for display and is not included in that factory offer. This does not settle a separate DollWOW clothing selection; on the [DollWOW Bea page](/products/funwest-dolls-bea-162cm-f-cup-tpe-companion-doll-fwd041), check the clothing add-on and final summary independently.

Likewise, a photograph showing two hairstyles need not mean two wigs are included. Ask which reference belongs to the selected build and how many wigs the order contains.

## An included wig is not an included wardrobe

IDO's [factory configuration description](https://www.idodoll.com/ido-advantage/) lists a premium wig among its standard items. That is useful, specific information. It does not establish inclusion of every photographed garment, necklace or pair of shoes.

Keep hair construction separate from hair styling. Implanted eyebrows or eyelashes do not establish that scalp hair is implanted. If changing hairstyles matters to you, compare the actual hair choice using our [wig versus implanted-hair guide](/learn/implanted-hair-vs-wig).

For clothes you plan to buy separately, use the doll's measurements rather than the dress size you associate with its appearance. A close-fitting campaign outfit may not represent an easy everyday dressing choice.

## Check tools and accessories by name

"Care set" and "accessory pack" are useful labels only when their contents are listed. Ask for the item names and quantities, and check whether a tool is appropriate for the exact model.

An extra head also needs its own identification. Confirm the head code, material, connector and any appearance choices. "Free extra head" does not answer those questions by itself.

Do not buy duplicate tools just because a photograph leaves them out. Equally, do not assume essential care supplies are included because another model came with them. Your order's written contents are the useful reference.

## Save an appearance reference and a contents list

Before ordering, keep a short record of the selected doll, chosen appearance, paid additions and included loose items. Ask support to resolve any disagreement between the gallery, option summary and description.

For an eligible custom build, factory photographs can help compare visible appearance with that record. A photograph of the completed doll still does not necessarily show every packed accessory. Review visible configuration and package contents as separate questions.
