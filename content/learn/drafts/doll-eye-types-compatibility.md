# Fixed, Movable and Replaceable Doll Eyes: What to Confirm

Eye colour, movable eyes and replaceable eyes are three different choices. None automatically means the eyelids blink. Before ordering, establish what the exact head supports and which changes are intended for the owner rather than the factory.

The useful question is not simply "Can the eyes move?" It is "Which part moves, how is that adjustment supported, and does it apply to this head version?"

## Separate four features

| Feature | What it tells you | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colour selection | The ordered iris appearance | Owner replacement or movement |
| Gaze adjustment | A supported way to change eye direction | Automatic tracking or blinking |
| Replaceable eyes | A supported replacement arrangement | Compatibility with any same-sized eye |
| Movable eyelids | A specifically documented eyelid function | Inclusion on every head in the brand |

Treat "fixed" as a term to clarify. It may describe a non-adjustable gaze, an eye not intended for removal, or simply a seller's comparison with another option. Ask for the actual supported behaviour.

This distinction also helps when looking at photographs. A sideways gaze shows an appearance, but it does not explain whether the eye was positioned at the factory or adjusted later.

## Two factory examples with different meanings

FANREAL's official Vanessa 170 cm D-cup listing describes its default eyes as moving and presents eye codes E01 through E16. The colour menu and the movement description are separate pieces of that product specification. They do not establish a universal replacement size or removal procedure. [FANREAL's factory configuration](https://www.fanreal.com/product/vanessa-ae/)

Irontech's ROS MAX warning is more restrictive about the eye area: the head does not include dynamic eye movement, and the factory says not to pull its upper or lower eyelids to simulate blinking. That warning should not be turned into an assumption about every other eye mechanism. [Irontech's ROS MAX eye guidance](https://www.irontechdoll.com/help-faq/)

Use this two-source matrix instead of treating every eye label as the same mechanism:

| Exact source scope | Colour reference | Documented movement | Replacement or blinking conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FANREAL Vanessa 170 cm D-cup factory configuration | E01-E16 choices | Default eyes described as moving | Neither owner replacement nor blinking established by that description |
| Irontech ROS MAX head family, factory FAQ warning | Confirm the selected head's own reference | No dynamic eye movement; do not pull eyelids to imitate blinking | No blinking permission or universal replacement procedure; warning alone does not define manual gaze adjustment |

The second row is a restriction, not proof that every part of the eye is permanently fixed. If a seller says "fixed eyes," ask whether that means gaze, removal or eyelids. A colour choice does not override a mechanical restriction, and a newer-sounding head version does not automatically add blinking. Both sources were checked on October 16, 2026.

## Make a head-specific compatibility note

For the head you want, record the complete code, version and material. Then add the supported eye colours, whether gaze adjustment is documented, and whether replacement is intended for owners or service support.

If replacement matters, ask for the approved part reference and the matching head version. An advertised diameter alone does not confirm how a component seats, stays in place or clears the eyelids. Do not buy a generic replacement just because the colour looks right.

The same caution applies when ordering an extra head. Confirm the eyes supplied with that extra head separately from the main doll. A shared face name does not necessarily mean both heads use identical construction.

## Choose colour from the right reference

Use the manufacturer's labelled eye reference for the applicable head rather than identifying a colour from a heavily styled photograph. Save the code as well as the descriptive name. "Blue" can cover more than one reference choice.

For a custom build, check that the code appears in the final configuration. For a stocked unit, ask which eyes are already installed and whether any change is offered before dispatch. A custom menu is not proof that a warehouse unit can be altered.

If the desired look depends on a particular gaze, request relevant confirmation instead of assuming you can recreate a gallery pose yourself.

## Do not turn selection into an adjustment experiment

Follow the exact head instructions for any permitted movement. Do not pull lashes or eyelids, insert tools or force an eye because another brand's tutorial makes it look straightforward. Makeup, lashes and the surrounding material are part of the assembly you are protecting.

If an eye appears displaced on arrival, photograph it in place and ask for guidance before trying to reseat it. Keep the order's head and eye references with the request. That makes a compatible answer much easier to obtain.

## Buy the appearance and function you actually need

For many buyers, the right colour and a convincing neutral gaze matter more than extra movement. If changing expressions is important, select a head with clearly documented support for that purpose rather than expecting a fixed design to do more.
