## Conventional Companion or Robot?

RealDoll's current official range separates Classic companions from Robot products. A conventional silicone doll and a robotic product should not be compared as if they deliver the same experience.

A conventional companion may include an articulated skeleton and supported options, but that does not mean it can move independently, speak, learn, sense touch, or run AI software. Terms such as “robot,” “AI,” “interactive,” and “smart” need product-level evidence.

Before paying for a technology claim, ask:

1. What exact function is demonstrated on the product being sold?
2. Is the function in the head, body, an app, or a separate accessory?
3. Does it require Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, an account, or a subscription?
4. What personal data does it collect or store?
5. What happens if the app, server, battery, motor, or electronics stop working?
6. Who provides software updates, parts, and repairs?
7. Is the shown function included in the quoted configuration?

DollWow's products should be treated as conventional dolls unless the exact listing clearly identifies and supports a powered feature. If your main goal is advanced robotics, tell our team first. We will not relabel a standard doll to force a match.

## Compare Premium Silicone Correctly

“Silicone” is a material family, not a complete quality grade. Two full-silicone dolls can differ substantially in sculpting, softness, surface finish, reinforcement, body weight, hand and foot detail, makeup, and maintenance.

Check the exact listing for:

- full-silicone body and head construction
- removable or fixed head design
- firmness or softness choices
- weight-reduction construction where offered
- standing system and skeleton options
- gel-filled or layered areas where supported
- implanted or wig-based hair
- painted, implanted, or interchangeable details
- visible seams, connection points, and care limits
- listed weight in both pounds and kilograms

Do not infer medical, hypoallergenic, body-safe, or platinum-cured status from the word silicone alone. Those claims require manufacturer documentation for the selected product.

Use the [TPE vs silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) if material is still undecided. A well-matched TPE or hybrid doll may suit a buyer better than full silicone, even when silicone carries a more premium reputation.

## Compare Realism Without Shopping From One Photograph

Premium presentation can make almost any doll look convincing in one carefully styled image. Judge realism across several kinds of evidence:

- front, profile, and three-quarter face views
- body proportions without extreme camera distortion
- close-ups of eyes, lips, hands, feet, and skin transitions
- consistency across product and factory images
- the exact head, body, tone, wig, eyes, and makeup selected
- neutral lighting as well as promotional lighting
- poses the skeleton can maintain safely

Separate the physical sculpt from styling. Wigs, clothing, makeup, lighting, and image editing can change the impression substantially. Ask whether the pictured styling is standard, optional, or only promotional.

## Common Comparison Mistakes

### Treating RealDoll as a generic product name

RealDoll is a specific brand. A “realistic doll” listing does not become a RealDoll because a seller uses similar wording.

### Confusing RealDoll with RealSexDoll

The names are similar, but the businesses are different. Confirm which company and URL a review, policy, or offer concerns.

### Comparing a robot with a conventional doll

A skeleton, heating option, or electronic head feature does not automatically make a product a robot. Compare demonstrated functions and support requirements.

### Assuming every silicone doll is comparable

Full-silicone products can differ in formulation, softness, reinforcement, finish, weight, sculpting, and care. Compare the exact body and head.

### Choosing from promotional styling

Wigs, clothing, makeup, lighting, and editing can dominate the impression. Confirm what the selected product includes.

### Ignoring weight and access

Premium full-silicone dolls can be substantial. Check pounds and kilograms, doors, stairs, cleaning space, and storage before ordering.

### Comparing the headline price

Match product form, material, configuration, technology, delivery, and ownership needs before calling one option less expensive.
