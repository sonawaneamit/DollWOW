# WM Dolls Buying Guide: Materials, Sizes, And Options

By Alex, Doll Collector and DollWow Product Educator with 20+ Years of Experience

## Quick Answer

WM Dolls is a long-established manufacturer with one of the broadest selections of TPE and silicone bodies, heads, sizes, and appearance options. That choice is useful, but it makes the exact model more important than the brand name. Set a handling-weight limit, choose material, then confirm the body, head, options, authenticity, and final configuration before production.

## Is WM Doll A Good Brand?

WM is a practical brand to consider when you want a large choice of body proportions, face styles, sizes, and configuration paths inside one manufacturer system. It can suit a first-time buyer who needs room to compare, as well as an experienced collector who already knows the measurements and features they prefer.

The same breadth creates a risk: a review of one WM model cannot describe the entire range. Current DollWow listings include shorter and taller bodies, substantial differences in listed weight, TPE and silicone construction, female and male models, and different option sets. A useful WM review must therefore answer, “Which WM build fits this buyer?” rather than award one blanket score.

**WM may be a strong fit if you want:**

- Many body and head combinations
- A choice between TPE and silicone construction
- Female and male full-body models
- A wide range of sizes and proportions
- Product-specific customization with a human check before production
- An authenticity record from an approved seller

**Pause and compare carefully if you want:**

- The lightest doll without checking the exact listed weight
- A feature seen on another WM product but not shown for your model
- A simple fixed order with almost no configuration decisions
- A guaranteed electronic or powered function based only on brand marketing

## WM Dolls Brand History

WM Doll traces its manufacturing history to **2010**. The company, Zhongshan Jinsan Mannequin Co., Ltd., is based in Zhongshan, Guangdong, China. An official Zhongshan government profile also dates the manufacturer to 2010 and describes its development from bionic-skin products into newer technical systems.

WM became widely known for TPE dolls and a large body-and-head catalog. The manufacturer later added silicone products, newer TPE material names, lightweight constructions, revised hand and skeleton systems, and selected powered or AI-related projects.

For buyers, the useful part of that history is continuity and range. The less useful part is assuming that every old or new feature belongs to every current product. WM has multiple material, body, head, and option generations. Always identify the exact product being ordered.

## What WM Dolls Is Known For

### A broad body and head library

WM's defining strength is variety. Buyers can compare many heights, proportions, cup sizes, face styles, and presentation directions. This can make it easier to find a close visual match, but it also increases the chance of confusing a head image with a complete configured product.

Record the body code and head model separately. Then verify that the selected head, neck connection, skin tone, makeup, eyes, and hair path are supported together.

### TPE experience

WM built much of its reputation around TPE. Current WM TPE products span different sizes and weights, so “TPE” does not mean light, compact, or identical in feel. Formulation, body geometry, internal construction, and options all affect the result.

WM also uses newer material names in manufacturer literature. A label such as S-TPE should be tied to a specific current product and care sheet. Do not assume that every WM TPE listing uses the same generation or treatment routine.

### Silicone expansion

Current DollWow products also include full-silicone WM models. Silicone can provide a more defined surface and facial finish, but body weight still varies widely. Some current WM silicone listings are lighter than much shorter TPE bodies, which is a useful reminder to read the weight rather than infer it from height or material.

### Female and male models

WM offers both female and male full-body products. Male buyers should compare shoulder width, chest, waist, hips, anatomy, height, weight, and storage route. A 175 cm / 5 ft 9 in TPE male body and a 186 cm / 6 ft 1 in silicone male body can have very different listed weights because construction matters as much as height.

### Authenticity and traceability

WM publishes an anti-counterfeiting code system intended to help identify genuine products and connect them with production records. When authenticity matters, buy through an approved seller, keep the final build details, and retain the manufacturer's code or documentation supplied with the doll.

## WM TPE Versus Silicone

| Construction | Why a buyer may choose it | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TPE | Softer-feeling material path, broad body choice, and often a lower starting price | Exact material generation, listed weight, stain prevention, care treatment, and supported options |
| Full silicone | Defined sculpting and surface finish with a different care and repair path | Exact body construction, softness, head pairing, listed weight, and supported upgrades |
| Mixed or head/body combination | A specific head finish paired with another body construction where offered | Exact head and body materials, neck compatibility, color match, and care for both components |

Material should narrow the shortlist, not end the decision. Read the [TPE versus silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls), then compare two exact WM products. Include handling, storage, care, repair, appearance, configured price, and supported features.

## Size And Weight: Read Both

WM's range demonstrates why height alone is a poor handling guide. Current products can differ by dozens of pounds even when they are close in height. Body proportions, internal construction, material, and weight-reduction systems all matter.

Before choosing, plan these four movements:

1. Delivery point to the private room
2. Storage position to cleaning area
3. Cleaning area back to drying and storage
4. Safe repositioning without pulling hands, feet, neck, or head

If any movement feels unrealistic at the listed weight, choose a lighter body or plan two-person assistance. The [size and weight section of the complete buyer's guide](/learn/sex-doll-guide#size-and-weight-matter-more-than-buyers-expect) and [storage guide](/learn/sex-doll-storage) can help.

## WM Bodies, Heads, And Appearance Options

WM product photographs often show a particular head, body, wig, makeup, eye color, skin tone, and outfit together. Some of those details may be permanent choices, some may be optional, and some may be styling used only for the shoot.

Use this order:

1. Select the body by measurements and weight.
2. Confirm which heads fit that body.
3. Choose material and skin tone.
4. Confirm the exact eye and hair path.
5. Add skeleton, hand, foot, softness, and function options only after compatibility is checked.

Do not build the order backward from a wig or outfit. Those details can often change later, while body construction and permanent options cannot.

## Skeleton, Hands, And Standing Options

WM has published multiple skeleton and hand developments over time, including articulated or ball-jointed hand systems on selected products. The exact construction can affect pose range, finger movement, puncture resistance, and handling habits.

No finger system makes the hand load-bearing. Move fingers individually and gently. Never lift the doll by a hand, wrist, arm, head, or foot.

Standing-foot options may also be available on selected WM bodies. Confirm the exact foot construction, floor protection, balance method, and duration guidance. Standing feet are useful for supervised posing but do not prove that unsupported standing is the correct long-term storage method. See the [standing feet guide](/learn/standing-feet-sex-doll-guide).

## Softness, Weight Reduction, And Newer Features

WM products may present softness upgrades, lighter constructions, breathing-style features, powered functions, or newer technology labels. These features must be evaluated separately.

For any named option, ask:

- Is it available on this exact body and head?
- Does it change weight, softness, surface durability, or care?
- Does it conflict with another selected option?
- What hardware and power source does it use?
- What is included in the final configured price?
- What support exists after delivery?

WM's involvement in AI and emotional-robot development does not turn every WM doll into an AI or robotic product. A conventional doll, a powered feature, an electronic head, conversational software, and an integrated robot are different product levels. The [AI dolls guide](/learn/ai-sex-dolls) explains how to separate them.

## How To Choose A WM Doll

### 1. Decide what you can handle

Set a maximum weight in pounds and kilograms. Include stairs, tight corners, bathrooms, and storage access. Do this before choosing a face.

### 2. Choose a material path

Compare TPE and silicone ownership. Consider feel, finish, stain risk, cleaning, treatment guidance, repair, and final price.

### 3. Shortlist bodies by measurements

Use height, weight, chest, waist, hips, shoulders where relevant, and body proportions. Do not use cup size or height as a complete fit description.

### 4. Add a compatible head

Confirm the exact head model, neck connection, material, skin-tone match, eyes, hair, and makeup. A head pictured on one body may not recreate the same appearance on another.

### 5. Rank options by importance

Separate essential construction choices from preferred upgrades and replaceable styling. This keeps the order focused when compatibility or budget forces a tradeoff.

### 6. Review the final build

DollWow's [Human Build Check](/care-for-life) reviews the selected product and configuration before production. For eligible custom builds, factory media can provide a further comparison with the approved order before shipment. Specific media remains dependent on the order path.

## How To Read WM Doll Reviews

WM reviews are most useful when they identify the exact body, head, material, options, seller, delivery path, and ownership period. A review that says only “WM Doll” leaves too much unknown.

Use owner reports to learn what questions to ask, not as proof that your selected build will be identical. Look for:

- Exact body and head identifiers
- Measured or listed weight
- TPE or silicone construction
- Permanent options and softer areas
- Photos that clearly match the reviewed product
- Time owned and frequency of handling
- Cleaning, storage, staining, joint, and repair context
- Seller service separated from manufacturer quality

## Ready-To-Ship Versus Custom WM Dolls

A ready-to-ship WM listing is a completed configuration with less option flexibility. Confirm the exact item, current warehouse, stock state, included build, and delivery estimate.

A custom WM listing starts with a base body and head path, then moves through supported options and production. Compare the configured price, not only the starting price. The [ready-to-ship versus custom guide](/learn/ready-to-ship-vs-custom-sex-dolls) covers the decision.

## Common WM Buying Mistakes

### Treating WM as one product

The range includes different constructions, sizes, and option generations. Judge the exact body and head.

### Choosing by height without checking weight

Current products show that a taller silicone body can weigh far less than a shorter TPE body.

### Copying every detail from a promotional image

Separate permanent configuration from wig, clothing, lighting, and styling.

### Assuming a feature is brand-wide

Confirm compatibility for the exact body, head, material, and other selected options.

### Ignoring authenticity records

Use an approved seller and retain the manufacturer documentation and build record.

### Comparing only starting prices

Compare the final configuration, shipping, applicable tax, service, and ownership support.
