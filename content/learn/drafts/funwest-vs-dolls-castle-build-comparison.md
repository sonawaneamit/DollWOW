# FunWest vs Dolls Castle: Comparing Two Real TPE-Body Builds

FunWest Bea and Dolls Castle Nyla offer two distinct starting points for a TPE-body purchase. Bea is shorter and has a lower listed weight. Nyla is taller and pairs its TPE body with a silicone head.

If these two are on your shortlist, let's look at what the differences mean for your home and budget. This is a comparison of their listings, rather than a hands-on test or a verdict on either brand's value.

## Check the size difference first

Nyla is 21 cm taller, and the listed weights differ by 5.3 kg. Give the proportions, space available and your preferred configuration a little attention before choosing.

The factories' published tables also show different proportions. [FunWest's Bea page](https://funwestdoll.com/product/fwd041-006-european-bea/) lists an 80 cm bust, 55 cm waist and 85 cm hips. [Dolls Castle's Nyla page](https://dolls-castle.com/product/curvy-slim-tpe-sex-doll-183cm-nyla/) lists 83 cm, 57 cm and 97 cm respectively.

Nyla isn't just the same shape made taller. Alongside the face you love, give the silhouette, storage space and clothing fit a little attention: this is the doll you'll be moving and caring for.

Nyla has hip figures of 97 cm and 97.5 cm, and weight figures of 42 kg and 42.5 kg. Ask us to confirm your selected build's measurements before buying a tightly fitted garment or case.

## Understand Nyla's hybrid construction

Nyla's DollWOW description explicitly identifies the JS061 silicone head and TPE body. The words "silicone head" do not mean that its entire body is silicone.

That matters when comparing care guidance and optional body materials. Keep the head and body construction in separate fields on your shortlist. A material change supported on one body should not be transferred to the other model by assumption.

Similarly, compare the precise head version and written selections rather than inferring functions from a facial photograph. A styled expression is not a specification for a movable mechanism.

## Compare the included configuration before upgrades

Nyla includes the EVO skeleton and articulated fingers. Check which hands and skeleton come with your preferred Bea configuration too, so each doll has the features you want.

Start with the features you want, then check what's included and what costs extra. Nyla's included features shouldn't be charged twice, and a feature in a higher Bea preset may not come with her base build.

If your main requirements are appearance and practical handling, the base build may already answer them. More selected options can raise the cost without improving the part of ownership you care about most.

## What does the $650 gap cover?

The $650 difference between these displayed base prices is not the price of a silicone head upgrade. It also spans different bodies, dimensions and included configurations.

Choose the supported options you'd actually order, note the subtotal and ask about any compatibility questions. Then compare delivery charges and taxes for the same address.

A supplier's direct promotion may differ from the DollWOW offer. Check that the seller, configuration and terms match the price you're comparing.

Bea deserves closer attention if its shorter proportions and lower listed weight suit your space. Nyla deserves closer attention if the taller silhouette and identified hybrid pairing are what you want. Neither point establishes a universal quality or durability winner.
