# FunWest vs Dolls Castle: Comparing Two Real TPE-Body Builds

FunWest Bea and Dolls Castle Nyla offer two distinct starting points for a TPE-body purchase. Bea is shorter and has a lower listed weight. Nyla is taller and pairs its TPE body with a silicone head.

That makes them useful for a practical comparison, not a controlled contest between brands. If you are choosing between these particular dolls, the differences below matter more than a general claim that one manufacturer is better value.

## The two builds being compared

The following DollWOW listing details were checked on 16 October 2026. Prices are displayed USD base-configuration prices, before shipping, tax and checkout discounts, not normalized upgrade totals.

## Check the size difference first

Nyla is 21 cm taller and 5.5 kg heavier by the listed figures. Those arithmetic differences are useful when planning space; they are not measured handling-test results.

The factories' published tables also show different proportions. [FunWest's Bea page](https://funwestdoll.com/product/fwd041-006-european-bea/) lists an 80 cm bust, 55 cm waist and 85 cm hips. [Dolls Castle's Nyla page](https://dolls-castle.com/product/curvy-slim-tpe-sex-doll-183cm-nyla/) lists 83 cm, 57 cm and 97 cm respectively.

The difference is therefore not simply the same shape made taller. Compare the full silhouette, storage space and dressing requirements. A preferred face should not distract from the physical build you will move and care for.

One measurement needs clarification: Nyla's factory table gives 97 cm hips, while the checked DollWOW specification gives 97.5 cm. The factory's prose also rounds its weight to 42 kg, while its table and DollWOW list 42.5 kg. This comparison uses the table's weight and keeps each source's hip figure separate; confirm the current build's measurements before buying a tightly fitted garment or case.

## Understand Nyla's hybrid construction

Nyla's DollWOW description explicitly identifies the JS061 silicone head and TPE body. The words "silicone head" do not mean that its entire body is silicone.

That matters when comparing care guidance and optional body materials. Keep the head and body construction in separate fields on your shortlist. A material change supported on one body should not be transferred to the other model by assumption.

Similarly, compare the precise head version and written selections rather than inferring functions from a facial photograph. A styled expression is not a specification for a movable mechanism.

## Compare the included configuration before upgrades

Nyla's factory page identifies the EVO skeleton and articulated fingers in its included configuration, consistent with the checked DollWOW listing. That is not a reason to assume the two dolls' skeletons or hands perform identically.

Write down the features you actually need and check their included or paid status on each product. Do not charge Nyla again for an included feature, or assume Bea includes a feature because it appears in one of its higher presets.

If your main requirements are appearance and practical handling, the base build may already answer them. More selected options can raise the cost without improving the part of ownership you care about most.

## Normalize only what can be normalized

The $650 difference between these displayed base prices is not the price of a silicone head upgrade. It also spans different bodies, dimensions and included configurations.

For a fair personal budget, select the supported choices you would genuinely order on each page. Record the configured subtotal and any remaining compatibility questions. Then compare delivery charges and taxes on the same destination basis.

Do not use a supplier's direct promotion as the DollWOW selling price. The seller, configuration and offer terms all need to match.

## Keep the offer and the weight definition together

A 23 October 2026 DollWOW catalog spot-check returned the same $1,349 Bea and $1,999 Nyla base prices shown above. Both records describe custom-made offers. That is a dated price-field check, not a fresh factory measurement, physical-stock confirmation or delivery guarantee.

The current product records label 37 kg and 42.5 kg simply as Weight. The earlier factory references use net figures, but those labels alone do not demonstrate that both totals include the selected head and the same installed options. Treat the stated 5.5 kg difference as arithmetic between source entries, not a verified difference between the complete dolls you would receive. Ask for the same weight definition before deciding which you can comfortably handle.

Bea's head 006 and Nyla's JS061 pairing should stay on the comparison record. Nyla's documented silicone-head/TPE-body construction is not proof that a similar-looking head on another product has the same material or functions. Keep the previously identified hip-measurement discrepancy open rather than averaging the figures.

For the price decision, write two separate lines: what is supplied in the chosen offer, and what you are adding. Record each extra as included, a confirmed charge, unavailable or awaiting confirmation. This avoids charging twice for an included feature without assuming a menu choice is already part of the base package.

## Choose between the actual dolls

Bea deserves closer attention if its shorter proportions and lower listed weight suit your space. Nyla deserves closer attention if the taller silhouette and identified hybrid pairing are what you want. Neither point establishes a universal quality or durability winner.
