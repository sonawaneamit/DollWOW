## Start With the Exact Look You Want

Realism is not one feature. Decide which visual details actually control the purchase:

- face shape and expression
- eyes and eye alignment
- skin finish and makeup transitions
- head-to-body proportion
- hands, fingers, feet, and nails
- hairline, wig, or implanted hair
- body proportions and clothing fit
- how the selected options change the final build

Inspect front, profile, three-quarter, full-body, hands, feet, and surface close-ups. Ask whether the images show the exact head and body combination or a manufacturer styling example.

Use the [most realistic sex dolls guide](/learn/most-realistic-sex-dolls) for a five-pass inspection method. If a photographed detail is non-negotiable, ask DollWow to confirm whether it is standard, optional, or unavailable on the selected product.

## Understand the Construction, Not Only the Label

### Full TPE

TPE is often selected for softness and value. Compare the exact formulation, firmness, body design, listed weight, cleaning routine, stain precautions, powder guidance, and compatible repair method.

### Full silicone

Full silicone is often considered for fine sculpt detail, controlled finish, and simpler surface cleaning. It can cost more and may be heavier. Confirm whether the complete body and head are silicone and which softness, gel, or weight-related options are supported on the exact model.

### Silicone head on a TPE body

This path can combine a silicone facial finish with a TPE body. Confirm the neck connection, tone match, head-body pairing, total weight, and separate care requirements.

### Hybrid construction

Hybrid can describe more than one combination. Do not assume the materials or benefits from the word alone. Ask which areas use which material and how that affects cleaning, repair, handling, and price.

Read [TPE versus silicone sex dolls](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls), then compare current [TPE](/shop/tpe) and [silicone](/shop/silicone) listings.

## Compare Weight and Measurements Together

Height is easy to scan but does not predict ownership effort. Review:

- listed weight in pounds and kilograms
- shoulder width
- bust or chest, waist, and hips
- torso and limb proportions
- foot size and standing system
- package dimensions where available

A shorter doll can still be dense, wide, or difficult to turn during cleaning. A taller product can sometimes have a manageable listed weight because construction varies. Choose a maximum handling weight before falling in love with a photograph.

Use [lighter dolls](/shop/lightweight-sex-dolls) when frequent movement is important, and measure the delivery route and storage opening before ordering.

## Customization Should Follow the Base Product

Start with the body, head, material, height, and weight. Then verify product-specific appearance, structural, and electronic options.

Common choices can include skin tone, eyes, hair, makeup, skeleton, standing feet, softness, gel filling, heating, or electronic-head functions. Availability and pricing vary by brand, body, head, and material. Some choices conflict. Implanted hair can be incompatible with certain electronic head systems, for example.

## Common Mistakes

### Choosing realism from one image

Lighting, styling, angle, and editing can flatter a product. Compare multiple views and confirm the exact head and body.

### Treating TPE as one formulation

TPE products can differ in firmness, weight, finish, care, and repair. Verify the exact body and manufacturer guidance.

### Selecting options before the base doll

The body, head, material, and product family determine which choices are supported and compatible.

### Comparing starting prices

Use the configured and delivered order. Match the same product and options before judging value.

### Ignoring handling until delivery

Measure the route and set a maximum weight first. A beautiful doll that cannot be moved or stored safely is a poor fit.
