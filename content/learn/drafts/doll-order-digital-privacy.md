# Privacy Beyond the Box: Order Emails, Accounts and Shared Devices

Plain packaging addresses one part of privacy. Your inbox, phone notifications, saved photographs and shared computer can reveal information separately from the delivery itself.

A few settings checked before ordering can make the process more comfortable. The aim is ordinary control over who can see your purchase information, while keeping the records you need for delivery and support.

## Know what DollWOW's policy covers

DollWOW's [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) describes practical, discreet order updates, neutral billing language and information used to process, review and deliver orders. It also explains that purchase details support continuity when you need help.

That is not a promise that an order leaves no digital record. Payment providers, delivery services and your own devices can display information outside the packaging. If an exact billing description, email subject or contact method matters, ask support to confirm it before purchase.

Do not assume every message will use a particular subject line or that changing a marketing preference will stop transactional updates. Those are separate questions worth resolving directly.

## Choose an inbox you control

Use an email address you can access privately and reliably. Check whether it forwards to another account, appears in a shared mail app or sends notifications to a tablet, watch or desktop used by someone else.

Keep account recovery details current and use the security options provided by your email service. Losing access to the inbox can make order confirmation, tracking and later support harder.

Avoid solving privacy by entering a false address or one you rarely check. Missing a message about an unclear build or a delivery issue can create more difficulty than choosing a suitable inbox at the start.

## Review notifications before they arrive

Look at the devices where order-related messages could appear:

| Place | Setting or habit to review |
| --- | --- |
| Phone lock screen | Whether message previews are visible while locked |
| Shared tablet or computer | Whether the same inbox is signed in |
| Smartwatch | Whether notifications mirror the phone |
| Carrier app | Whether shipment alerts appear on shared devices |
| Browser | Whether shopping tabs reopen on startup |

The names of these settings differ by device. Use the device's own controls and check what a locked-screen notification actually reveals. You do not need to disable all useful alerts; concealing previews may be enough for your needs.

## Treat browser privacy as one layer

A private browsing window is not an anonymity guarantee. Google's [Chrome Incognito guidance](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95464?hl=en) explains that downloaded files remain and that sites and network operators can still observe activity. Private browsing also does not control what appears in your inbox or on a payment statement, or make a shared account private.

On a shared computer, sign out of shopping and email accounts after use. Avoid saving passwords into a browser profile other people use. Close product and support tabs, and check whether bookmarks or saved reading lists are shared.

If you use a personal browser profile, confirm that it is genuinely separate from other household accounts. A familiar profile icon is easy to overlook when moving between devices.

## Keep photos and order records in the right place

Factory media and support photographs may be more revealing than a short email. Before downloading or taking them, consider whether your photo library or downloads folder is shared or automatically synced elsewhere.

For support photographs, frame the relevant product detail without household documents, address labels or reflective backgrounds that add unnecessary personal information. Send information through the support route provided for your request rather than a public review or social post.

Keep the final specification, receipt and support correspondence somewhere you can find them. Privacy should not mean discarding the evidence needed to resolve a problem.

## Confirm communication needs early

Before payment, tell [support](/support) about a practical communication constraint and ask what can be accommodated. Do not assume that requesting email-only contact changes a carrier's independent delivery process.

Then review [how ordering works](/how-ordering-works) so you know when a confirmation, approval request or tracking update may need your attention. A private order works best when the communication channel is both discreet for you and dependable throughout ownership.
