# FunWest Buying Guide: Match the Product Code, Head and Material

Choose a FunWest doll by its complete identity: product/SKU code, body specification, head code, material and order path. A name can help you remember a favourite face, but it is not a reliable specification by itself.

That matters when a gallery, a stock offer and a custom listing look similar. Before comparing their prices, establish whether they represent the same build. The [FunWest collection](/brands/funwest-dolls) keeps the shopping choices together; this guide explains how to read a shortlist.

## Use Bea to understand the naming problem

The dated DollWOW listing for [Bea FWD041](/products/funwest-dolls-bea-162cm-f-cup-tpe-companion-doll-fwd041) identifies a 162 cm F-cup TPE custom build with head 006. FunWest's corresponding factory listing is titled [FWD041 #006 European Bea](https://funwestdoll.com/product/fwd041-006-european-bea/).

DollWOW also has a separate [155 cm Bea FWD028 listing](/products/funwest-dolls-bea-155cm-f-cup-tpe-companion-doll-fwd028). The shared name does not make those bodies identical. Keep the FWD code and height attached to every question you ask.

These examples identify live listings checked on 16 October 2026. FunWest labels FWD041 as the product SKU; it should not be treated as a unique body-mould code. The factory sheet for that product lists 162 cm height, 80 cm bust, 55 cm waist, 85 cm hips and 37 kg net weight. DollWOW's FWD028 listing instead gives 155 cm, 80 / 52 / 88 cm and 34 kg, with the same head 006. These are two product configurations, not measurements taken by DollWOW or proof of brand-wide option support.

## Read the material before the styling

Write the body material and head material in separate fields. If a title identifies TPE, do not silently reinterpret it as silicone because another product uses a similar face. If an alternative material appears in the exact configurator, preserve that selection in your comparison.

Ask whether changing material also changes the body version, head pairing or available finish. Without those answers, a material upgrade can become a comparison between two substantially different products.

The [materials guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) covers the broad ownership differences. Here the practical task is simpler: ensure the material you want is the material written into your chosen build.

## Build a short inclusion list

Use three columns: supplied as standard, selected extra, and not confirmed. Fill them from the current product specification, not from another model's menu or the most elaborate gallery photograph.

| Part of the offer | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Main build | Full body and head identifiers, with materials |
| Construction | Exact skeleton, hand and foot descriptions |
| Appearance | Tone, makeup, eye and hair selections |
| Installed extras | Supported choices actually selected |
| Loose items | Wig, accessories and any storage item explicitly supplied |
| Order path | Fixed stock unit or factory production |

An option name in a menu does not prove it is included. A preset is also worth opening in detail: compare its actual selections with your preferences instead of assuming the package name defines a factory standard.

Keep cosmetic choices separate from construction. A wig colour and an installed system have different consequences if you change your mind later.

## Compare practical dimensions, not just height

Use bust, waist, hip and shoulder measurements when available. They help you assess the silhouette, clothing and storage footprint. Cup letters alone are not enough to compare bodies, particularly across factories.

For weight, ask whether the figure is body-only, complete doll or packed shipment. Do not calculate an apparent saving from two differently defined figures. If the configured weight is not confirmed, leave it open and ask before choosing a build near your handling limit.

Think through the route from delivery to storage, including turns and stairs. A body that fits the final space may still be awkward to move there. Plan around your own handling needs rather than a general description such as "easy to carry."

## Decide between stock and factory order

A stock unit should be compared as the existing configuration it is. Confirm its warehouse, installed features and supplied items. Do not assume a custom product's full menu can be applied to a completed unit.

For a factory order, settle the head and body pairing before choosing permanent extras. Save a dated configuration summary and ask about any unresolved combination before production.

Read [ready-to-ship versus custom dolls](/learn/ready-to-ship-vs-custom-sex-dolls) when the same look appears through both routes. Production and transit are different stages, so compare the complete order path rather than one headline timescale.

## Choose the exact offer you can explain

A good shortlist should let you describe each candidate in one sentence with its code, material, head and order path. Then compare the inclusions and configured totals in the same currency.

Send those links to [DollWOW support](/support) when a specification needs clarification. You do not need every available upgrade; you need a supported build whose identity and practical requirements are clear.
