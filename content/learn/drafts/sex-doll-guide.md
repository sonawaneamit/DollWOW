### Step 1: decide where the doll will live

Start with the physical environment. Measure the storage space, the route from the front door, and the area where the doll will be cleaned and dried. Include door width, stairs, tight corners, and any point where the package or doll must be lifted. A discreet closet is not useful if the body cannot lie without pressure or if ventilation is poor after cleaning.

Write these measurements down in both inches and centimeters. A listing height does not describe shoulder width, hip width, bent-leg storage, or carton dimensions. When space is tight, ask support for the measurements that control the purchase rather than relying on a photograph.

### Step 2: set a handling limit

Choose a maximum weight that can be lifted and turned without unstable twisting. Compare it with a real object or supervised gym movement rather than imagining the number. A doll behaves like an awkward dead weight. It cannot shift its center of gravity, hold a rail, or help during a transfer.

Buyers with stairs, pain, limited grip, or a need for frequent cleaning should make weight a primary filter. A compact model or torso can be a sensible choice even when a larger product is affordable. A wheeled surface, lifting strap, or another adult's help may improve the routine, but equipment should not become a reason to exceed a safe personal limit.

### Step 3: choose the construction family

Decide whether TPE, silicone, a silicone-head hybrid, or a specialty construction best fits the desired feel, finish, care routine, and budget. Do this before comparing dozens of faces. Material is not a complete quality score, but it determines which products belong in the first shortlist.

When softness is the priority, compare the manufacturer's formulation rather than assuming every TPE body feels the same. When fine surface finish is the priority, compare silicone products but still check firmness, weight, and painted-detail care. When budget is the priority, compare the configured total and current care requirements rather than the base price alone.

### Step 4: choose order type and deadline

If the doll is needed by a particular date, start with verified warehouse inventory and leave room for carrier delays. A factory order gives more control over the final build but includes production, approval, dispatch, and transit stages. A date shown as an estimate is not a guarantee unless DollWow can operationally support the promise in writing.

### Step 5: compare the final configuration

Create a simple shortlist of three to five exact products. Record the body, head, material, height, weight, starting price, selected options, order type, and unanswered questions. Remove a product when an essential fact cannot be confirmed. More options do not automatically make an order better; each option adds cost, compatibility questions, or production complexity.

### Step 6: plan the first month of ownership

Before checkout, know where the doll will be inspected, cleaned, dried, dressed, and stored. Have the manufacturer-approved basics ready, but avoid buying strong cleaners, generic adhesives, or powders before the material is confirmed. Save the order summary, support conversation, and product screenshots. These records make delivery inspection and any later support request much easier.

## TPE, Silicone, and Hybrid Construction

TPE and silicone are families of formulations, not single universal recipes. Two manufacturers can use materials with noticeably different softness, oil behavior, finish, tear resistance, and care requirements. Brand and product instructions therefore matter more than generic claims.

| Factor | TPE often offers | Silicone often offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feel | Softer and more flexible in many formulations | Firmer feel in many standard formulations, with softer premium variants available |
| Surface detail | Good realism with formulation-dependent finish | Often chosen for fine sculpt and painted detail |
| Staining | Usually needs close attention to dark fabrics and dye transfer | Often less absorbent, but still needs stain prevention |
| Surface care | May require product-specific powder or renewal care | Often simpler to wipe and rinse at the surface |
| Price | Common at lower entry prices | Common at higher entry prices |
| Weight | Depends heavily on body construction | Can be heavy, although lightweight technologies exist |

Neither material wins every comparison. Choose based on the actual formulation, body construction, handling needs, care routine, and total price. Avoid blanket claims such as "odor-free," "hypoallergenic," or "medical grade" unless the manufacturer provides documentation for the exact product.

A hybrid doll may use a silicone head with a TPE body or another approved combination. Confirm the neck connection, color match, head weight, and separate care requirements. Do not assume that any head from a brand fits every body from that brand.

### Understanding TPE

TPE stands for thermoplastic elastomer, a broad family of materials that combine elastic behavior with thermoplastic processing. In the doll category, manufacturers tune formulations for softness, stretch, surface feel, color, and manufacturing needs. This is why one TPE doll can feel or behave differently from another.

TPE is often selected for softness and value. Many formulations have a flexible, yielding feel, particularly in areas with more material volume. The same softness can make the surface more vulnerable to pressure marks, tearing at stressed areas, or dye transfer. Formulation, body geometry, internal foam, and handling all affect the result.

Some TPE formulations release process oils over time. That does not justify applying the same powdering schedule to every product. Ask which surface product the manufacturer approves, how often it is expected, and whether the face or painted areas need separate treatment. Avoid talc inhalation and do not substitute a scented or medicated powder merely because it has a similar texture.

TPE care usually rewards gentle handling. Support the weight instead of pulling limbs, avoid forcing joints, protect the surface from dark fabrics, and store without sharp folds or prolonged compression. Heat guidance should come from the manufacturer. A temperature mentioned for another TPE product is not a safe operating limit for the selected doll.

### Understanding silicone

Silicone is also a family of formulations. Manufacturers may vary catalyst system, hardness, pigments, fillers, surface finish, and the way soft areas are constructed. Full-silicone does not describe one uniform feel.

Silicone is often chosen for sculpt and surface detail. It can support finely defined facial features, texture, body painting, veins, freckles, and makeup. Some manufacturers use very soft silicone in breasts, buttocks, or selected body regions while maintaining firmer structure elsewhere. Others prioritize lower weight or tear resistance. These choices can create noticeable differences between brands.

Silicone surfaces are often described as less absorbent than TPE, but they are not immune to staining, abrasion, compression, or damage. Dark fabric should still be tested. Makeup, eyelashes, implanted hair, and painted details remain delicate. Cleaning a plain silicone body surface is different from scrubbing a finished face.

Silicone can also be heavy. Newer foam cores and weight-reduction methods may reduce mass, but marketing names do not provide the final number. Compare the listed weight. The 24-product shortlist in this guide includes full-silicone examples from about `57.3 lb / 26.0 kg` to more than `100 lb / 45.4 kg`, with several weights still awaiting confirmation.

### Hybrid and component-level care

Mixed construction means the buyer should identify each component. A silicone head on a TPE body is common, but other combinations exist. Ask which cleaner, lubricant, powder, repair material, and storage method applies to each part. A product safe for the body may damage face paint, adhesive, eyelashes, or electronics.

The neck connection deserves attention. Confirm connector type, thread, adapter, and whether the chosen head is approved for the body. A heavy head can affect neck handling and storage. Skin-tone names may also vary between materials, so request a confirmed match instead of assuming identical option labels produce an identical color.

### How to make a material decision

Use this sequence:

1. Decide the desired surface feel and finish.
2. Set a maximum handling weight.
3. Compare exact products in both materials.
4. Review stain prevention, cleaning, drying, and ongoing surface care.
5. Check whether the desired head, body, and options exist in that construction.
6. Compare the complete configured price.
7. Ask for clarification when a named formulation or care claim matters.

A buyer who values softness may prefer one TPE formulation or an ultra-soft silicone region. A buyer who values detailed facial finish may prefer a silicone head without needing a full-silicone body. A buyer with a strict handling limit may choose whichever exact product meets the weight constraint. Material is a decision tool, not a status label.

### Claims that need evidence

Treat the following words as claims that require product-specific documentation: body-safe, medical-grade, hypoallergenic, odor-free, antibacterial, non-toxic, tear-proof, stain-proof, waterproof, self-healing, and maintenance-free. A retailer repeating the words does not establish them. Ask what test, certification, formulation, or manufacturer statement supports the description.

The same caution applies to lifespan. A seller cannot know how long an individual product will last without considering formulation, construction, frequency of handling, storage, cleaning, and accidental damage. A range offered as marketing should not become a guarantee.

## Advanced Materials Need Brand-Level Verification

Terms such as S-TPE, ultra-soft silicone, SLE, gel-filled areas, and lightweight construction may describe meaningful manufacturer technologies. They may also be used inconsistently across the market. Ask what the named feature changes on the exact body: softness, weight, finish, care, repair, compatibility, or price.

When a special material is important to the purchase, confirm:

- Which body or head uses it.
- Whether it changes softness, weight, durability, or care.
- Whether it is standard or a paid option.
- Whether it changes production time.
- Which cleaning, powder, lubricant, and storage instructions apply.

Manufacturer terminology belongs in the brand or product section. It should not be presented as a universal industry standard without evidence.

### Named TPE developments

Terms such as S-TPE or other enhanced TPE names may refer to a manufacturer's attempt to change softness, resilience, tear behavior, oil release, or finish. The name alone does not establish how much the formulation differs from standard TPE or whether it is available across every body.

Before using a named TPE technology as a buying reason, ask which current products use it, whether the head and body both use it, and which care instructions differ. If the brand publishes comparative test data, record what was tested and under which conditions. A multiplier such as "eight times stronger" is incomplete without a test method, comparison material, sample geometry, and source.

### Named silicone and lightweight systems

Manufacturers may offer ultra-soft silicone, platinum-cure descriptions, soft-butt or soft-breast systems, foam cores, lightweight skeletons, and proprietary reduced-weight bodies. Each label should be connected to a listed body and a current weight.

Softness can introduce tradeoffs. A softer region may need more support, gentler clothing, or different expectations around compression. A lighter body may use more internal foam, which can change the feel or distribution of weight. Neither change is automatically an improvement for every buyer.

### Gel-filled and layered areas

Some dolls offer gel-filled breasts, buttocks, thighs, or other soft regions. Confirm what is filled, whether it is standard or optional, and how the area should be handled. Avoid sharp pressure, prolonged compression, or unsupported claims about the filler. If damage occurs, do not attempt a generic puncture repair without the manufacturer's instruction.

### Evidence checklist for a special material

- Official name and manufacturer.
- Exact bodies or heads that use it.
- Current listed weight for the selected build.
- Stated purpose, such as softness, detail, or weight reduction.
- Care differences from the brand's standard material.
- Compatibility with heating, electronics, implanted hair, or other options.
- Repair path and approved materials.
- Source date and any stated test conditions.

When these details are unavailable, the honest answer is that the feature needs confirmation. Popularity and confident marketing language do not prove how a specific product works.

## How to Read Product Specifications

A specification table looks objective, but it still needs interpretation. Values can describe a base body, a body with one head, a packaged shipment, or a particular material version. Translations and catalog updates can also create conflicts. Treat specifications as evidence to reconcile, not numbers to copy without context.

### Identify what was measured

For every important value, ask which physical configuration it describes. A body weight may exclude the head. A package weight may include accessories and protective materials. Height may be measured with or without the head, feet, or standing hardware. Bust or chest measurements can vary with soft construction and measuring tension.

The best listing makes the measurement basis clear. When it does not, ask support before using the value to set a handling limit or compare shipping.

### Preserve the source value

If a manufacturer supplies `165 cm`, DollWow can display approximately `5 ft 5 in / 165 cm`. The conversion helps a US reader, but `5 ft 5 in` should not replace the original value in the product record. Likewise, a source weight of `35 kg` converts to approximately `77.2 lb / 35 kg`; rounding it to `77 lb` is appropriate for display, while the original remains the reference.

Useful conversion constants are:

- `1 in = 2.54 cm`
- `1 ft = 12 in`
- `1 kg = 2.20462 lb`
- `1 lb = 0.453592 kg`
- Celsius from Fahrenheit: `(F - 32) x 5/9`
- Fahrenheit from Celsius: `(C x 9/5) + 32`

Conversions should be consistent across the guide and storefront. They should not add false precision. If the source says approximately `80 lb`, publishing `36.2874 kg` suggests accuracy that does not exist. Use approximately `80 lb / 36 kg`.

### Height and body scale

Height is most useful for visual scale and space planning. It is not a complete proxy for clothing size, weight, or ease of handling. Review shoulder, torso, hip, and limb measurements whenever those factors affect the decision.

For a seated or torso product, total height may describe a different orientation than a standing full body. Check product diagrams and do not compare the number as though both products use the same reference points.

### Weight and shipping weight

Product weight helps plan lifting and storage. Shipping weight helps plan delivery and can include the carton, foam, accessories, head, and other components. Both matter, but for different stages.

If only shipping weight is available, do not publish it as body weight. If only body weight is available, do not assume the packaged carton is equally manageable. Request the missing value when it controls the purchase.

### Circumference measurements

Bust or chest, waist, and hip values can help with clothing and body comparison. Check whether bust refers to the fullest point and whether underbust is listed separately. Softness and gel filling can affect how a garment fits even when the circumference appears correct.

Use flexible measuring tape without compressing the surface. Garment sizing varies by brand, so match actual garment measurements and stretch rather than relying only on labels such as small or medium.

### Fixed and removable components

Specifications should identify removable inserts, heads, wigs, eyes, and electronic modules where relevant. "Removable" can describe a component intended for routine removal or one intended only for service. Confirm the expected frequency and connection method.

### Material labels

Check material separately for the body, head, insert, and soft or gel-filled regions. A listing titled "silicone doll" may refer to a full silicone build, a silicone head with a TPE body, or a selectable material. The configured product determines the claim.

Named materials such as platinum silicone, ultra-soft formulations, or proprietary blends need manufacturer definitions. Do not translate a branded material name into a universal performance promise.

### Option and inclusion tables

Some product tables combine standard features, selectable options, paid upgrades, and accessories. Separate them into four questions:

1. What is always included in the starting configuration?
2. What can be selected at no added cost?
3. What adds cost or production time?
4. What appears in photos but is not included?

The final order summary should answer all four. A product card cannot carry the complete option system, so use it to enter the product page rather than as the final purchasing record.

### Resolving conflicting values

When the same page shows two heights or weights, do not silently choose one. Record the conflict, identify whether different materials or versions are involved, and request confirmation. Update the catalog only when the corrected value can be tied to the exact product.

Conflicts are especially important when they affect safe handling, shipping, compatibility, or a paid option. A small difference in a decorative measurement may be less consequential, but it should still be corrected when reliable evidence is available.

## Size and Weight Matter More Than Buyers Expect

Height changes visual scale. Weight changes the entire ownership routine. A doll that weighs `79 lb / 36 kg` may be manageable for one buyer and impractical for another. The question is not whether the number sounds reasonable. The question is whether the buyer can lift, turn, clean, dress, and store that weight safely in the available space.

| Before checkout | What to measure or test |
| --- | --- |
| Carrying route | Doorways, stairs, corners, elevators, and distance from delivery point |
| Cleaning area | Tub or shower dimensions, lifting position, drainage, and drying space |
| Storage area | Length, width, ventilation, privacy, and pressure-free support |
| Buyer handling | Ability to lift a similar dead weight without unstable twisting |
| Clothing | Body measurements rather than height alone |

Height should always appear in `ft/in + cm`, and weight in `lb + kg`. Body measurements should show `in + cm` where available. Conversions help comparison, but they do not improve an inaccurate source value. If a supplier measurement looks inconsistent, ask for confirmation before publishing or ordering.

Compact models can reduce storage demand, but height does not guarantee low weight. Material, internal foam, skeleton, head construction, and manufacturer weight-reduction methods all matter. Use [mini sex dolls](/shop/mini-sex-dolls) for smaller builds and compare each listed weight separately.

### Build a handling plan before choosing a height

Start with the most difficult movement in the ownership routine. For one buyer that may be carrying the package upstairs. For another it may be turning the doll during cleaning or transferring it from storage to a bed. That movement should set the practical weight ceiling.

A useful home test is to handle a safely packed object with a similar weight. Keep the load close to the body, avoid twisting, and do not attempt the test alone if there is any doubt. A rigid gym weight is easier to grip than a soft articulated body, so passing the test does not mean a doll of the same weight will feel equally manageable.

Consider these details together:

- **Lift frequency:** occasional positioning is different from moving the doll several times per week.
- **Grip points:** hands, feet, fingers, wrists, ankles, and the neck are not carrying handles.
- **Transfer height:** lifting from a low case or floor position is harder than moving between surfaces at similar heights.
- **Stairs and corners:** the package may be longer and wider than expected even when its listed weight seems manageable.
- **Cleaning method:** a removable insert can reduce how often the complete body needs to be moved.
- **Mobility aids:** a stable cart, lift, storage case, or second adult may make a heavier model practical.

### Read dimensions as a set

Height is the easiest number to market and often the least useful number by itself. Compare shoulder width, bust or chest, waist, hips, limb length, foot size, and package dimensions. Two dolls at `5 ft 3 in / 160 cm` can have very different visual scale, clothing fit, storage needs, and weight.

Body measurements also help prevent misleading labels. Words such as petite, curvy, athletic, or plus-size are descriptive categories, not standardized dimensions. The measurements are the evidence.

### Plan clothing from measurements

Do not buy a wardrobe from the doll height alone. Match clothing to bust or chest, waist, hip, inseam, and foot measurements where those details are available. Stretch fabrics can tolerate some variation, while fitted garments may need tailoring. Remove dark or strongly dyed clothing after a short test period and check for transfer.

### When a listed weight is missing

Do not estimate weight from a similar body or from a competitor listing. Ask DollWow to confirm the current manufacturer value for the exact body, head, material, and selected upgrades. A silicone head, gel-filled areas, electronic head, or skeleton option can change the final weight.

## Skeletons, Joints, Standing Feet, and Positioning

The skeleton determines movement and support inside the body. Marketing may describe an "EVO," "upgraded," "shrugging," or "flexible" skeleton, but the exact joint set varies by manufacturer and body.

Important questions include:

- Can the shoulders shrug or rotate beyond a standard joint?
- Are fingers articulated, wired, or fixed?
- Can the doll stand, and does standing require bolts or a support?
- Which positions are supported without forcing a joint?
- Does an upgrade add weight or change the body feel?
- Are any poses intended only for assisted photography?

Standing feet do not make a doll independently stable in every situation. Use support, avoid falls, and follow the exact supplier guidance. Do not leave a doll standing for long periods unless the manufacturer explicitly supports that storage method.

Read the detailed [sex doll skeleton guide](/learn/sex-doll-skeleton-options) and [standing feet guide](/learn/standing-feet-sex-doll-guide) before adding an upgrade.

### What a skeleton can and cannot tell you

A skeleton diagram shows the internal joint layout, but it does not fully describe movement resistance, range, or long-term behavior. Joint tension varies by body and may change with use. Soft material can also limit a position before the metal joint reaches its theoretical range.

Useful manufacturer evidence includes a movement chart, official demonstration, body-specific option list, and care instructions. A generic pose photo is weaker evidence because the body may be supported outside the frame or prepared for a short photo session.

### Joint questions worth asking

Ask about the exact model rather than whether the brand offers a feature somewhere in its range:

1. Does the neck move independently, and is the head removable?
2. Do the shoulders shrug, rotate, or support a raised-arm position?
3. What movement is available at the spine or waist?
4. Are hips designed for a wide range, and are there stated limits?
5. Are the fingers wired, articulated, or sculpted in place?
6. Do the ankles move, and how does that interact with standing feet?
7. Does the manufacturer recommend periodic joint adjustment or support?

Never force a joint through strong resistance. Support the limb near the joint and move it slowly. If a joint produces an unusual sound, becomes unexpectedly loose, or changes the surface shape sharply, stop and ask for product-specific guidance.

### Standing systems and floor protection

Standing bolts or reinforced feet concentrate weight into a small area. Check whether hardware is exposed, concealed, or optional. Use a clean, stable, level surface and protect delicate flooring when appropriate. Standing should be supervised, with the body supported against a stable surface if the manufacturer recommends it.

Some feet prioritize appearance and others prioritize supported standing. Confirm which construction is shown in the selected product photos. Shoes may hide hardware, but they should fit without compressing toes or transferring dye.

### Posing for storage is different from posing for a photo

A pose that is acceptable for a few minutes may place pressure on soft areas when held for days. Long-term storage should distribute weight, keep joints in a neutral position, avoid sharp bends, and prevent contact that creates dents or creases. Follow the body-specific storage instructions over a general posing tutorial.

### Start with the body and head, then add options

Choose the body around handling, proportions, material, and intended use. Choose the head around sculpt, compatibility, and maintenance. Only then evaluate cosmetic, structural, and electronic upgrades. This order keeps optional features from distracting from the two components that define most of the finished product.

Review the complete configuration after each pass. If changing an early choice removes or changes a later option, ask for a revised written summary.

An eye-color swatch or skin-tone photograph can vary with lighting, camera settings, display calibration, and production batch. It is useful for comparison but not a promise of an exact screen color. Ask whether the reference is specific to the selected material because the same named tone may appear different on TPE and silicone.

Wig images show style and color, but wigs are removable styling items. Implanted hair is part of the head and requires a different maintenance decision. Confirm density, hairline, material, and whether the selected head supports implantation.

### Understand compatibility before paying

Compatibility rules may involve more than two visible options. For example, a powered head could affect implanted hair, eye movement, oral construction, neck connection, or body compatibility. The safest record is a product-specific configuration approved by the seller or manufacturer. A brand-wide menu is not proof that every combination can be built.

### Factory photos and approval

When factory photos are available, ask what they will show and when approval is requested. Check the head, body, skin tone, makeup, eyes, hair choice, and visible options against the saved order. Factory photos are a quality checkpoint, not a substitute for written specifications or final inspection after delivery.

If something appears wrong, describe the mismatch precisely and refer to the saved configuration. Do not approve because production feels urgent. At the same time, recognize that lighting and phone cameras can alter color, so ask for clarification or another view when color is the only concern.

## Heating, Electronic, AI, and Robotic Features

Advanced features deserve more scrutiny than a visual option because they affect power, weight, compatibility, care, and after-sale support. A feature name alone is not enough.

Confirm the supported function, power source, controls, operating limits, cleaning restrictions, warranty path, and added weight. Temperatures should be presented in both `F + C` when the manufacturer provides an operating range. Never infer a safe temperature or usage routine from another brand's product.

AI-related features may involve a separate head, wearable device, speaker, app, or cloud service. Ask what works without a subscription, what data leaves the device, whether the service is available in the buyer's country, and what happens if the software is discontinued.

Only consider electronic products currently offered by DollWow and supported by verified brand documentation. Interest in robotic products does not make an unverified capability real.

### Heating is not one universal feature

The word "heating" may describe an internal warming system, an external accessory, a heated insert, or a brand-specific module. Confirm which areas warm, how the system is powered, whether it is removable, the documented operating range, and whether it changes cleaning or storage.

Do not use a heating system while the product is wet or damaged. Do not improvise power supplies or exceed the documented temperature. If the manufacturer provides a limit, present it in both `F + C` and keep the original source value visible.

### Electronic head functions

Electronic heads may offer combinations of sound, jaw movement, eye movement, app control, or other functions. The feature set can differ between head models from the same manufacturer. Check:

- Exact supported head and body connections.
- Included controller, cable, battery, or power supply.
- Charging time and stated operating duration.
- Water restrictions and cleaning method.
- App name, operating-system support, and regional availability.
- Replacement, repair, and warranty process.
- Added weight and any effect on neck support.

Buyers should not assume that every product using "AI" can move, see, converse privately, or operate without another device.

## Ready-to-Ship vs Custom Order

A ready-to-ship listing usually offers a fixed or limited configuration already assigned to a warehouse. A custom order is produced or configured after purchase. They are different buying processes.

| Factor | Ready to ship | Custom order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choice | Usually fixed or limited | Usually broader, but product-specific |
| Timing | Potentially faster after stock confirmation | Includes production plus shipping |
| Photos | Existing listing or warehouse photos | Factory photos may be available before shipment |
| Risk to check | Stock accuracy and exact included configuration | Compatibility, production details, approval, and lead time |
| Best for | Buyers prioritizing timing and simplicity | Buyers prioritizing a specific build |

Never promise a delivery date that operations cannot support. Confirm current warehouse stock, dispatch expectations, destination, and any customization before checkout. ### Verify what "in stock" means

An in-stock label should correspond to a specific physical configuration and warehouse. Ask whether the listed head, body, material, skin tone, and included items match the photographed unit. Adding a major customization may convert an in-stock purchase into a factory order.

Warehouse location matters because it can affect dispatch, carrier options, transit, and import handling. It does not guarantee a delivery date. Current stock can also change quickly, so confirmation should be close to checkout.

### Decide which tradeoff matters most

Choose ready to ship when timing, a known configuration, and a simpler order are the priorities. Choose custom when a specific head-body combination or option is more important than speed. Neither route is automatically lower risk; each requires a different set of checks.

For a ready-to-ship unit, request confirmation of current condition and exact inclusions. For a custom order, focus on compatibility, production records, factory photos, and approval. In both cases, save the final order details.

Privacy includes more than the outer box. Buyers may care about billing language, email subject lines, support channels, delivery appointments, tracking, and who can see the package.

Prepare a clear floor area before the package arrives. If the package weight exceeds what one person can safely handle, arrange assistance or suitable equipment.

## Cleaning by Material and Construction

The basic routine is clean gently, rinse or wipe as instructed, dry fully, and store without pressure. The exact method depends on material, face construction, fixed or removable parts, electronics, hair, and manufacturer instructions.

Use mild products approved for the material. Avoid harsh solvents, abrasive tools, extreme heat, and unverified oils. Protect eyes, eyelashes, makeup, implanted hair, and electronic components from unnecessary water.

Internal areas require complete drying before storage. A surface that feels dry does not prove that an internal channel is dry. Removable parts may have a different care path from fixed construction.

Read the full [sex doll cleaning guide](/learn/how-to-clean-a-sex-doll). Jesse reviews this section for clear risk language, but it remains general product-care information. The exact manufacturer instruction should win when it differs.

### Identify the cleaning path first

Before choosing supplies, identify body material, head material, fixed or removable construction, hair type, painted areas, and electronics. Write these down with the manufacturer care source. A hybrid doll can require one routine for the head and another for the body.

Use a dedicated set of clean, soft tools. Keep cloths used for dark clothing, household chemicals, or other materials away from the doll. Patch-test any newly approved product in an inconspicuous area when the manufacturer recommends it.

### General external cleaning sequence

1. Remove clothing, wig, jewelry, and detachable electronic parts as instructed.
2. Support the body so no joint or soft area carries concentrated pressure.
3. Use the manufacturer-approved mild cleaner and water conditions.
4. Wipe or wash with low friction, especially over makeup, veins, body paint, and textured details.
5. Remove cleaner residue according to the material instructions.
6. Pat dry with a clean, soft, light-colored towel.
7. Allow complete air drying in a protected position before dressing or storage.

This sequence is intentionally general. Water temperature, immersion, face care, powder, and product choice must follow the exact construction.

### Internal cleaning and drying

Fixed internal areas can retain moisture beyond what is visible at the opening. Use only approved tools that do not scratch, stretch, or puncture the material. Rinse as instructed, drain thoroughly, and use the documented drying method.

Removable inserts should be taken out only as designed. Clean and dry the insert separately, then inspect both the insert and receiving area before reassembly. Do not store a damp insert inside the body.

Complete drying is more important than speed. Avoid unapproved high heat, improvised electrical devices, or sharp drying tools. If the manufacturer provides a drying accessory, use it within the documented limits.

### Face, eyes, and hair

Treat the face as a detailed finish area. Use less water and friction around eyes, eyelashes, brows, lips, and makeup. Do not soak an electronic head or expose connectors to moisture. Remove a wig when possible and care for it separately.

Implanted hair should not be pulled, scrubbed, or saturated without specific guidance. Protect the hairline while cleaning the face. If styling products are allowed, confirm that they cannot transfer to the skin material.

### TPE and silicone are families, not single recipes

General material guidance can help organize care, but individual formulations and surface finishes vary. Do not assume that a cleaner, powder, stain treatment, or adhesive approved for one brand is safe for another. Named soft materials and hybrid builds especially require current manufacturer instructions.

### A practical care log

Keep a private note with the product model, materials, approved supplies, cleaning dates, joint or surface observations, and any support advice. A short log helps identify gradual changes and prevents uncertainty about which product was previously used.

## Storage, Stains, Hair, and Surface Care

Store the doll clean, fully dry, supported, and protected from prolonged pressure. Avoid tight clothing, dark untested fabrics, sharp objects, direct sunlight, extreme temperatures, and positions that stress joints or compress soft areas.

Test clothing for dye transfer before extended wear. Light fabrics are usually easier to manage, but any dyed material can transfer. Do not assume a stain treatment that works on one formulation is safe for another.

Wigs can usually be removed for separate care. Implanted hair needs gentler handling around the scalp and may conflict with some electronic head options. Face makeup, eyelashes, and body painting should be cleaned with less friction than unpainted areas.

### Choose a storage method around weight and access

Common approaches include a manufacturer-approved case, a supported flat position, or a suspension system designed for the exact body. Each has tradeoffs.

| --- | --- |
| Case or box | Is it long and wide enough, ventilated, clean, and padded without compressing soft areas? |
| Supported flat storage | Does the surface distribute weight, protect privacy, and keep the doll away from heat, sunlight, pets, and dye? |
| Suspension | Does the manufacturer support it, is the attachment correct, and can the hardware safely carry the total weight? |

### Prevent pressure and transfer

Use light, clean, non-dyed barrier materials that the manufacturer considers safe. Keep limbs from pressing sharply into the torso and avoid long contact with hard edges. Reposition when recommended, especially with very soft or gel-filled areas.

Dark wigs, clothing, blankets, foam, printed fabrics, and some metals can transfer color or react with surfaces. Test unfamiliar materials briefly and inspect. Separate the doll from other TPE or silicone products unless compatibility is confirmed.

### Control the environment

Store in a dry, moderate environment away from direct sunlight, open flames, radiators, and extreme temperatures. If the product has electronics or batteries, follow separate charging and long-term storage instructions. Do not seal a body in storage while moisture remains.

Wash new clothing separately before use when appropriate, then test for dye transfer. Remove metal pieces or rough fasteners that press into the skin. Choose sizes from actual body measurements and avoid tight elastic that creates prolonged compression.

After removing clothing, inspect contact areas such as shoulders, waist, hips, feet, and under dark fabric. Early detection gives support a clearer problem to evaluate.

## Common Buying Mistakes and Better Decisions

### Choosing from the hero image alone

The hero image is designed to present the product attractively. It may use a particular head, wig, makeup, eye color, skin tone, clothing, and lighting setup. It rarely communicates weight, storage, cleaning, or the complete standard configuration.

**Better decision:** identify the photographed body and head, then rebuild the look through the option selector. Confirm every visual detail that matters and compare the result with the written specifications.

### Treating height as the handling measure

A shorter product can still be dense, wide, or difficult to grip. A taller lightweight body may have a lower listed weight but require more space. Height answers only one part of the handling question.

**Better decision:** compare height, weight, width, body measurements, package size, and the physical route together. Test the personal weight ceiling before browsing by appearance.

### Assuming all TPE or silicone is the same

TPE and silicone are material families. Formulation, firmness, surface finish, construction, care, and repair guidance vary. A comparison copied from one brand may not apply to another.

**Better decision:** use category-level information to create a shortlist, then verify the manufacturer and body-specific material details before ordering.

### Selecting every available upgrade

An option menu can make more features feel like more value. In reality, each upgrade may add cost, weight, maintenance, production time, or compatibility rules. Some combinations cannot be produced.

**Better decision:** add an option only when its documented benefit matters to the intended routine. Confirm the final combination and remove upgrades whose effect is unclear.

### Comparing starting prices

Starting prices can refer to different materials, heads, sizes, and included features. A heavily discounted listing may become more expensive after matching the desired configuration.

**Better decision:** compare the same brand and model with equivalent options, inclusions, shipping, and destination charges. Save each configured total with a date.

### Believing an in-stock label without checking the unit

Stock can refer to a body, a sample configuration, a regional warehouse range, or an item that changed after the page was indexed. A custom request can also remove the speed advantage.

**Better decision:** confirm that the exact selected unit is physically available, where it is located, and which components are included.

### Ignoring the carton and delivery route

The doll may fit the storage area while the shipping carton cannot turn through a stairwell or enter a managed package room. Delivery can become stressful even when product dimensions were checked.

**Better decision:** obtain package dimensions and shipping weight, then plan the complete route and adult assistance before dispatch.

### Planning to store the doll in its clothes

Dark fabric, tight elastic, metal pieces, and prolonged pressure can stain or mark soft material. A visually appealing outfit may not be suitable for long storage.

**Better decision:** store clean and dry with approved light barriers, test clothing for transfer, and remove pressure from soft areas.

### Buying generic care chemicals early

Online care advice often mixes products intended for different materials. An unverified cleaner, powder, oil, adhesive, or stain treatment can cause damage.

**Better decision:** identify the exact body and head materials, then obtain current manufacturer-approved care guidance. Buy only what belongs in that routine.

### Forcing a joint to match a pose

Catalog images do not show external supports or the movement required to reach a pose. Forcing resistance can stress the skeleton or surface.

**Better decision:** use body-specific movement guidance, support limbs near joints, move slowly, and stop at unusual resistance or deformation.

### Treating factory photos as a formality

Approving quickly can allow a visible configuration mismatch to proceed. At the same time, rejecting a build solely because of warm or cool lighting can create confusion.

**Better decision:** compare factory photos with the written order systematically. Ask for neutral clarification images when color or detail is uncertain.

### Discarding packaging immediately

Cartons, inserts, labels, and documentation may be relevant if damage or a mismatch is discovered. They also help establish how the product was protected in transit.

**Better decision:** keep packaging intact until the complete inspection and function check are finished.

### Attempting a repair before contacting support

A generic repair can use the wrong adhesive, solvent, heat, or technique. It may enlarge the issue and affect warranty review.

**Better decision:** stop, document, identify material and location, and request an approved method before changing the surface or structure.

### Expecting AI features from a product label

Terms such as smart, AI, interactive, or robot can describe very different capabilities. A conversational app does not prove physical movement, and an electronic head does not prove private offline processing.

**Better decision:** translate the desired experience into individual functions and verify each one against current documentation.

### Using a universal best-brand list

Brands can have strong and weak fits within the same catalog. A broad reputation cannot resolve the weight, material, options, or evidence for an exact body.

**Better decision:** compare models that meet the same requirement, then use brand-level support and documentation as part of the final evaluation.

## The First 30 Days of Ownership

A simple first-month plan helps establish safe handling and care before habits become difficult to change. Adjust every step to the manufacturer instructions and actual frequency of use.

### Before arrival

- Save the order, configuration, support confirmations, and product images.
- Prepare a neutral, clean inspection area.
- Clear the package route and arrange adult assistance if needed.
- Confirm the approved cleaning and drying basics for the exact materials.
- Prepare storage that supports the body without pressure.
- Keep a camera or phone available for the arrival record.

Do not pre-open strong chemicals or improvise a hanging system. The goal is to be prepared without committing to an unverified care path.

### Arrival day

Photograph the carton before moving or opening it if practical. Record visible damage and labels. Unpack slowly, preserve protective materials, and compare the contents with the saved order.

Attach the head or components only according to the instructions. Check the identity, configuration, surface, joints, standing system, accessories, and electronics. Report a concern before cleaning, repair, or extended handling.

### First cleaning and setup

Read the instructions again with the physical product present. Identify water-sensitive areas and removable components. Use the approved routine, dry fully, and note how long the complete process takes.

This first routine is a useful reality check. If lifting or turning feels unsafe, revise the setup with help or appropriate equipment rather than repeating the same movement alone.

### First week

Inspect areas that experienced pressure during shipping or storage. Check clothing contact points, hairline, makeup, fingers, toes, and standing hardware. Confirm that the chosen storage position distributes weight and is easy to access without pulling a limb.

Create a private care record containing:

- Product, body, head, and materials.
- Approved cleaner, powder, lubricant, and repair references.
- Cleaning and drying notes.
- Storage method and barrier materials.
- Electronic model, app, charger, and account details where relevant.
- Support contact and order number.

### Weeks two through four

Refine the routine around real use. Note whether the storage space ventilates, whether the body is practical to transfer, and whether clothing or wigs show signs of transfer. Inspect joints and surfaces without forcing movement.

Avoid introducing several new care products at once. When a change causes a problem, a simple routine makes the cause easier to identify.

### End-of-month review

Ask five questions:

1. Can the doll be moved and positioned safely with the current setup?
2. Is cleaning followed by complete drying every time?
3. Does storage avoid pressure, dye, moisture, and environmental extremes?
4. Are electronic and hair components being handled according to their separate instructions?
5. Are any surface, joint, or configuration concerns worth documenting for support?

Update the care record and keep product-specific guidance accessible. A stable routine should become easier, not more complicated, after the first month.

## How to Resolve Confusing or Conflicting Product Claims

New buyers often find two different weights, option names that sound similar, or a product photo that does not match the written description. That does not always mean the seller is dishonest. Manufacturers revise bodies, retailers combine data feeds, and older photography can remain in circulation. It does mean the conflict should be resolved before payment.

### Start with the exact configuration

Write down the body, head, material, skin tone, skeleton, standing system, and every paid option. A general brand page cannot confirm a detail that changes by body or head.

### Use the most specific source

Check information in this order:

1. Written details for the exact product and selected configuration.
2. Current manufacturer documentation for that body, head, material, or option.
3. Written confirmation from support that identifies the product.
4. General articles, videos, forum posts, and older reviews.

A forum post can reveal a useful question, but it should not overrule current instructions for a different formulation or body.

### Ask for a direct answer

Quote both conflicting claims and ask which one applies. For example: "The specifications show `77 lb / 35 kg`, but the option description shows `88 lb / 40 kg`. Which weight applies with this head and lightweight body option?"

Specific questions are easier to verify than "Is this listing accurate?" Save the response with the final order.

### Know which facts change most often

Price, stock, warehouse location, production time, app support, and available options can change quickly. Material, body mold, height, and basic construction are usually more stable but can still be revised. Check a current product page before ordering even when an older guide or video appears detailed.

### Treat photography as evidence with limits

Product photos can establish sculpt, proportion, and potential finish. They may not establish the standard wig, clothing, makeup, eyes, skin tone, or included accessories. Ask which options created the pictured configuration.

Generated illustrations can explain lifting, measuring, cleaning, or storage, but they should not be used to judge what an actual doll looks like. Use real product images for that decision.

### Leave unknown details unknown

If a decision-critical weight, material, option, or electronic function cannot be confirmed, remove the product from the shortlist or postpone the order. A plausible estimate is not useful when the purchase is expensive or difficult to return.

## FAQs

### What should a first-time buyer prioritize?

Prioritize manageable weight, clear measurements, straightforward material care, storage space, a simple configuration, and responsive support. A visually impressive doll that is difficult to handle is rarely a good first purchase.

### Is TPE or silicone better?

Neither is universally better. TPE is often softer and available at lower entry prices, while silicone is often chosen for fine surface detail and simpler surface cleaning. Manufacturer formulation, body construction, weight, care, and budget should decide the comparison.

### How heavy is a full-size sex doll?

Weight varies widely by height, body shape, material, internal construction, and lightweight technology. Check the exact listing in both pounds and kilograms, then plan lifting, cleaning, stairs, and storage around that number.

### Are mini dolls always lightweight?

No. Smaller height can reduce material volume, but construction and material still matter. Compare the listed weight of each mini or petite model rather than assuming it will be easy to lift.

### What makes a sex doll look realistic?

Realism comes from proportion, head sculpt, eyes, surface texture, body painting, hair, hands, feet, styling, and the match between the selected head and body. Lighting and clothing can make catalog photos look different from the standard configuration.

### Can every doll be customized?

No. Options vary by brand, body, head, material, and model. Product-specific option data should take priority over brand-wide defaults.

### How long does a custom doll take?

Timing depends on the manufacturer, configuration, production queue, factory-photo process, destination, and shipping method. Request a current estimate before checkout and treat it as an estimate unless DollWow can operationally guarantee it.

### What does discreet shipping mean?

It generally refers to plain or neutral outer packaging, but buyers should also ask about billing language, tracking, delivery communication, and package handling when those details matter.

### How often should a sex doll be cleaned?

Clean according to use, material, construction, and manufacturer guidance. Internal areas should be cleaned when needed and dried completely before storage. Avoid universal schedules that ignore the exact product.

### Can a doll remain standing for storage?

Only when the exact product and manufacturer support it, and usually with appropriate assistance or support. Standing feet do not guarantee independent stability or make prolonged standing suitable for every body.

### Are factory photos important?

They can provide a useful checkpoint for custom orders by showing the completed configuration before shipment. Confirm what the approval process covers and what happens when a visible detail does not match the order.

### How can I avoid a misleading listing?

Verify the brand, model, materials, measurements, included items, stock status, option costs, and support process. Save the final configuration and request written clarification when the page contains conflicting details.

### Does a more expensive doll always last longer?

No. Price can reflect material, size, finish, brand, options, and production complexity. Longevity also depends on formulation, construction, handling, cleaning, storage, and repair support.

### Can DollWow compare another seller's offer?

Yes. Submit the exact product and configured offer through the [price-match page](/price-match). Comparable products need the same brand, model, options, shipping context, and included items.

### What size sex doll is easiest to manage?

There is no universal easiest height. The most manageable product is one whose weight, width, grip, cleaning method, and storage path fit the buyer. Start with a personal weight ceiling, then compare compact and lightweight models using exact specifications.

### How do I convert a doll's height from centimeters to feet and inches?

Use `1 in = 2.54 cm`, then divide total inches into feet and remaining inches. DollWow presents both systems where the source measurement is available. A converted number should not imply more precision than the manufacturer supplied.

### Why can two dolls of the same height have different weights?

Body volume, material density, foam core, skeleton, head construction, breast or chest filling, and lightweight systems all affect weight. Compare the complete build rather than estimating from height.

### Is a torso doll a good first purchase?

It can be when easier storage, lower overall scale, or a specific form is the priority. Some torsos are still dense and heavy, so check listed weight, dimensions, cleaning construction, and the area needed for storage. Browse [torso dolls](/shop/torsos) as a distinct category.

### What is a hybrid sex doll?

Hybrid commonly describes a build using different materials for the head and body, such as a silicone head with a TPE body. The term can also be used differently by manufacturers. Confirm the material of each component and the care routine for both.

### Is a silicone head compatible with every TPE body?

No. Neck connection, scale, skin tone, power connections, and manufacturer compatibility can differ. Use the option list for the exact body and ask for confirmation before combining parts.

### Are standing feet necessary?

No. They are useful when supported standing is part of the intended routine, but they may introduce visible or concealed hardware and do not make every doll independently stable. Buyers planning flat or case storage may prioritize other features.

### What is an upgraded or EVO skeleton?

It is manufacturer-specific language for revised joints or movement. The name does not define one standard feature set. Check the body-specific diagram or documentation for shoulder, spine, hip, finger, and other movement.

### Can a sex doll sit in a chair for storage?

Only when the manufacturer supports the position and pressure is distributed appropriately. Long-term sitting can compress soft areas or stress joints. A pose that works for a photograph may not be suitable for storage.

### Should I choose a fixed or removable insert?

A removable insert can simplify separate cleaning and drying for some buyers. Fixed construction may be preferred for other reasons but requires a reliable internal cleaning and drying plan. Confirm the exact product construction and replacement path.

### Do sex dolls arrive assembled?

It varies. A body and head may be packed separately, and accessories or electronic components may require installation. Follow the exact unboxing and connection instructions rather than forcing a part that looks familiar.

### What comes with a sex doll?

Included items vary by manufacturer and product. A wig, clothing, stand, care kit, insert, tools, storage item, or electronic accessory shown in photography may be optional. Use the written inclusion list and final configuration.

### Can I use ordinary soap or household cleaner?

Do not assume it is compatible. Use products and conditions approved for the exact material and finish. Household solvents, abrasives, oils, dyes, and strong cleaners can damage surfaces or painted details.

### Does TPE always need powder?

Care requirements vary by formulation and finish. Follow the current manufacturer guidance for the exact body. Do not apply a generic powder because another TPE brand recommends it.

### How do I prevent clothing stains?

Wash and test clothing when appropriate, favor light and low-transfer fabrics, avoid prolonged tight contact, and inspect contact areas. A stain treatment must be approved for the exact material before use.

### Can TPE and silicone dolls touch in storage?

Keep products and loose material parts separated unless compatibility is confirmed. Formulations can react or transfer oils and color. Use approved light-colored barriers and manufacturer storage guidance.

### How should a doll with implanted hair be cleaned?

Protect the hairline, avoid pulling and unnecessary saturation, and follow the head-specific instructions. Implanted hair may also affect compatibility with electronic head features. A removable wig usually has a separate care routine.

### Are sex doll apps private?

Privacy depends on the specific app, device, account, and service. Review what data is collected, where processing occurs, which permissions are requested, whether recordings are stored, and what works offline. Do not infer privacy from the product category.

### Is an AI sex doll the same as a sex robot?

Not necessarily. An AI doll may combine a conventional body with conversational software or an electronic head. A sex robot may imply physical movement, but the term is used loosely. Compare documented functions rather than labels.

### Does heating warm the whole doll?

Not always. Systems may warm selected internal or external areas, use a separate accessory, or depend on a specific body. Confirm coverage, power, limits, and cleaning restrictions for the exact option.

### What should I inspect in factory photos?

Check the selected head and body, skin tone, makeup, eyes, hair, standing construction, and visible options against the saved order. Ask for clarification when lighting affects color. Factory photos do not replace checking written specifications.

### What should I do if the delivered skin tone looks different?

Photograph it in neutral lighting and compare it with the order and factory photos. Screen color, camera processing, lighting, material, and production variation can affect appearance. Contact support before using an unapproved surface treatment.

### Can a small tear be repaired at home?

Some minor damage can be repaired with manufacturer-approved materials and instructions, but the correct method depends on formulation and location. Document the issue and ask support before applying adhesive, heat, or solvent.

### How should I compare prices between stores?

Match the exact brand, model, head, body, material, options, inclusions, shipping origin, and delivered charges. Starting prices and discount percentages are not comparable when the configurations differ.

### Are ready-to-ship dolls used or returned products?

Ready to ship describes availability, not condition. Ask whether the exact unit is new, which configuration it has, where it is stored, and what inspection applies. Do not infer condition from the stock label alone.

### Can I customize an in-stock doll?

Only within what is available for that physical unit. Changing the head, wig, eyes, or removable accessories may be possible, while changing body material, skeleton, or molded construction generally requires a different unit or factory order. Confirm before checkout.

### How often should I review care instructions?

Review them when the product arrives, before using a new care product, after adding an electronic or replacement component, and whenever the manufacturer updates documentation. Keep the exact model and material in a private care record.

### What records should I keep after purchase?

Keep the order summary, selected options, product screenshots, support confirmations, factory photos, shipping record, arrival photos, and manufacturer care instructions. These records help with future maintenance and support.

The detailed profiles focus on brands whose current products you can inspect and compare through DollWow. Other manufacturers exist, but naming a brand without current products, specifications, and a support path would not help you complete a purchase.

Product cards use current catalog information when the page loads. Price, availability, and specifications can still change, so open the product page and confirm the final configuration before purchase.
