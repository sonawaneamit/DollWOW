# Clothing Dye Transfer: A Material-Specific Prevention Checklist

Preventing dye transfer starts with what touches the doll, including the inside of an outfit, wig cap, bedding and storage lining. Choose light-coloured, smooth fabrics and follow the guidance for the exact material and finish. "Silicone" does not mean stain-proof, and washed clothing is not automatically risk-free.

The aim is a manageable clothing routine, not giving up the looks you enjoy. Separate short-term styling from what stays in contact during storage.

## Begin with the material you own

For its TPE dolls, Tantaly warns about dye from dark clothing and recommends avoiding it where possible. Where dark clothing is used, it advises repeated washing first. Its silicone guidance also includes clothing precautions and inspection. Those are manufacturer instructions, not a guarantee that any fabric becomes safe after washing. [Tantaly's official care guide](https://www.tantaly.com/pages/cleaning-and-maintenance-guide-for-sex-doll)

A hybrid needs two care references. A silicone head on a TPE body does not make the entire doll silicone, and guidance for the body should not automatically be used around the face, eyebrows or hair.

| Construction | Clothing decision to make |
| --- | --- |
| TPE body | Use its manufacturer's dye-transfer and storage guidance |
| Silicone body | Check both material and painted-finish restrictions |
| Hybrid build | Identify what touches the head and body separately |
| Detailed painted finish | Protect against friction as well as colour transfer |

Keep cleaning and chemical treatment separate from clothing selection. For the broader routine, see [how to clean a doll](/learn/how-to-clean-a-sex-doll).

## Inspect the inside, not only the colour outside

Turn an outfit inside out before dressing. Look at the lining, waistband, printed labels, seams and fastenings. A pale outer layer can hide a dark lining or coloured elastic that rests directly against the surface.

For each garment, ask three questions: what will touch the doll, where will it press, and how easily can it be removed? If you cannot answer one, choose something simpler until you have suitable guidance.

A soft, light outfit with generous openings is often a more practical first choice than a fitted garment that requires pulling over hands or painted areas. Use the clothing measurements in the [size and weight guide](/learn/sex-doll-size-weight-guide) and the garment's own chart rather than squeezing into a familiar label size.

## Treat washing as preparation, not certification

Wash new garments according to their own care labels before considering them for use. If they visibly release dye, do not put them on the doll. An absence of visible colour in the wash does not establish long-contact compatibility with the doll's surface.

Do not use the doll as a fabric-testing surface. A hidden-area experiment can still leave a permanent mark, and an unmarked short exposure does not prove long-term safety. Ask about the particular fabric and manufacturer guidance when the outfit matters to you.

There is no universal safe number of hours for every material, finish and garment. Follow the exact instructions available rather than borrowing a contact-time limit from another brand.

## Protect the finish while dressing

Irontech advises smooth fabrics and gentle clothing changes to reduce friction-related fading of body painting. That supports a different but related precaution: an outfit can affect the finish even without obvious dye transfer. [Irontech's finish-care guidance](https://www.irontechdoll.com/help-faq/)

Open fastenings fully, avoid dragging seams across painted areas and keep hard hardware away from the surface. Do not use tight clothing as a way to force the body into a different shape. Stop if dressing requires stressing a joint or pulling on delicate fingers.

A barrier is not automatically safe because it is called protective. Ask which lining or barrier material the manufacturer permits. Do not improvise with adhesive films or rubber-like sheets.

## Make storage a separate decision

After styling, inspect the areas that were in contact with fabric, including the neck, waist and wrists. For storage, follow the approved supported position and avoid leaving an uncertain garment or wig cap against the surface.

Apply the same check to bedding, cushion covers and case lining. Keep the contact surface clean and dry. The [storage guide](/learn/sex-doll-storage) covers positioning and space planning beyond clothing.

## If you notice a mark

Remove the suspected source gently and photograph the affected area before applying anything. Record the material, garment and approximate contact period. Avoid scrubbing, heat or a stain-removal recipe intended for a different formulation.

Use [Care for Life](/care-for-life) to ask for model-specific guidance. A clear photo and an accurate description are more useful than trying several treatments and losing track of what changed. Prevention remains the simpler routine: suitable fabric, gentle dressing and a separate storage plan.
