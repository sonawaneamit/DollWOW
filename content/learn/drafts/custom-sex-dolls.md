# How To Customize A Sex Doll: Complete Option Guide

By Alex, Doll Collector and DollWow Product Educator with 20+ Years of Experience

## Quick Answer

To customize a sex doll, start with the correct body, compatible head, material, height, weight, and measurements. Then choose only the appearance, skeleton, feet, hair, and electronic options supported for that exact model. Compare the final configured price, resolve conflicts before production, save the approved specification, and review eligible factory media before shipment. Custom does not mean every feature can be combined.

## Key Takeaways

- The base body and head matter more than optional styling.
- Brand-wide option menus are starting points, not proof of SKU compatibility.
- TPE, silicone, and hybrid choices affect appearance, care, price, and option paths.
- Hair, electronics, skeleton, feet, and internal functions can conflict.
- Starting price and final configured price are different numbers.
- Eligible DollWow custom orders receive Human Build Check before production.
- Eligible factory media helps compare the finished build with the confirmed order before shipment.
- The Doll Passport is intended to preserve build, approval, care, and support records.

## Start With The Base Model

The best custom build begins with a body you can comfortably own. Appearance options cannot fix a body that is too heavy, too large for the delivery route, difficult to store, or built from the wrong material for your care routine.

Confirm first:

- Brand and body model
- Full doll, torso, hips, head, or another product form
- Height in feet and inches and centimeters
- Listed dry weight in pounds and kilograms
- Bust, waist, hips, shoulders, and other useful measurements
- TPE, full silicone, or hybrid construction
- Compatible head system
- Ready-to-ship or made-to-order status
- Delivery, lifting, cleaning, and storage plan

Use [custom dolls](/shop/custom) to compare current base models. Narrow by practical fit before choosing color, hair, or functions.

## Choose Body And Head Together

The body sets physical ownership requirements. The head sets much of the facial appearance and can also determine hair, jaw, oral, electronic, and connector options.

Before selecting a head, ask:

- Is this head officially compatible with the body and neck connection?
- Does head material match the body, or is the build intentionally hybrid?
- Will the skin-tone match be exact, close, or subject to normal production variation?
- Are the photographed eyes, wig, makeup, and head features part of the selected option?
- Does the head support implanted hair or electronic functions?
- Does choosing one head remove another desired feature?

Do not assume every head shown by a brand fits every body. A compelling face is only useful when the complete pairing is supported.

## Material Choices

### TPE Body

TPE can offer a soft feel and broad model selection, but it has its own cleaning, drying, powdering where instructed, dye-transfer, and storage requirements. Confirm whether the selected head is TPE or silicone and how the hybrid connection is presented.

### Full Silicone

Full-silicone construction can support detailed sculpting and finish options, but firmness, weight, price, and care still vary by body. Do not assume every silicone body accepts every electronic or skeleton upgrade.

### Hybrid Construction

A hybrid build commonly combines different head and body materials. Confirm the exact combination, tone match, connection, care routine, and how the final specification is described.

Read [TPE versus silicone](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) before committing to appearance options. Material affects the whole ownership routine.

## Appearance Options

Appearance options are easier to judge when the base model is settled.

### Skin Tone

Use brand-specific references for the selected material. Screens, studio lighting, camera settings, and production variation can affect color. Ask which swatch or sample belongs to the body you are ordering.

### Eyes

Confirm the color, eye system, included set, and whether the product supports movement or replacement. Do not infer function from close-up photography.

### Wig Or Implanted Hair

A wig offers easier changes and replacement. Implanted hair can create a more integrated look but is head-specific and needs gentler care. Some electronic systems can conflict with implantation. Read the [implanted hair versus wig guide](/learn/implanted-hair-vs-wig).

### Makeup And Body Finish

Faceup, lip color, brows, body painting, nails, freckles, veins, tattoos, and other finish options vary by manufacturer. Request the closest current reference and ask whether it represents the exact material and production option.

## Skeleton, Hands, And Standing Feet

Internal options should be chosen from the movement you will actually use.

Ask which joints, shoulders, waist, hands, fingers, ankles, or toes differ from the standard body. EVO, EXP, flexible, advanced, and similar terms are supplier labels rather than universal specifications.

Standing feet must be confirmed with the body, skeleton, ankle, weight, floor, footwear, and external support plan. They do not automatically permit unsupported or long-term standing.

Use the [skeleton options guide](/learn/sex-doll-skeleton-options) and [standing feet guide](/learn/standing-feet-sex-doll-guide) before adding internal or foot upgrades.

## Electronic And Internal Functions

Manufacturers may offer heating, sound, movement, suction, electronic heads, or other powered features on selected products. Each function needs its own evidence.

Confirm:

- Exact body or head compatibility
- What the feature physically does
- Warming or movement zones where relevant
- Power supply, controller, plug, and regional suitability
- Included components
- Operating and shutoff instructions
- Cleaning and moisture limits
- Storage requirements
- Conflicts with hair, skeleton, body, or another function
- Repair and replacement path

Do not treat “AI,” “robotic,” “smart,” or “heated” as complete specifications. Read the [body heating guide](/learn/body-heating-sex-doll-guide) or [AI doll guide](/learn/ai-sex-dolls) when those features matter.

## Compatibility: Why Options Conflict

Custom options can share the same physical space, production step, power path, or structural requirement.

Examples include:

- Implanted hair and selected electronic head systems
- Head connectors and body neck systems
- Standing feet and skeleton or ankle construction
- Internal heating and another powered feature
- Weight-reduction construction and an internal option
- A finish or color that is unavailable on one material
- A ready-to-ship unit that cannot be rebuilt without becoming a factory order

The correct response is not to hide the conflict. It is to identify the non-negotiable feature, explain the tradeoff, and offer the closest supported path.

## Included, Standard, Optional, And Unsupported

Every option should fall into one of four clear states:

1. **Included:** Part of the selected base configuration.
2. **Selectable at no extra charge:** A supported choice within the current base price.
3. **Paid upgrade:** A supported option that changes the configured total.
4. **Unavailable or pending confirmation:** Not orderable on the current model, or not yet confirmed.

Product photography does not decide the state. A promotional image may show a paid upgrade, styling prop, accessory, or another configuration.

Ask for a written final specification showing what will be built and what will travel with the order.

## Starting Price Versus Final Configured Price

A custom product page often shows a base price. The final total can change with:

- Material or body upgrade
- Premium head
- Skeleton, hand, or foot system
- Implanted hair
- Makeup or finish option
- Heating or electronics
- Accessories
- Delivery path, tax, or duties where applicable

Compare equivalent final builds. A low base price is not cheaper if the desired configuration requires several paid upgrades.

## Ready-To-Ship Versus Custom

A ready-to-ship doll is an existing configuration. It can offer a shorter fulfillment path but usually fewer changes. A made-to-order doll offers more product-specific choices but adds production, compatibility review, and approval steps.

Do not convert an urgent purchase into a complex build without accepting the different process. Read [ready-to-ship versus made-to-order dolls](/learn/ready-to-ship-vs-custom-sex-dolls) before deciding.

## How To Use Reference Images

Reference images should help identify a specific option, not promise an identical photograph.

Check:

- Same brand and option
- Same head or body where relevant
- Same material
- Same skin tone or finish reference
- Whether the image shows factory evidence, catalog photography, or an illustration
- Whether styling items are included

AI-generated illustrations can explain an option, but they should not be treated as actual product photography or proof of the final result.

## Common Mistakes

### Choosing Appearance Before Practical Fit

Body weight, measurements, material, delivery, and storage come first.

### Assuming Every Option Combines

Hair, electronics, skeleton, feet, and internal systems can conflict.

### Comparing Base Price With Final Price

Compare equivalent configured builds and delivered value.

### Treating Photos As The Included Specification

Promotional images may show upgrades or styling not included in the order.

### Changing The Build After Production Starts

Resolve non-negotiable details during Human Build Check. Later changes may be unavailable.

### Expecting Factory Media To Prove Internal Features

Photos and video can confirm visible details but not every internal component or performance claim.

## FAQs

### What Can I Customize On A Sex Doll?

Depending on the exact product, choices can include body, compatible head, material, skin tone, eyes, hair, makeup, body finish, skeleton, feet, heating, electronics, and accessories.

### Can I Customize Every Sex Doll?

No. Options depend on the exact brand, body, head, material, production method, and current manufacturer rules. Ready-to-ship units are usually more fixed.

### How Much Does A Custom Sex Doll Cost?

The final price depends on the base model and paid options. Compare the configured total, delivery, taxes or duties where applicable, and ownership needs rather than relying on a starting price.

### How Long Does A Custom Sex Doll Take?

Timing varies by manufacturer, body, options, production queue, review, and shipping route. Use the order-specific information confirmed for the final build.

### Can I Choose Any Head For Any Body?

No. Head and body compatibility depends on the brand, neck connection, material, tone, and supported pairing.

### Can I Combine Implanted Hair With An Electronic Head?

Sometimes, but not always. Scalp construction, wiring, and mechanisms can conflict. Confirm the exact head and option combination before production.

### Are Standing Feet Included?

Do not assume they are. Feet can be standard, a paid upgrade, or unavailable depending on the body and skeleton.

### Can I Add Heating To Any Custom Doll?

No. Heating depends on the exact body, material, internal construction, power system, and compatibility with other options.

### Will My Custom Doll Look Exactly Like The Product Photo?

Product images are references. The final result should be compared with the confirmed specification, while normal production, lighting, styling, and material differences can affect appearance.

### Can I Change Custom Options After Ordering?

Contact DollWow immediately. Changes may be possible before production locks, but can become limited or unavailable once manufacturing begins.

### Do Custom Dolls Receive Factory Photos?

Eligible custom builds can receive factory photos or video before shipment where supported. The exact process depends on the product and order.

### Can DollWow Help Build A Configuration?

Yes. Send the product, practical requirements, and non-negotiable features through live chat or email. DollWow can review current choices, price changes, conflicts, and manufacturer-confirmed options before production.
