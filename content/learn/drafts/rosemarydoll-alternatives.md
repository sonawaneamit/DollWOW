## Compare TPE, Silicone, and Hybrid Correctly

Material labels frequently create false comparisons.

### Full TPE

Both the head and body use TPE unless the listing says otherwise. TPE often offers a softer feel and lower starting-price path, but formulation, firmness, weight, finish, staining, and care vary by manufacturer and model.

### Full silicone

Both the head and body use silicone. Full silicone often sits at a higher starting price and may support fine sculpt detail, but firmness, weight, finish, and care still vary.

### Hybrid construction

A common hybrid uses a silicone head with a TPE body. It should not be compared with full silicone as if the construction were identical. Confirm the neck connection, tone match, head options, and separate care needs.

Use the [TPE versus silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls), then compare current [TPE](/shop/tpe), [silicone](/shop/silicone), and [hybrid](/shop/hybrid) listings.

## Compare Ready-to-Ship Inventory

Ready-to-ship means an existing or allocated configuration is associated with inventory. It does not create a guaranteed arrival date.

Confirm:

- the exact unit is currently available
- its warehouse region
- the body and head shown for that unit
- material, dimensions, and listed weight
- included configuration
- whether any change would require a made-to-order replacement
- current dispatch and delivery expectations

Inventory can change between page view and checkout. Ask for confirmation when timing affects privacy, travel, or receipt.

## Compare Product Images Carefully

Supplier imagery can appear across several authorized sellers. That is normal in a multi-brand category, but it makes image interpretation important.

Ask whether an image shows:

- the exact ready-stock unit
- the selected body with a different head
- a manufacturer reference configuration
- optional styling or clothing
- a clearly labeled promotional image based on the real product
- factory approval media for a completed custom build

Do not assume clothing, wigs, props, accessories, or background items are included. Keep enough unedited gallery imagery to inspect the actual product.

## Compare Measurements and Handling

Height describes scale, but listed weight determines much of the ownership routine. A taller doll can sometimes weigh less than a shorter model because of body volume, material, skeleton, or weight-reduction construction.

Compare:

- height in ft/in and cm
- listed weight in lb and kg
- shoulder, bust, waist, and hips where available
- storage dimensions
- route from delivery point to storage
- cleaning and drying setup
- how often the doll will be moved

## Common Comparison Mistakes

### Comparing store reputation instead of the order

Reputation matters, but the buyer still needs an accurate product, configuration, price, and delivery path.

### Treating similar supplier photos as identical products

Head, body, material, tone, skeleton, and included options can differ even when the main image looks similar.

### Comparing full silicone with a silicone-head hybrid

The constructions, care, feel, and price are different.

### Assuming every custom option is available

Options depend on the exact product and manufacturer rules.

### Treating stock as permanent

An existing unit can sell before the comparison is complete.

### Ignoring support after arrival

Cleaning, storage, minor damage, and repair questions often appear after the purchase, when support continuity matters most.
