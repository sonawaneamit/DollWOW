# Starter, Enthusiast or Collector? Nyla's Three Listed Builds

Preset names are shortcuts, not quality grades. The useful question is what changes between them, whether you want those changes and how much they add to the same underlying doll.

Dolls Castle Nyla provides a concrete example. Its [DollWOW product page](/products/dolls-castle-nyla-183cm-e-cup-js061-hybrid-companion-doll) displays three listed preset configurations for the same 183 cm E-cup TPE body with JS061 silicone head. The displayed selections and prices below were checked on 16 October 2026; this is a read-only pricing example, not a cart or checkout test.

## Keep the base doll constant

All three presets start from Nyla's identified body and head pairing. They do not represent three different heights or a change from hybrid to full-silicone construction.

The [manufacturer's Nyla page](https://dolls-castle.com/product/curvy-slim-tpe-sex-doll-183cm-nyla/) identifies the TPE body and JS061 pairing. For the customer purchase, use DollWOW's actual supported selections and totals rather than importing a price or function from the factory's separate store.

That distinction is especially useful when a promotional gallery and an option menu show more possibilities than any single preset contains.

## The three displayed totals

These are USD product totals before shipping, tax and checkout discounts. They are not a promise of the final amount charged in every destination or currency.

| Preset | Displayed total | Main configuration difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $1,999 | Regular TPE, detailed body painting; articulated fingers and EVO skeleton included |
| Enthusiast | $2,286 | Adds S-TPE, wired toes and movable jaw |
| Collector | $2,455 | Retains Enthusiast selections and adds body heating |

The difference from Starter to Enthusiast is $287. Collector adds $169 beyond Enthusiast, or $456 beyond Starter. Those are calculations from the displayed totals, not separate bundle discounts.

## What the first step actually buys

Starter is not an unfinished doll. In this example, the listed articulated fingers and EVO skeleton are already included. You should not assign them extra charges merely because another product treats similar-sounding features as upgrades.

Enthusiast changes the selected body material to S-TPE and adds wired toes and a movable jaw. The choice is worthwhile only if those particular changes matter to you. A softer-sounding material name is not a substitute for understanding the factory's description and care requirements.

Ask about the specific toe and jaw movement you expect rather than interpreting an upgrade label as an unlimited range of motion. Never force a joint to discover its limits.

## What the second step adds

Collector adds body heating while retaining the Enthusiast selections. It does not automatically mean every function in the full product menu has been selected.

Before choosing heating, confirm the operating instructions, supplied power equipment, regional suitability and care restrictions for the build. A convenience feature adds an operating routine as well as a price difference.

The highest preset is therefore not automatically the most practical choice. If you do not want heating, Enthusiast may already contain the changes you value. If neither set of additions matters, Starter remains a distinct, supported starting point.

## Fill a preference-to-price record

Suppose an illustrative buyer wants the listed S-TPE, wired toes and movable jaw, but does not want heating. Those preferences are hypothetical; they are not an account of a customer purchase.

| Decision field | Filled reading of Nyla's listed presets |
| --- | --- |
| Required selections | S-TPE, wired toes, movable jaw |
| Unwanted selection | Body heating |
| First listed tier containing the requirements | Enthusiast, displayed at $2,286 |
| Difference from Starter | $287 for the listed set of changes, not a price assigned to any single one |
| Difference from Collector | $169 lower, without Collector's heating addition |
| Still to establish for a purchase | Final selected summary, compatibility, delivery and tax |

This record identifies the closest displayed starting point without placing an order. If the buyer changes a requirement, make a new record rather than assuming that every edited version keeps the same tier total.

## Compare the summary after editing

Presets can help you begin, but your final selections are what count. Review the configuration again if you change a material, function or appearance field after selecting a tier.

Do not assume an independently added option is included in the displayed preset price. Likewise, removing a choice can change what the resulting build should cost. Save the final summary you intend to purchase rather than a screenshot of the preset buttons alone.

Some choices may depend on others. If the interface or support identifies a compatibility limit, resolve it before treating the new total as a confirmed build. A manual-only request needs confirmation, not an invented preset price.

## Check the purchase total separately

Before payment, compare the configured product subtotal with the cart or order summary. Review any discount, shipping charge and applicable tax separately. This article reports listed configurations; it does not describe a newly completed checkout or an order placed for these examples.
