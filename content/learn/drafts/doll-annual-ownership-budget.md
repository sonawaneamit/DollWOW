# Doll Ownership After Year One: A Practical Budget Worksheet

There is no useful universal annual maintenance bill. Your material, existing supplies, care routine, styling choices and need for replacement parts determine what you spend. A practical budget separates regular purchases from optional changes and money reserved for an unexpected problem.

The worksheet below uses hypothetical ownership patterns, not customer spending data or a priced service package. It does not provide a measured annual average or a completed priced budget. Fill it with current public prices or written quotes for compatible items. The [purchase-cost guide](/learn/sex-doll-cost) covers the original doll purchase; this guide starts after it.

## Begin with what you already have

List usable care supplies, cloths, brushes, storage equipment and wigs. Note condition and quantity before deciding what needs replacing. A durable item bought in the first year should not automatically appear as a new annual purchase.

Keep a separate column for items that are nearly empty, damaged or no longer suitable. That turns a vague shopping list into a realistic refill plan. Check compatibility before replacing a care product with a cheaper alternative.

Do not assign a fixed replacement date to the doll, wig or internal parts without evidence. A budget reserve is a way to prepare for uncertainty, not a prediction that something will fail.

## Use four budget categories

| Category | Include | How to estimate it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recurring care | Approved cleaning and surface-care supplies; cloths as needed | Packs you expect to buy, less usable stock |
| Optional presentation | Wigs, clothing and styling accessories | A deliberate spending allowance |
| Storage and equipment | Replacement covers or a supported storage change | Only purchases actually planned |
| Repair reserve | Compatible parts, specialist work and possible transport | A separate amount you choose to set aside |

Do not roll the original doll price, a still-usable first-year storage case or a full bottle you already own into recurring spending again. Conversely, a new storage setup belongs in the year you actually plan to buy it; it is not an automatic annual charge.

Keep shipping, tax and duties in the item total where applicable. Several small orders can cost more to deliver than one planned replenishment, but do not buy large quantities of an unfamiliar product just to reduce freight.

## Work through two hypothetical patterns

For a light-handling year, imagine that you keep the same hairstyle and clothing, retain your existing storage setup, and only replenish compatible supplies as they run low. The budget contains recurring care and your chosen reserve, with no automatic allowance for a new wig or storage case.

For a more frequent dressing-and-photography year, imagine the same doll and storage, but plan one optional replacement wig and a clothing allowance. Those additions are presentation choices, not proof that regular handling requires annual wig replacement.

Use the same current supply prices in both scenarios. Change the quantities and optional purchases openly. This shows what drives your own total without presenting invented "low-use" and "medium-use" industry averages.

For each consumable, multiply the number of packs you expect to purchase by the delivered pack price. Add planned one-off purchases. Keep the reserve separate so you can see the difference between expected spending and cash you are simply holding back.

Use a separate line for each planned item:

| Planning field | Your entry |
| --- | --- |
| Item and compatibility | Exact approved supply or supported replacement, not just a category name |
| Usable stock on hand | Quantity and unit: packs, bottles or individual items |
| Additional quantity planned | Purchases needed after allowing for that stock |
| Delivered unit price | Your dated quote, currency and included charges |
| Expected spending | Additional quantity multiplied by delivered unit price |
| Reserve contribution | Money set aside, shown separately from purchases |

For a hypothetical quantity-only example, suppose your plan calls for three packs over the coming year and you already have one usable pack. Enter two additional packs, not three, then apply your own current quote. This is planning arithmetic, not a measured consumption rate. A reserve you do not spend remains savings; do not report it as a repair expense.

## Make a quote record you can update

For every priced item, record the exact product, quantity or pack size, source link, currency and date. A larger bottle and a smaller bottle are not a like-for-like comparison. Neither are a compatible replacement part and a generic item with no confirmed fit.

If a part needs supplier confirmation, write "quote pending" rather than entering zero. If a repair needs photos before it can be assessed, leave it outside the priced total and explain that uncertainty in your own notes.

Once you have a complete expected annual total, dividing by twelve gives a monthly savings target. It is a planning convenience, not a monthly subscription or a promise about when expenses will occur.

## Understand what changes after day 365

DollWOW's [Care for Life terms](/care-for-life), checked on 16 October 2026, distinguish the first-year eligible accidental-damage rescue from ongoing Repair Concierge. After day 365, repair guidance and compatible-parts help continue. Free basic repair kits remain available for life, while kit shipping, taxes and duties are the customer's responsibility.

That does not mean every future part, repair service or shipment is free. Ask about the actual issue before buying a repair product or budgeting around a presumed replacement entitlement.

## Review the plan instead of overbuying

After a few months, compare purchases with your assumptions. Reduce an allowance you are not using and adjust refills from your actual experience. Keep optional styling separate so it does not make routine care look more expensive than it is.

The most useful ownership budget is modest, specific and easy to revise. Start with the material and model you own, obtain current prices for compatible items, and ask [support](/support) before an uncertain repair purchase becomes an avoidable expense.
