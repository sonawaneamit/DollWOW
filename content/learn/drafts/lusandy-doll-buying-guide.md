# Lusandy Buying Guide: Body Sizes, Options and Stock Checks

Choose a Lusandy doll by its complete body specification, not the face name or the phrase "super light." Confirm the body height, proportions, listed weight and head pairing first. Then establish which appearance choices and construction options belong to the exact product you would order.

Keep separate records for the two ordering routes: ship from warehouse (a finished, ready-to-ship configuration) or custom-made (produced for you and shipped from the factory). A factory catalog entry does not establish that either route is currently available through DollWOW.

## Understand the factory's body families

The [official Lusandy site](https://lusandydoll.com/) groups its bodies into 157, 159, 161, 162, 165, 167 and 170 cm height categories as checked on 16 October 2026. It also has a separate heads section. These are useful navigation categories, not proof that every head fits every body or that each height describes only one sculpt.

Use the [factory body collection](https://lusandydoll.com/collections/body-types) to understand the range, then read the specific product's measurements. Height alone cannot tell you hip width, storage depth, clothing fit or the weight you will handle.

For each candidate, keep the body identifier and the selected head together. This prevents a photograph of one combination from quietly becoming the reference for a different build.

## Read a real specification, not a range-wide weight claim

Lusandy's [Nadia 159 factory page](https://lusandydoll.com/products/lusandy-nadia-thicc-silicone-doll-159cm) lists a silicone body with 159 cm height, 96 cm bust, 63 cm waist and 109 cm hips. It labels the body weight under its super-weight-reduction specification as 35 kg. Those are manufacturer-published figures, not DollWOW measurements.

The table calls this a body weight, so confirm what the assembled order weighs with its selected head. Do not substitute a homepage weight-saving headline for this model's actual figure.

For storage, compare the fullest dimensions as well as length. For dressing, a 109 cm hip circumference raises different practical questions from a narrow-body photograph, even when two listings share the same height. Garment openings and fabric stretch matter alongside the label size.

## Separate material from optional construction

Lusandy's [factory FAQ](https://lusandydoll.com/pages/lusandy-faq) describes its dolls as platinum silicone. Treat that as the manufacturer's material description, not a laboratory certification or a promise of a particular lifespan.

Ask which features are included in the chosen build and which are optional. For anything described as lightweight, soft, reinforced or upgraded, request the model-specific explanation and the resulting weight where relevant.

Record appearance choices separately: head, skin tone, hair, eye colour and makeup reference. A factory's feature gallery can introduce a concept without establishing that it is standard on every product. The final configuration should explain what your selected product actually contains.

## Warehouse stock needs an exact-unit check

Open the [Lusandy ready-to-ship filter](/shop/ready-to-ship?brand=lusandy) to see the listings returned now. Use the warehouse region on each listing rather than assuming every stock offer ships from the US. Ask for the body, head, skin, hair, installed features and dispatch location of the exact unit before payment. The filter is a way to check availability, not a promise that a particular configuration remains in stock.

A factory page or another retailer's warehouse banner does not establish DollWOW availability. It also does not tell you whether the photographed choices can still be changed. Stock products should be compared as existing builds, while custom products need a separate production estimate.

If you want one specific look, decide whether a confirmed stock build is close enough or whether a custom order is worth the wait. Our [ready-to-ship versus custom guide](/learn/ready-to-ship-vs-custom-sex-dolls) explains that tradeoff.

## Build a practical shortlist

Use these four questions in order:

1. Does the body fit my storage and care space in its recommended supported position?
2. Is the confirmed assembled weight manageable with my planned assistance and equipment?
3. Are the head, finish and desired options supported together?
4. Is the full offer clear about configuration, dispatch or production, delivery and support?

Write a short answer beside each product link. If a candidate fails the first two questions, a more appealing face or extra feature will not solve the ownership problem.

Keep a separate budget for any storage support and approved care supplies you still need. Compare totals for the same destination and currency, rather than mixing a factory direct price with another seller's configured offer.

## Confirm the order you will actually receive

Before payment, review the written configuration against the photographs you have saved. Clarify anything that affects everyday ownership, especially missing weight scope or uncertainty about an existing warehouse unit.
