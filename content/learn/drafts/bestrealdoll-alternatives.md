## Start With Your Real Constraints

Before opening dozens of product pages, define the limits that can eliminate an otherwise attractive doll.

### Handling

Choose a maximum weight you can lift, clean, position, and store without strain. Check both pounds and kilograms. A doll’s flexible limbs and center of mass can make it harder to move than a rigid object of the same weight.

### Space

Measure the route from delivery to storage. Include doors, corners, stairs, elevators, cleaning space, and the storage opening. Height alone does not describe shoulder width, hip width, or required depth.

### Material

Decide whether you prefer to begin with TPE, full silicone, or a hybrid. Then compare the exact body rather than assuming every product made from that material feels, weighs, or wears the same.

### Timing

Decide whether a confirmed existing configuration is acceptable or whether specific custom choices matter more than speed.

### Budget

Set a range for the configured and delivered order, not only the starting product price. Leave room for handling, care, storage, and any options you genuinely value.

## Compare the Exact Product

When comparing stores, identify the manufacturer, body, head, material, height, measurements, product form, and selected options. Similar titles or shared photographs do not prove the orders are identical.

Check:

1. Is it the same brand and body?
2. Is the same head included or optional?
3. Is it full TPE, full silicone, or hybrid?
4. Do the height, measurements, and weight match?
5. Is it a full doll, torso, hips product, head, or another format?
6. Are skeleton, standing, hair, eyes, makeup, heating, and electronic choices equivalent?
7. Is one a confirmed warehouse unit and the other made to order?

## How to Judge Realism

Realism is not one material, one face, or one heavily edited photograph. Compare the full system:

- body proportions and head scale
- face from several angles
- eyes and expression
- skin finish and transitions
- hands and feet
- hair, brows, and facial hair
- visible seams or connections
- posing and joint behavior
- exact selected head, body, tone, and styling

Full silicone can support crisp sculpt detail and controlled finish, while TPE can provide a softer feel at a different price point. Hybrid builds can combine priorities. None of those labels guarantees that a particular doll matches your idea of realistic.

## Ready-to-Ship Versus Custom

Ready-to-ship is often best for a buyer who accepts an existing fixed configuration and values a shorter route. Custom is often best for a buyer who needs specific supported choices and accepts production time.

For a ready unit, confirm:

- exact product and configuration
- current warehouse stock
- whether images show the unit or a manufacturer reference
- what can and cannot be changed
- expected dispatch after confirmation
- delivery access and signature needs

For a custom build, confirm:

- body and head compatibility
- material and tone
- product-specific options
- option conflicts
- configured price
- production timing
- factory media where supported
- when the order becomes final

## Use Reviews Without Letting Them Choose for You

Reviews can reveal communication quality, packaging, delivery experience, care demands, and long-term annoyances that a product description may not show. They are most useful when they identify the exact product and configuration.

Be cautious when a review:

- promises timing without separating production and transit
- shows images that cannot be connected to the order

## Common Comparison Mistakes

### Trusting a best list without checking fit

A highly ranked doll can still be too heavy, too large, difficult to store, or built around the wrong priorities for you.

### Treating popularity as product quality

Popularity can show broad interest. It does not prove material, craftsmanship, durability, or suitability for your home and routine.

### Comparing a warehouse product with a custom build

One prioritizes an existing configuration and timing. The other prioritizes supported choices and production review. Compare like with like.

### Shopping from the hero image

Styling, clothing, wigs, and accessories may be optional. Inspect several angles and read the inclusions.

### Ignoring handling weight

Height does not predict weight. Check the exact product in pounds and kilograms before planning cleaning and storage.

### Comparing discount percentages

The configured and delivered total matters more than the largest percentage printed on a banner.

### Treating delivery as the end of the decision

Arrival support, care guidance, minor damage, parts, and repair coordination can affect ownership value for years.
