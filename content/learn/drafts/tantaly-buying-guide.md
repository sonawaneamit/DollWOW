## Choose The Product Format First

Start by deciding how much of the body you want the product to include. Do this before comparing appearance details.

| Format question | What it changes | What to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which body areas are included? | Use, scale, cleaning, storage, and weight | Front, rear, torso, thighs, shoulders, neck, arms, legs, or other included areas |
| How large is the complete product? | Shelf, case, drawer, closet, and handling fit | Height, width, depth, and circumference where available |
| Does it have a rigid or soft base? | Stability, positioning, and long-term storage | Base shape, supported positions, and manufacturer guidance |
| Is any component removable? | Cleaning access and replacement planning | Exact removable part, removal method, and whether it is included |
| Is it designed for posing? | How it can be positioned safely | Internal structure, supported movement, and pressure limits |

A height such as 29 cm or 92 cm does not describe the same thing across every product form. On a torso, height measures the product from one end to another. It does not mean the product represents a person of that height. Width, depth, weight, and included body areas may matter more.

## Compact, Mid-Size, And Larger Formats

### Smaller compact products

The smallest Tantaly products can suit buyers who prioritize discreet storage and easy handling. They still need enough clearance for cleaning and complete drying. Verify the full dimensions, because a short product can still be wide or deep.

### Mid-size torso products

Mid-size models generally provide more body shape while remaining easier to store than many full-body dolls. Compare the product's balance, base, thigh length, shoulder or hip width, and recommended resting position. Do not assume that two products with a similar height will occupy the same space.

### Larger torso products

Larger Tantaly formats can offer more extensive body proportions but may approach full-body dolls in handling demands. Check the listed weight and plan the route from delivery to cleaning and storage. A compact label does not automatically mean lightweight.

### Male torso products

Tantaly also offers male formats. Compare chest, waist, hip or pelvic proportions, included anatomy, height, weight, base design, and storage position. Use the same product-level checks as you would for a female torso rather than assuming every compact male model follows one construction.

## Dimensions And Handling

Compact products are often easier to manage, but “compact” is not a weight rating. Before ordering, picture these movements:

1. Carrying the parcel from the delivery point to a private room
2. Removing the product without pulling a narrow body area
3. Moving it to the cleaning area
4. Supporting it while rinsing and drying
5. Returning it to a relaxed storage position

Ask for height, width, depth, and weight in both U.S. and metric units when those details are not visible. A storage box should allow the product to rest without sharp folds, compression, pressure from hardware, or contact with dark fabrics that may transfer dye.

## How To Read Tantaly Product Photos

Product photographs should help you identify the exact shape and included body areas. They do not always prove scale, material, internal construction, included clothing, or accessories.

## Care And Storage

Tantaly's published care guidance separates TPE and silicone routines. The safe baseline is gentle cleaning with a product-compatible cleanser, a complete clean-water rinse where supported, thorough drying, and relaxed storage away from heat, sunlight, pressure, and dye-transfer risks.

Do not use one generic routine for every product. Confirm:

1. The exact material
2. Whether any component is removable
3. Which cleaning products are approved
4. Whether treatment powder is recommended
5. Which surfaces or fabrics should be avoided
6. The correct resting position

Never seal a product away while moisture remains on the surface or in an internal area. Follow the [cleaning guide](/learn/how-to-clean-a-sex-doll) and the instructions supplied for the exact model.

On a compact or torso product, listed height describes the product itself. Review every dimension and the included body areas.

### Using one care routine for every Tantaly product

TPE and silicone need different care. Follow the exact product guidance.
