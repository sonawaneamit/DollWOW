# Fixed vs Movable Jaw Heads: Options and Ownership Tradeoffs

Choose a movable-jaw head when its documented movement is something you want, not because it sounds like a necessary upgrade. A fixed construction may suit a buyer who mainly wants a particular appearance and does not need that movement.

The important comparison is between exact head versions. "Soft head," "ROS" and "movable jaw" should not be treated as interchangeable technical standards across manufacturers.

## Start with the manufacturer's actual distinction

FANREAL's Vanessa 170 cm D-cup factory listing presents a hard silicone head as the default and a soft silicone movable-jaw head as an alternative. That establishes a choice for that listing. It does not mean every hard head from every brand is identical, or that every soft head has the same mechanism. [FANREAL's official head choices](https://www.fanreal.com/product/vanessa-ae/)

Irontech lists ROS MAX as an upgraded ROS construction. Use its exact designation and selectable head reference rather than treating "MAX" as a promise that all possible facial movements are included. [Irontech's official ROS MAX listing](https://www.irontechdoll.com/product/sex-doll-head-ros-max/)

These examples are useful vocabulary, not a cross-brand compatibility chart. Ask what the version you are buying actually does.

## Compare appearance and movement separately

A fixed head can be the right choice because you prefer its sculpt, expression or available finish. A movable-jaw version may be appealing for supported changes in presentation. Neither choice establishes that the eyes, eyelids or other facial elements move too.

Look at clear references of the same face in the versions being compared. Confirm whether makeup, hair and lighting are similar before attributing every visible difference to the construction.

If one version is shown in a demonstration, establish the exact head code and version in that demonstration. A video of a related head is not a movement instruction for yours.

## Weigh the ownership questions

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Which movement is supported? | Separates the feature from a vague label |
| Is it manual or powered? | Identifies the relevant instructions and equipment |
| What care restrictions apply? | Helps protect the finish and mechanism |
| Which body pairing is approved? | Keeps mechanical fit separate from appearance |
| What changes in the total? | Distinguishes an included head from a paid selection |

Do not infer durability from firmness or softness alone. A reliable comparison would need applicable construction and care evidence, not a general claim that one type "lasts longer."

Similarly, do not assume the more complex version needs a particular service interval unless its manufacturer specifies one. The useful answer is an actual instruction for the head, not an invented maintenance schedule.

## Keep eye and hair options independent

Irontech specifically warns that ROS MAX does not include dynamic eye movement and says not to pull the eyelids to imitate blinking. That restriction matters even though the head is marketed as an upgraded construction. [Factory eye-area warning](https://www.irontechdoll.com/help-faq/)

Check eye colour, gaze adjustment and eyelid movement as separate fields. Do the same for wigs and implanted hair. A construction option is not proof that every appearance or powered option can be combined with it.

For the wider hair decision, see [wig versus implanted hair](/learn/implanted-hair-vs-wig). Ask for the supported combination on your selected head instead of copying another model's menu.

## Confirm the pairing before ordering an extra head

An extra head needs a confirmed body connection, supported material pairing and appropriate colour reference. Matching a thread description alone does not answer the complete fit question.

If you already own the body, send its exact code and order details when asking about another head. If you are ordering a complete custom build, keep the chosen head version in the final specification. Avoid a note that says only "same face as photo."

For warehouse stock, establish which version is already installed. An option offered for new production does not establish that a stocked unit can be rebuilt.

## Choose with a simple priority list

Write down your preferred face, whether jaw movement matters, the supported care routine and the complete configured total. If movement is not a priority, you do not need to buy it simply to have a satisfactory build.

If it is a priority, ask for the applicable operating instructions before deciding. Do not force, stretch or experiment with a new head while trying to discover its limits.
