By Alex, Doll Collector and DollWow Product Educator with 20+ Years of Experience

## Quick Answer

Starpery is a Shenzhen-based doll manufacturer best compared through the complete build: body material, silicone head type, height, weight, finish, skeleton, hands, feet, and supported options. Its range includes full-silicone dolls and silicone-head models with a different body-material path. Start with the exact product rather than assuming every Starpery doll shares the same construction or features.

## Is Starpery A Good Doll Brand?

Starpery can be a strong choice for buyers who care about facial detail, body finish, silicone-focused construction, and a wide selection of body and head combinations. The brand's own product and technical pages place considerable emphasis on realistic proportions, skin texture, hands, feet, head systems, and weight-reduction development.

The important qualification is that **Starpery is not one specification**. A compact torso, a silicone-head model, and a current full-silicone build can have very different handling, care, storage, price, and option requirements. Even two products with similar photography may use different bodies, heads, skeletons, or production systems.

Starpery may be a strong fit if you want:

- A broad choice of adult female body and face styles
- Full-silicone or silicone-head construction paths
- Detailed face, eye, hand, foot, and surface presentation
- A choice between compact and full-body formats
- Product-specific head and appearance choices
- A custom build reviewed before production

Compare another path if your priority is:

- The lowest possible starting price
- A very light product without checking its listed weight
- A single universal option list that applies to every model
- A feature shown in marketing that is not confirmed for the selected body and head
- A ready-to-ship order with extensive changes to the pictured configuration

The DollWow catalog currently distinguishes two important Starpery paths.

| Full silicone | Silicone body with a silicone head | Exact silicone version, softness, listed weight, head, skeleton, hands, feet, and care |
| Silicone head | Silicone head paired with a different body-material path | Body material, head type, connector, weight, finish match, and available options |

Do not use the phrase "silicone doll" as a shortcut for both. A silicone head does not make the entire body silicone. The [TPE versus silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) explains how full-silicone and hybrid ownership paths differ.

Starpery's official option material describes both ordinary and ultra-soft silicone choices in parts of its range. It presents ordinary silicone as the firmer, easier-maintenance path and ultra-soft silicone as the softer path with different compression and elasticity tradeoffs. That distinction is useful only when the selected body supports both versions. It should not be generalized to every listing.

## Head Types And Face Options

Starpery states that its heads use silicone, but the construction and functions can differ. The most useful comparison is not simply "which face looks best?" It is "which exact head type works with the appearance, hair, function, and care routine I want?"

### Hard silicone head

A hard silicone head generally prioritizes shape stability and detailed facial presentation. Starpery's current documentation connects hard heads with implanted-hair availability in supported configurations. Do not assume that one implanted-hair option fits every head or that the pictured hair is included.

### Soft silicone head

A soft head uses a different construction and may support different functions. Starpery's guidance notes different oil, hair, and care considerations than its hard-head path. Confirm current hair compatibility and any functional opening on the exact head.

### ROS-style head

ROS is a selected-head system rather than a universal Starpery feature. Current manufacturer material associates supported ROS heads with an upgraded soft-head construction and additional mouth details. Availability, hair compatibility, price, and body pairing must be checked for the selected head.

When comparing faces, review:

- Front, profile, and three-quarter angles
- Eye placement and current eye options
- Eyebrows, eyelashes, freckles, makeup, and lip finish
- Wig or implanted-hair compatibility
- Head-to-body color and proportion match
- Whether the photo shows the default or a styled configuration

## What Creates Starpery Realism?

Realism comes from the complete finished build, not one close portrait. Starpery's strongest presentation often combines a detailed face with careful body painting, realistic proportions, hands, feet, eyes, hair, and photography. Each layer needs its own check.

### Face and eyes

Look for consistent sculpt detail across several images. Check whether eye color and movement are options, defaults, or photographic styling. A dramatic close-up is useful evidence of the pictured sample, but it does not confirm the final head selection by itself.

### Body proportions

Compare shoulder width, waist, hips, bust, leg length, hands, and feet. Height alone cannot tell you whether the body will feel compact, curvy, tall, or easy to handle.

### Surface finish

Body painting, visible texture, color transitions, freckles, veins, and makeup can change the final appearance. Confirm which finish is standard and which treatment is optional for the exact body.

### Hair and styling

Wigs, implanted hair, outfits, jewelry, and set dressing can transform a product photo. Ask what arrives with the doll. Do not treat every photographed item as part of the included configuration.

## Height And Handling Weight

Starpery's current DollWow listings show why height and weight must be read together. Two dolls of similar height can differ substantially in listed weight because of body volume, material, internal construction, and weight-reduction system.

Before ordering, map the entire ownership route:

- Delivery point to the room
- Doorways, stairs, corners, and narrow passages
- Cleaning surface and drainage access
- Storage position and clearance
- Whether one person can lift and reposition the listed weight safely

Convert both units when comparing products. DollWow displays centimeters with feet and inches, and pounds with kilograms, wherever verified measurements are available. The [storage guide](/learn/sex-doll-storage) includes a pre-delivery route test.

## Weight Reduction And Skeleton Systems

Starpery publishes ongoing development under its W.R. naming. Its official pages describe W.R. 4.0 as a weight-reduction system available on selected bodies and indicate continued development beyond it. This does not make every Starpery product lightweight, nor does it prove that every body uses the same generation.

For the exact listing, confirm:

- Whether weight reduction is included, optional, or unavailable
- The final listed weight for the chosen body and material
- Whether a softer material selection changes handling or care
- Which skeleton is used
- Which standing, hand, and foot choices work with that skeleton

Starpery's current site also promotes a newer gear-skeleton path. Treat it as model-specific. A general brand page cannot confirm that a particular DollWow listing includes it.

## Hands, Feet, And Standing Choices

Hands and feet matter because they affect appearance, posing, maintenance, and storage. Manufacturer imagery may show upgraded hands, detailed feet, standing hardware, or a specific skeleton. Confirm what belongs to the exact product.

Questions worth asking include:

- Does the selected body use wire fingers, articulated fingers, or another hand system?
- Are the photographed hands standard for this listing?
- Are standing feet supported on this material and skeleton?
- Do standing feet use visible hardware?
- Does the option change care, posing, or storage guidance?

Standing feet help with supported posing. They do not automatically prove that a doll should be left standing for long periods. Use the exact manufacturer instructions and a stable support method.

## Ready-To-Ship Versus Made-To-Order

A ready-to-ship Starpery doll is an existing unit with a fixed configuration. Confirm the warehouse, exact head and body, material, included styling, listed weight, and dispatch path. Do not assume that a stock unit can accept the same changes as a factory order.

A made-to-order doll begins with a base product and supported choices. Eligible DollWow custom builds receive a Human Build Check before production. Where the supplier supports it, eligible orders can also receive factory media for approval before shipment.

Choose ready-to-ship when the confirmed unit already fits your needs and timing matters. Choose made-to-order when a supported head, finish, hair, eye, skeleton, or appearance choice matters more than the shortest fulfillment path.

## FAQs

### Are Starpery dolls full silicone?

Some Starpery dolls are full silicone. Others use a silicone head with a different body-material path. Confirm the body and head materials separately on the exact listing.

### Is Starpery based in China?

Starpery's public company material identifies the business as Shenzhen Starpery Technology Co., Ltd. DollWow verifies current products through catalog and supplier information rather than relying on location alone.

### When was Starpery founded?

Starpery publishes a company roadmap dated March 2020, but DollWow has not found a sufficiently clear primary-source incorporation date to present a founding year as settled fact.

### Does Starpery use platinum silicone?

Starpery states that its silicone dolls use platinum-cured silicone. Buyers should still confirm the exact product material and current supplier specification before ordering.

### What is Starpery W.R. 4.0?

W.R. 4.0 is Starpery's name for a weight-reduction development used on supported bodies. It is not proof that every Starpery doll uses that system or has the same final weight.

### Are Starpery dolls lightweight?

Some current Starpery listings have lower weights than others, but the range also includes substantial full-body products. Compare the exact listed weight in pounds and kilograms.

### Can Starpery heads have implanted hair?

Implanted hair may be supported on selected hard heads. Hair compatibility varies by head type and should be confirmed before production.

### What is a Starpery ROS head?

ROS is a selected upgraded head path described by Starpery with additional mouth details and functions. It is available only on supported heads and configurations.

### Can I change the head on a Starpery doll?

Some made-to-order products support alternate heads, but body compatibility, skin match, connector, price, and production details must be confirmed for the exact product.

### Do Starpery dolls have standing feet?

Standing choices may be available on supported bodies and skeletons. Confirm the exact option and follow the product-specific posing and storage guidance.

### Do Starpery photos show the included configuration?

Not necessarily. Product photography may include a particular wig, outfit, eyes, makeup, or optional finish. Check the written configuration and ask support about anything unclear.

### Should I choose ready-to-ship or made-to-order?

Choose ready-to-ship when the confirmed unit already fits your needs. Choose made-to-order when supported appearance or construction choices matter more than the shortest fulfillment path.
