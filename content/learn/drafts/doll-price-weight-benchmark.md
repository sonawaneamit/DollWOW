# Build a Doll Price and Weight Snapshot: A Three-Product Worksheet

To build a useful price-and-weight snapshot, define what counts as one observation before calculating anything. Then keep the source date, price basis and reason for excluding a candidate with the numbers. The exercise here shows how a shortlist changes when you apply your own limits; it does not estimate the market.

This small snapshot was checked on 16 October 2026. It describes three named products, not a catalog-wide benchmark, a controlled material comparison or a set of DollWOW scale measurements.

## Three public listings, checked on one date

These are the same three named product examples shown alongside DollWOW's earlier size-and-weight analysis, with their public pages checked again for this article. Each is a full-body custom-made listing, produced for the order and shipped from the factory; no extra head, hairstyle or warehouse duplicate has been added to the count. They are deliberately selected examples, not a representative sample of the catalog.

| Product | Listed construction | Height | Listed weight | Displayed USD base price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Real Lady Viki R10](/products/real-lady-viki-150cm-r10-silicone-doll) | Silicone | 150 cm | 26 kg | $3,181 |
| [Starpery Yedda](/products/starpery-yedda-167cm-e-cup-silicone-head-companion-doll-1mzjd) | Silicone head, TPE body | 167 cm | 37 kg | $1,799 |
| [Dolls Castle Zarina](/products/doll-castle-zarina-175cm-x-cup-tpe-companion-doll-1ct53) | TPE | 175 cm | 69 kg | $2,199 |

Prices are the public base-configuration amounts, before selected paid additions, shipping, taxes and checkout discounts. The weight column transcribes each page's product weight, not its packed shipment weight. The pages do not establish a shared head-and-option weighing protocol, so these are listing observations rather than normalized finished-build measurements.

## What the sample calculations show

The denominator is three product listings representing three different bodies. Ordering the prices gives $1,799, $2,199 and $3,181: a sample median of $2,199 and a range of $1,799 to $3,181.

The listed weights are 26, 37 and 69 kg: a sample median of 37 kg and a range of 26 to 69 kg. These calculations use the displayed metric figures without converting rounded pounds back into kilograms.

Those medians describe this selected trio only. They are not the typical price or weight of a DollWOW purchase, and they say nothing about sales volume or which model buyers prefer.

Viki's base price is $982 higher than Zarina's despite its lower listed weight. That shows why price per kilogram is not a useful quality score. It does not establish which material is better, softer or more durable.

## Recalculate when your shortlist changes

For an illustrative buyer only, set two screening limits: a listed product weight no greater than 40 kg and a displayed base price no greater than $2,500. These are hypothetical preferences, not recommended lifting limits, a delivered-cost budget or a claim about the weight of a configured unit.

| Candidate | Listed weight at most 40 kg? | Base price at most $2,500? | Keep under both rules? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Viki | Yes: 26 kg | No: $3,181 | No; price rule |
| Yedda | Yes: 37 kg | Yes: $1,799 | Yes; pending configuration and delivered-cost checks |
| Zarina | No: 69 kg | Yes: $2,199 | No; weight rule |

Two rows pass each rule separately, but only one passes both. Do not add those two counts or describe all three as affordable and manageable. Passing this screen does not establish head-inclusive weight, safe handling or the final purchase total.

If you apply only the weight rule, two observations remain. Their price median is ($1,799 + $3,181) / 2 = $2,490; their listed-weight median is (26 + 37) / 2 = 31.5 kg. If you apply both rules, only Yedda remains, so its one price and one weight are the entire remaining sample. The changed medians describe a changed selection, not a price trend or a change in any doll.

Keep a reproducible record: the three exact product links above, the observation date, one row per body in this exercise, both screening rules and each exclusion reason. If you later add a second head listing for the same body, group it with the original body or explicitly change the unit to configurations before recalculating. Do not count it silently as another independent body.

## Why this is not the August catalog analysis

The broader [size and weight guide](/learn/sex-doll-size-weight-guide) owns DollWOW's analysis dated 12 August 2026. Its unit is a catalog listing, which can include several appearances or configurations of the same body.

Rechecking three pages does not refresh that entire dataset. Nor can a three-body sample replace its distributions. The value here is narrower: a reproducible shortlist calculation using public observations dated 16 October 2026, with direct links you can inspect. Later visits require new dated observations, not relabelling these prices as current.

The selection is deliberate rather than random. It spans different heights, materials and proportions. One example per construction category cannot support a material-level average or a claim that a particular brand charges more for equivalent quality.

## Build your own comparison on a tighter basis

Choose your practical height and handling range first. Then record each candidate's body and head, source date, availability and price basis. Keep the written material combination rather than shortening "silicone head" to "silicone."

For a matched handling comparison, ask whether every figure includes the selected head and options. Leave missing answers unresolved rather than estimating. A lower displayed figure is not enough if it describes a different weighing basis.

For price, configure only the supported choices you would actually order. Keep base price, configured subtotal and delivered cost separate. The [cost guide](/learn/sex-doll-cost) covers those budgeting layers.

Count shared bodies consistently if you expand the shortlist. Five hairstyle listings should not become five independent body-weight observations simply because they have different names.

## Use a snapshot as a starting point

These three listings show a substantial spread in both displayed price and listed weight. They do not identify the right purchase for you. Storage, proportions, included features and the support path still need their own judgment.

Open [the catalog](/shop/sex-dolls) and recheck the exact configurations when you are ready to buy. A dated observation is useful precisely because you know what it measured, and what it did not.
