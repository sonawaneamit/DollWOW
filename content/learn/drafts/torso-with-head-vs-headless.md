# Renata With or Without a Head: Reading the Factory Figures

A head changes the appearance, handling weight and storage needs of a torso without changing the underlying body into a full-length doll. To judge the difference fairly, compare two versions of the same torso rather than an unrelated lightweight model and a larger head-equipped one.

Climax's ULW 885 Renata factory sheet lists versions with and without a head. The figures below are that manufacturer's reference specifications checked on 16 October 2026, not DollWOW scale measurements, confirmed retail configured weights or a hands-on test.

## What the factory lists

| Detail | Without head | With head |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Net weight | 19.85 kg / 43.76 lb | 21.85 kg / 48.17 lb |
| Packed weight | 24 kg / 52.91 lb | 26 kg / 57.32 lb |
| Torso height, excluding head | 84 cm / 33.07 in | Same underlying torso |
| Body measurements | Bust 86 cm, waist 59 cm, hips 104 cm | Same underlying torso |

The factory gives a carton size of 97 x 45 x 40 cm and identifies an M16 neck connection for its head option. Confirm the selected head and assembled dimensions when planning storage. The DollWOW retail listing and factory sheet did not agree on the head-equipped weight at that check. That retail discrepancy remains unresolved. This article quotes the dated factory table only; it does not adopt a disputed retail weight, infer why the records differ, or calculate a confirmed weight difference between the DollWOW offers. Ask support to confirm the selected head, supplied components and weight basis before ordering around a handling limit.

## Read net and packed weight differently

Net weight describes the unboxed item on the factory sheet; packed weight includes its shipment. Use those labels to identify which measurement you need confirmed for the actual order. Do not compare one version's net weight with the other's packed weight, or treat the factory reference as resolution of the retail discrepancy.

Even the headless version represents a substantial object to lift and rotate. Whether it is manageable depends on your own strength, the route through your home and where you intend to place it. "Torso" describes the product form; it does not guarantee easy handling.

Plan the receiving location and a clear route before delivery. Avoid choosing storage that requires repeated awkward lifting merely because the item will fit inside it.

## Decide what the head adds for you

Choose the head-equipped version if facial presentation is central to how you want the doll to look, dress or photograph. A face and hair can make a larger visual difference than a small change in body finish.

Choose torso-only if that presentation is not important to you and you prefer fewer components to store and care for. There is no separate head or hairstyle to manage; the actual configured handling weight still needs confirmation.

Neither is the more complete purchase for everyone. The useful question is whether the additional component adds something you will value during ownership, rather than only in the first gallery photograph.

## Compare inclusions and price as a pair

DollWOW lists [Renata with a head](/products/climax-renata-104cm-i-cup-ulw-silicone-torso-with-head) and [Renata without a head](/products/climax-renata-84cm-i-cup-ulw-silicone-torso-without-head) separately. Open both offers and check the supplied components and current totals in the same currency.

Record the selected head, hair, body finish and extras before calculating the difference. A factory-direct head surcharge is not automatically the surcharge in another seller's offer. Likewise, photographed clothing should not be counted as included unless the written offer says so.

Check the destination-specific delivery terms separately. A small difference between product prices does not, by itself, establish the difference between delivered totals.

## Think ahead about a later head purchase

If you plan to start headless and add a head later, confirm the intended pairing now. The M16 label is one compatibility detail, not proof that every head with a similar fitting will seat correctly or match the finish.

Ask which connector parts are supplied, whether an adapter is needed, and how the head should be installed and supported. Save that answer with the torso's code. The [custom doll guide](/learn/custom-sex-dolls) explains the wider body-and-head selection process.

For storage, follow the instructions for the actual torso and head. Do not assume that the shipping carton dimensions describe the assembled display height, or that the head should remain attached in every storage arrangement.

## Make the choice around ordinary ownership

Picture a normal care day: moving the torso, cleaning it, changing clothing and returning it to storage. Decide whether the head improves that experience enough to justify the extra component and cost.
