## What Counts as a Sex Robot?

A useful definition starts with observable behavior. A sex robot is an adult companion product with powered hardware or software that creates some form of automated or responsive interaction. The amount of robotics can range from one moving component to a humanoid platform.

| Product type | What it may include | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conventional articulated doll | Manual skeleton, poseable joints, realistic body, optional accessories | Powered movement, speech, sensors, or AI |
| Doll with electronic options | Heating, sound, powered oral function, limited jaw or eye movement | Conversation, autonomous body movement, or learning |
| App-connected AI companion | Voice or text conversation, profiles, memory, personality settings | A physical body, local processing, or independent movement |
| Robotic head or upper body | Motors, facial movement, cameras, microphones, speakers, software | Full-body mobility or consumer availability |

The label should follow the demonstrated function. A standard doll does not become a robot because a seller adds futuristic imagery or uses “AI” in the title.

## AI Doll Versus Sex Robot

AI and robotics overlap, but they are not interchangeable.

An AI doll may use conversational software, a connected app, a voice model, memory, or personality settings. The physical body may remain completely passive. A robot may have motors, sensors, and physical movement but limited intelligence. A product can have both, one, or neither.

## Sex Robot Features to Verify Separately

### Speech and conversation

Ask whether speech comes from an app, cloud service, speaker in the head, or separate device. Confirm supported languages, account requirements, subscription costs, internet dependence, and whether conversation data is retained.

### Mouth and jaw movement

Confirm what moves, how it is powered, whether movement synchronizes with audio, and how the area can be cleaned without exposing motors or connectors to moisture.

### Eye and facial movement

Ask whether eyes, eyelids, brows, lips, or the neck move. Check the demonstrated range, noise, controls, calibration, replacement parts, and compatibility with hair and makeup options.

### Heating

Heating is not proof of robotics. Confirm the heated area, power source, controls, warm-up expectations, operating limits, and manufacturer instructions. Read the [body heating guide](/learn/body-heating-sex-doll-guide).

### Body movement

Distinguish vibration or one powered function from articulated arm, torso, hip, or leg movement. Ask whether movement is autonomous, pre-programmed, app-controlled, or manually assisted.

### Touch and other sensors

Confirm sensor location, sensitivity, response, calibration, and whether the sensor data leaves the device. A general “responsive” claim is not enough.

### Cameras and microphones

Treat cameras and microphones as privacy-sensitive hardware. Ask when they are active, whether there is a physical off switch, where recordings are processed, and how permissions are managed.

### AI and memory

Ask what model or service provides the intelligence, what the system remembers, who controls the account, and whether memory can be viewed, exported, corrected, or deleted.

## A Five-Level Evidence Test

Use this scale when evaluating a sex robot for sale.

| Evidence level | What you have | How much confidence it supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Marketing label | “AI,” “robot,” or “interactive” in a title | Very little |
| 2. Feature description | Written explanation of a specific function | A question list, not proof |
| 3. Current demonstration | Clear video of the exact model performing the function | Evidence of capability, not availability or durability |

If a seller cannot move beyond levels one or two, do not pay a robotics premium based on the label alone.

## Privacy and Security Questions

Connected adult products can process unusually sensitive information. Before creating an account or enabling sensors, check:

- account ownership and password controls
- data collected by microphones, cameras, sensors, and apps
- local versus cloud processing
- retention and deletion controls
- third-party analytics or advertising access
- software-update policy
- Bluetooth and Wi-Fi permissions
- physical camera, microphone, and power controls
- what happens when the product is sold, transferred, or discarded

Do not place a connected device in a private space until you understand when it is listening, recording, transmitting, or storing data.

## Cleaning and Care Around Electronics

Water, motors, wiring, batteries, speakers, connectors, and electronic heads need a different care plan from a conventional body.

- Disconnect power before cleaning unless the manufacturer says otherwise.
- Do not immerse an electronic head or powered component.
- Keep liquid away from ports, seams, sensors, speakers, and battery areas.
- Remove detachable electronics when the documented design allows it.
- Use only material-compatible products on the surrounding silicone or TPE.
- Dry the physical body fully before reconnecting power.
- Store cables, chargers, controllers, and removable parts together.
- Contact support before opening powered hardware or attempting a repair.

The exact manufacturer instructions always take priority. Use the [cleaning guide](/learn/how-to-clean-a-sex-doll) for conventional material areas and ask for an electronics-specific routine before ordering.

## Common Mistakes

### Treating “AI” as proof of movement

Software can converse without controlling any physical part of the doll.

### Treating one moving feature as a humanoid robot

A powered mouth, eye, or internal function is not the same as autonomous full-body movement.

### Buying from a concept video

A demonstration can show what is technically possible without proving normal consumer availability, final specifications, or support.

### Ignoring software dependence

An app-connected feature can change or stop working if the account, server, subscription, operating system, or company changes.

### Cleaning electronics like a standard head

Water and connectors are a poor combination. Obtain the exact cleaning instructions before use.

### Assuming every option is compatible

Hair, makeup, material, head, neck connection, and other electronics can limit which combinations are safe and supported.
