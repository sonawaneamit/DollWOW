## Compare the Exact Doll First

Start with the manufacturer or brand. Then identify the body, head, height, material, and product form. A useful match should answer all of these questions:

1. Is it the same brand and product line?
2. Is it the same body and head pairing?
3. Is the body TPE, silicone, or a hybrid construction?
4. Are the height and measurements the same?
5. Is it a full doll, torso, hips product, head, or another format?
6. Are the included and paid options equivalent?
7. Is one offer a fixed warehouse unit while the other requires production?

Do not assume a shared photo proves a shared order. Adult doll manufacturers often supply campaign imagery to approved sellers. The useful question is whether the page identifies the exact product shown and explains which styling or features are optional.

## Compare Materials Correctly

Material labels need more detail than a single word.

### Full TPE

A full-TPE doll uses TPE for the body and usually the head. TPE is often chosen for softness and value, but formulation, body shape, internal construction, and care guidance vary. Ask for the listed weight and the care routine for the exact model.

### Full silicone

A full-silicone doll uses silicone for both body and head. Silicone can support detailed sculpting and a controlled surface finish, but firmness, weight, flexibility, and maintenance vary by formula and body design. A higher price does not prove that every silicone doll is the right fit for every buyer.

### Hybrid construction

A hybrid may combine a silicone head with a TPE body or use more than one construction method. It can be a practical choice when facial finish and body softness matter, but it should not be described or priced as full silicone.

## Ready-to-Ship or Made-to-Order

Ready-to-ship is usually the better path when a confirmed existing unit fits your needs and timing matters more than customization. Made-to-order is usually the better path when a particular supported head, body, material, color, skeleton, or function matters more than speed.

For a ready-to-ship doll, confirm:

- the exact unit exists now
- the photographs represent the unit or clearly identify reference imagery
- the configuration is fixed or any permitted changes are documented
- the warehouse and expected dispatch are confirmed
- the listed weight and packed delivery requirements are clear

For a made-to-order doll, confirm:

- every selected option applies to the exact body and head
- conflicts or unavailable combinations are resolved before production
- paid changes and production timing are documented
- factory photos or video are available where supported
- the shipment does not proceed before any required approval step is complete

Eligible DollWow custom orders receive a Human Build Check before production. Where supported, Approve Before Shipping provides factory photos or video before release. Those checkpoints help catch an option mismatch while it can still be addressed.

## Compare Product Images Carefully

Images can be beautiful and still leave important questions unanswered. Separate four types of visual evidence:

1. **Manufacturer reference photography:** useful for understanding the sculpt and styling, but it may show optional features or accessories.
2. **Exact ready-stock media:** useful for reviewing a specific completed unit when the seller confirms that the images belong to it.
3. **Custom factory media:** useful for checking the selected head, body, tone, hair, eyes, and obvious build details before shipment.
4. **Promotional imagery:** useful for explaining or presenting a product, but it must not replace accurate product photographs.

Review multiple angles, close-ups, hands, feet, face, skin finish, and the head-to-body pairing. Ask which items in the image are included. DollWow keeps product-specific galleries for inspection and labels generated or enhanced promotional visuals so they are not mistaken for exact product photography.

## Compare Size, Weight, and Home Fit

Height alone does not predict handling weight. Two dolls of similar height can differ substantially because of body volume, material, internal foam, skeleton, and weight-reduction construction.

Compare both US and metric measurements. Check height in feet and inches plus centimeters, and weight in pounds plus kilograms. Then plan for the real route through your home:

- entrance and narrow doorways
- stairs, elevators, and corners
- lifting from the carton
- cleaning and drying access
- storage length, width, and depth
- how often the doll must be moved

## Common Comparison Mistakes

### Comparing store reputation instead of the order

Reputation matters, but it does not identify the exact doll, configuration, delivery path, or support attached to your order. Compare those details directly.

### Treating shared photos as proof of an identical product

Several approved sellers may use the same manufacturer imagery. The offered head, body, material, options, and availability still need to match.

### Comparing a base price with a configured price

Match the selected options and destination before deciding which offer costs less.

### Calling a silicone-head hybrid full silicone

A silicone head with a TPE body is not the same construction as a full-silicone doll. Confirm both body and head material.

### Assuming every option works on every doll

Option availability can vary by body, head, material, skeleton, and brand. Some choices can conflict. Ask for confirmation before production.

### Waiting until after payment to ask important questions

Stock, privacy, dimensions, options, and timing are easier to resolve before the order is committed. Live chat and [hello@dollwow.com](mailto:hello@dollwow.com) are available for those checks.

### Choosing a seller without comparing ownership support

The relationship should not end at delivery. Consider how the seller handles arrival questions, care guidance, minor damage, parts, and repair coordination.
