## Starting Price, Configured Price, and Delivered Cost

These three numbers answer different questions.

### Starting price

The starting price is the least expensive selectable configuration currently associated with the product. It helps buyers scan a large catalog, but it may not include a preferred head, material, skeleton feature, electronic function, accessory, or delivery cost.

### Configured price

The configured price reflects the body, head, material, and paid options selected on the product page. This is the useful number for comparing two made-to-order builds.

If a choice requires manufacturer confirmation, treat it as pending until confirmed. A product configurator can show supported choices and price changes, but it cannot make incompatible options work together.

### Delivered cost

Delivered cost combines the confirmed product configuration with applicable shipping, taxes, duties, or other destination-specific charges shown during the order process. Do not assume that every seller includes the same items in the headline price.

Before paying, ask for clarification if the checkout total, delivery path, or included configuration is unclear.

## What Changes the Price of a Sex Doll?

### Product form

A full doll, mini full doll, torso, hips product, and standalone head are different purchases. Low-price search results often mix these forms together. Confirm the title, images, dimensions, and product type before comparing value.

### Material and construction

The broad choices are full TPE, full silicone, and hybrid construction such as a silicone head with a TPE body.

- TPE often provides a lower starting point and a softer feel.
- Full silicone often carries a higher starting price and can support fine sculpt and surface detail.
- Hybrid construction can combine a silicone head with a TPE body, but it should not be described or priced as full silicone.

Formulations and production methods vary by manufacturer. Material alone does not prove softness, durability, realism, safety grade, or useful life. Read the [TPE versus silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) before paying more for a label you have not compared.

### Height, body volume, and weight

Larger bodies usually require more material, but height does not tell the whole story. Body proportions, internal foam cores, weight-reduction construction, skeleton design, and material all affect finished weight and price.

Use both height and listed dry weight. A lower-priced doll that is difficult to lift, clean, or store may be a poor practical value for the buyer's home.

### Head and body pairing

Some products include a standard head in the starting configuration. Alternate heads, premium face sculpts, silicone heads, implanted hair, oral functions, or electronic systems may cost more where supported.

Confirm that the selected head fits the body, material, neck connection, and chosen functions. A visually appealing combination is not necessarily a manufacturable one.

### Skeleton and standing features

Skeleton design can affect posing, weight, and price. Standing support, articulated fingers, shrugging shoulders, flexible waist or spine systems, and other model-specific features may be standard, optional, or unavailable.

Do not infer a feature from photographs. Check the exact product and selected variant.

### Cosmetic and functional options

Depending on the model, paid choices may include skin tone, eyes, faceup, hair, implanted hair, nails, body details, heating, voice, or other functions. Some appearance choices are included while others change the total.

Choose options for a reason. A long list of upgrades can make a build more expensive without making it better for the buyer's actual priorities.

### Ready-to-ship versus made-to-order

An existing warehouse unit has a largely fixed configuration. A made-to-order doll offers more product-specific choices and enters production after review.

Neither path is automatically cheaper. A ready unit may carry a promotion or a premium for a specific completed build. A custom model can begin at a competitive price and rise as options are added. The [ready-to-ship versus made-to-order guide](/learn/ready-to-ship-vs-custom-sex-dolls) explains the tradeoff.

### Brand and product line

Manufacturers use different materials, molds, skeletons, finishing processes, option systems, and quality-control methods. Price can also vary between product lines from the same brand.

Use brand as one comparison factor, not a substitute for checking the exact body and head. A less expensive model from a recognized manufacturer may fit the buyer better than a more expensive product with unsuitable dimensions or features.

## What Does a Lower Price Really Mean?

A lower price can be completely legitimate. It may reflect:

- a smaller or lighter product
- TPE instead of full silicone
- a simpler skeleton or fewer supported functions
- a fixed ready-to-ship configuration
- a manufacturer or retailer promotion
- a torso or hips product rather than a full doll
- a model from an earlier product line

It can also signal missing information. Pause when the listing does not clearly identify product form, material, dimensions, brand, images, stock status, shipping, or the final configuration.

Do not use price alone as proof of a scam or proof of value. Use the [buyer protection guide](/buyer-protection) and verify the offer.

## Costs After the Doll Arrives

The purchase price is the largest expense, but ownership also needs a practical setup.

Plan for:

- material-appropriate cleaning supplies
- clean towels and complete drying
- storage that avoids pressure, heat, moisture, and dark dye transfer
- suitable clothing or accessories if desired
- help or equipment for moving a heavier doll
- replacement wigs, care powder, or small repair supplies when appropriate

These costs depend on the product and the buyer's setup, so a universal lifetime-cost figure would be misleading. The larger decision is whether the doll fits the space, handling ability, and care routine available.

## How to Compare Two Prices Correctly

Use this sequence before deciding that one offer is cheaper.

### 1. Match the exact product

Compare the same brand, body, head, height, material, skin tone, skeleton, and functions. A similar photograph is not enough.

### 2. Match the product form

Confirm that both offers are full dolls, or that both are the same compact form. Do not compare a torso or mini product with a full-size body.

### 3. Match included options

List what is standard, selected, paid, included, and still subject to confirmation.

### 4. Match stock and production status

An existing unit and a made-to-order build have different configuration and timing tradeoffs. Confirm whether the exact unit is available.

### 5. Match delivery and taxes

Check the destination, shipping method, duties or taxes where applicable, and whether the quoted total includes them.

### 6. Match support and approval

Compare pre-production review, eligible factory media, arrival process, care support, and repair help. These services have value when the purchase is private, customized, and expensive.

### 7. Save the comparable offer

Keep the product URL, configuration, visible price, date, and checkout evidence. A screenshot without the exact product details may not show a comparable offer.

## A Practical Budget Method

### Set a complete ceiling

Choose the maximum total you are comfortable paying, including likely options and delivery charges. Do not spend the entire budget on the starting price if a required option is still missing.

### Write down three non-negotiables

Examples include maximum weight, full silicone, a particular height range, a compatible head, ready-to-ship status, or a private delivery requirement.

### Compare one band above and below

Look at products in the target range, then sample the neighboring price bands. This shows what changes when the budget moves and helps separate useful features from promotional language.

### Remove upgrades that do not solve a need

Paid options should support appearance, handling, care, or a function you actually want. Skip an upgrade when you cannot explain the benefit.

### Confirm the final build in writing

Review the configured product, price, options, production or stock status, and delivery expectations before checkout.

## Common Price Mistakes

### Treating the lowest number as the complete price

It may be a starting configuration, a smaller product form, or a model without selected upgrades.

### Assuming full silicone from the word silicone

A silicone-head/TPE-body hybrid is not a full-silicone doll. Check head and body construction separately.

### Comparing different sizes

A compact model and a large full-size body use different amounts of material and create different handling needs.

### Adding every available option

More options do not automatically create a better build. Compatibility and buyer fit matter more than option count.

### Ignoring ownership setup

Storage, cleaning, lifting, and minor care supplies are part of practical value.

### Using an old screenshot as a current quote

Catalog prices, stock, promotions, and options change. Open the live product page and confirm the comparable configuration.
