# 2026 Factory Upgrades: A Dated Buyer Check on Model Support

An upgrade announcement is useful only when it tells you something about the doll you are considering. Start with the exact body, head and selected construction, then establish whether the new feature is supported and included.

This is a selective review of factory documentation checked on October 16, 2026, not a complete industry release list. A review date confirms when the information was checked; it is not automatically the feature's launch date.

## The documented updates at a glance

| Update | Date information | Confirmed scope to use when shopping |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SE LSTPE lightweight option | Factory article shows September 24 and September 29, 2026 | Names STPE bodies 157H, 161F and 163E |
| Irontech ULW | Factory article shows June 22 and August 5, 2026 | Describes selected silicone bodies, not every Irontech build |
| Real Lady ULW documentation | 2026 white paper; checked October 16 | Uses Real Lady-specific body records and configuration limits |

The paired dates displayed on the SE and Irontech articles should not be treated as two separate launches. The underlying sources are the [SE LSTPE announcement](https://www.sedoll.com/stpe-series-introduces-a-new-custom-option-lstpe-lightweight-upgrade/), [Irontech ULW article](https://www.irontechdoll.com/blog/ultra-light-weight-technology/) and [Real Lady ULW white paper](https://www.real-lady.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Real_Lady_ULW_White_Paper_2026_Branded.pdf).

## SE: compare the supported body, not the largest saving

SE describes LSTPE as STPE with a lightweight internal foam core. Its announcement limits the option to the named bodies and presents different weight changes for each. The buyer's useful comparison is standard versus LSTPE for the same body, not the biggest reduction mentioned anywhere in the release.

It also treats a silicone-head selection as a separate upgrade. Cross-material head matching has its own supported choices; a body-weight upgrade does not turn the full doll into silicone or establish every head pairing.

Use the [factory's LSTPE weight table](https://www.sedoll.com/stpe-series-introduces-a-new-custom-option-lstpe-lightweight-upgrade/) for the body-specific figures. When choosing a product, check that both the material and the lightweight selection belong to the actual build you are configuring.

## Irontech: a technology label is not a universal percentage

Irontech's ULW article describes weight reduction for selected silicone body models. Its general performance statements do not establish the finished weight of every available doll.

For your shortlist, ask for the full body code and the weight of the selected standard or ULW version. Establish whether that figure includes the head. Do not calculate a personal handling weight by applying a headline percentage to a different listing.

The factory also reports its own testing. That is manufacturer-reported evidence, not a DollWOW test or permission to reproduce stress poses at home. Follow the actual product's care instructions.

## Real Lady: keep its records separate

The Real Lady white paper identifies its own production records and distinguishes them from Irontech data. It also notes an incomplete standard-weight comparison for RL170. A shared technology label does not justify combining different brands' figures into one guaranteed reduction.

For a Real Lady purchase, use the applicable body code and current confirmed configuration. The [Real Lady collection](/brands/real-lady-dolls) is the place to find available DollWOW listings; a factory document alone does not establish that an option is offered on every listing.

## Do not relabel older features as 2026 launches

Some features remain relevant without being new this year. FANREAL's UniSeries introduction is dated December 2025 and describes integrated head-and-body construction. Seeing that feature in a current catalog does not make it a 2026 release. [FANREAL's dated UniSeries introduction](https://www.fanreal.com/fanreal-uniseries-intro/)

Likewise, a new photograph, promotion or video folder may document an existing feature. Ask what changed in construction or availability before treating updated marketing as an upgrade.

## Check what the upgrade changes for you

For any new feature, write down the exact supported model, the benefit you need, the selected price and any compatibility restriction. Ask whether it must be chosen before production and whether the relevant stocked unit already has it.

Consider the whole routine. Reduced body weight may help, but delivery still has a packed weight and dimensions. A different head may change appearance, but it does not automatically add eye movement or a different connector.
