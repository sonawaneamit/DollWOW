# How to Read a Male Doll Specification Sheet

A male doll specification sheet becomes useful when you translate each figure into a buying decision. Height helps with overall scale; shoulder width helps with dressing and space; confirmed assembled weight helps you plan handling. None of those figures can replace the others.

This guide focuses on reading the sheet, rather than choosing a style or material. For that broader decision, use the [male doll buying guide](/learn/male-sex-doll-buying-guide).

## Start with the complete model identity

Record the manufacturer, body identifier, head identifier and materials before copying measurements. A face name can appear with more than one body. The exact body and head combination is what makes the specification relevant to your order.

For example, [Irontech's official male guide](https://www.irontechdoll.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-male-sex-dolls/) identifies 162, 170, 175 and 176 cm male body sizes. That range is a reason to record the exact body, not evidence that every named head is available on every size.

Check the specification's date or version where available. If an updated body shares an older name, ask which version the listing represents. Keep the source link beside your notes so you can return to the original rather than a detached screenshot.

This annotation yields a focused follow-up: "For this Kevin listing, which head is supplied, does 38 kg include it, and what are the packed dimensions and weight?" A blank field is not zero and does not mean a feature is absent.

For a hypothetical clothing check, suppose a shirt chart gives a 96 cm chest and 40 cm shoulders. Its chest figure exceeds Kevin's listed 91 cm, but its shoulders are 3 cm narrower than the listed 43 cm. That is a reason to investigate the cut and fastenings, not to declare the shirt suitable from the chest figure alone. These garment numbers are invented for the exercise; no shirt has been fitted or tested.

## Separate body, head and shipping weight

Ask whether the listed net weight includes the chosen head. If body and head weights are given separately, confirm whether accessories or a selected construction upgrade change the assembled figure before combining them.

Shipping weight includes packaging and may include other contents. Use it for delivery planning, not as a substitute for the doll's handling weight. A courier bringing a package to the entrance does not answer how you will move it through your home.

Convert units only after the scope is clear. A precise conversion of an ambiguous figure still leaves you with an ambiguous figure. See the [size and weight guide](/learn/sex-doll-size-weight-guide) for the wider planning process.

## Read clothing dimensions carefully

Distinguish circumference from width. Chest circumference goes around the body; shoulder width is a straight-line measurement between defined points. Comparing one with the other can produce a badly misleading clothing choice.

For shirts, look beyond chest circumference to shoulder width, upper arms, sleeve openings and how the garment fastens. For trousers, check hips, waist, thigh and leg measurements where available. A muscular sculpt can fit the waist of a garment but not pass through its narrowest opening.

For shoes, use foot length and width rather than a loosely translated size label. Choose clothing that can be put on without forcing joints or dragging tight seams across the finish. Exact measurements help, but they do not make every human clothing cut suitable.

## Translate options into construction questions

An articulated skeleton does not specify every joint's movement limits. Standing feet do not explain the approved support arrangement. A removable head does not establish the connector standard.

Ask for the exact skeleton generation, foot construction, head connection and relevant restrictions. Mark each optional feature as included, extra-cost, unsupported or awaiting confirmation. Do not transfer an option from a neighbouring product because its photographs look similar.

For powered features, keep the matching manual and component list separate from the dimension sheet. A specification table is not an operating guide.

## Use the completed sheet to rule products in or out

Translate the confirmed dimensions into your storage and clothing plan, and the confirmed weight into your moving arrangements. Then consider appearance and extras within those limits.
