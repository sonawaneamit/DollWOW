By Alex, Doll Collector and DollWow Product Educator with 20+ Years of Experience

## Quick Answer

Irontech is an established doll manufacturer with silicone, TPE, hybrid, female, and male models. Its range is unusually broad, so the best Irontech doll is not one universal model. Start with material, body measurements, listed weight, head system, and order path. Then confirm every option against the exact body and head before production.

## Irontech Brand History

Irontech's official company history dates the brand to **2015**. The manufacturer is based in Zhongshan, Guangdong, China, an established manufacturing region for the industry. Irontech describes its original focus as improving body proportions, facial sculpting, materials, and presentation for international buyers.

Over the following decade, the range expanded beyond its earlier TPE products into full-silicone bodies, hybrid construction, male bodies, multiple silicone head systems, and newer skeleton and weight-reduction options. The current catalog reflects that development: Irontech is not one narrow series but a family of constructions and option systems.

The useful buyer takeaway is not the marketing history itself. It is that an Irontech model should be evaluated as a specific **body + head + material + options** build. Brand familiarity does not make every option universal.

## What Irontech Is Known For

### Broad body and face selection

Irontech products span different heights, proportions, body presentations, and head styles. That range makes the brand useful for a buyer with a defined appearance in mind, but it also makes exact measurements more valuable than gallery photography alone.

### Silicone head systems

Irontech's official option documentation distinguishes hard silicone heads, soft silicone heads, ROS heads, and ROS MAX heads. These are not interchangeable labels.

- **Hard silicone heads** can support mechanical eyes on compatible models and may support implanted hair.
- **Soft silicone heads** use a softer mouth area and have a different eye and hair path.
- **ROS heads** add a movable jaw and more detailed oral structure on supported head models.
- **ROS MAX heads** are a further manufacturer-defined system with revised internal construction on supported models.

Compatibility lists change. A product title that mentions a silicone head does not prove that ROS, ROS MAX, implanted hair, or every eye option is available. DollWow checks the selected head and body combination during the [Human Build Check](/care-for-life).

### Full silicone, TPE, and hybrid choices

Irontech currently appears in DollWow's catalog across all three major construction paths:

- **Full silicone:** chosen for detailed surface finish, sculpt definition, and a firmer, more stable material path on many models.
- **TPE:** often starts at a lower price and may offer a softer feel, with different care and stain-management needs.
- **Hybrid:** typically combines a silicone head with a TPE body, aiming to balance facial finish with TPE body feel and price.

Read the [TPE versus silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) before deciding. Hybrid is not automatically the best of both materials, and it still needs exact care guidance for each component.

### Female and male models

Irontech is one of the carried brands with both female and male full-body models. Male-doll buyers should compare shoulder width, chest and waist measurements, anatomy, height, weight, skeleton, and storage route rather than assuming that height describes overall scale. The [male doll buying guide](/learn/male-sex-doll-buying-guide) covers those checks in more detail.

### Configuration depth

Depending on the product, Irontech documentation may present choices involving head type, hair, eyes, body softness, skin tone, skeleton, hands, feet, and other features. Some options add weight, change durability tradeoffs, or conflict with another feature. The product-specific configurator and support confirmation take priority over a brand-wide menu.

Irontech's head menu is one of the main reasons buyers need a product-level check.

| Head path | General manufacturer description | Important limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hard silicone head | Firmer construction with mechanical-eye compatibility on supported models | Mouth feel and supported options differ from soft or ROS systems |

The safest order sequence is to choose the body first, shortlist compatible heads, and only then choose hair, eyes, and head functions. Starting with a promotional photograph and attempting to recreate every visible detail can lead to incompatible selections.

## Skeleton, Hands, And Feet

### Standard and EVO skeleton paths

Irontech describes a standard high-strength frame for TPE models and an EVO skeleton option on supported builds. Its silicone documentation also describes EVO features such as revised joint movement. Treat those descriptions as model-specific option paths, not proof that every current listing includes the same frame.

Skeleton tightness may also be configurable. A tighter setup may suit frequent photography and pose holding, while a more relaxed setup can make repositioning easier. Neither choice removes the need to support the doll during movement or follow the manufacturer's pose limits.

### Hand construction

Supported Irontech silicone models may offer standard hands, harder hand construction, or different finger-joint systems. Harder material can change appearance or feel in exchange for added structural protection. Finger articulation does not make fingers load-bearing. Move each finger gently and never use a hand or wrist as a lifting point.

### Standing feet

Irontech documents both bolted standing feet and a hardened, bolt-free standing-foot option on supported models. Standing feet do not prove that a doll should remain unsupported for long periods. Confirm the exact foot construction, use a stable surface, supervise the pose, and follow model-specific storage guidance. Our [standing feet guide](/learn/standing-feet-sex-doll-guide) explains the planning questions.

## Softness And Filling Options

Softness labels are easy to overread. Irontech documentation describes standard and softer body paths plus selected breast, butt, belly, and thigh options. These choices can affect feel, weight, pressure sensitivity, durability, and compatible functions.

For example, a gel-filled area may add weight and may need more careful long-term positioning. A softer construction may trade some resistance to wear or paint retention for a softer feel. A named filling option may also be limited to a particular series. Confirm all of the following before paying for an upgrade:

1. The option is supported on the selected body.
2. The option does not conflict with another selected function.
3. The added weight is known or clearly marked as awaiting confirmation.
4. Care and storage requirements are acceptable.
5. The configuration shown in factory media matches the approved order.

## Irontech ULW And Electronic Features

## How To Choose The Right Irontech Doll

### 1. Set a handling limit

Decide what you can lift, rotate, clean, dress, and store without strain. Height is not a reliable substitute for weight. The two male examples above differ by 39.7 lb / 18 kg even though their heights differ by only 6 cm / 2 in.

### 2. Choose material before fine details

Compare full silicone, TPE, and hybrid ownership. Include stain risk, cleaning routine, powder or treatment guidance, repair path, feel preference, and cost. Do not choose from face photography alone.

### 3. Choose body and head as a pair

Write down the exact body code and head model. Ask which head systems fit that body and which options remain available after the pairing is selected.

### 4. Build an option hierarchy

Divide choices into three groups:

- **Essential:** requirements that would change whether you buy the doll
- **Preferred:** upgrades you value if compatible and within budget
- **Decorative:** styling that can be changed later, such as a separate wig or outfit

This prevents a decorative photograph from driving a permanent construction decision.

### 5. Review final measurements and weight

Recheck height, listed weight, chest, waist, hips, shoulder width where relevant, and any weight added by selected options. Plan the delivery route and storage location before production begins.

### 6. Confirm the complete build

DollWow's Human Build Check reviews the selected product and configuration before production. For eligible custom orders, factory media can provide another chance to compare the completed build with the approved order before shipment. Availability of specific media remains subject to the order path.

## Common Irontech Buying Mistakes

### Assuming every option fits every body

Irontech's menu is broad, but compatibility is specific. Confirm the body, head, material, and conflicts as one build.

### Choosing only by face photography

A face can lead the shortlist, but body scale, weight, material, storage, and care determine daily ownership.

### Treating TPE, hybrid, and silicone as minor variations

They create different care, handling, price, and finish tradeoffs. Compare construction before upgrades.

### Ignoring weight added by options

Soft fillings and other upgrades may change handling. Ask for the configured estimate when that affects your decision.

### Assuming standing feet mean permanent standing storage

They do not. Use model-specific support and storage guidance.

### Treating a named electronic feature as universal

Confirm the exact hardware, compatibility, power setup, controls, and support terms.

## FAQs

### When was Irontech Doll founded?

### What is an Irontech hybrid doll?

An Irontech hybrid generally combines a silicone head with a TPE body. Confirm the exact head, body, junction, care routine, and compatible options on the selected listing.

### What is the difference between Irontech ROS and ROS MAX heads?

Both are manufacturer-defined silicone head systems with a movable jaw and detailed internal structure. ROS MAX uses revised construction and has its own supported-head list. Confirm compatibility rather than assuming the newer label fits every body or head.

### Can all Irontech dolls have implanted hair?

No. Hair compatibility depends on head construction. Irontech states that its TPE heads use wigs, while implanted hair is available only on supported silicone heads.

### Are Irontech dolls heavy?

Weight varies substantially by body, material, and options. Current examples on DollWow range from about 71.7 lb / 32.5 kg to more than 123 lb / 56 kg. Check the exact listing and configured build.

### Does Irontech offer lighter silicone bodies?

### Can an Irontech doll stand?

Supported models may offer bolted or bolt-free standing feet. This does not make unsupported long-term standing safe. Confirm the exact foot system and follow the product-specific instructions.

### Are Irontech product photos the exact final build?

Not necessarily. Photography may show a particular head, body, makeup, wig, skin tone, clothing, or option set. Compare the approved order details and eligible factory media with the listing.
