# Requesting a Replacement Part: A Worked Aftercare Worksheet

When a small part is damaged or missing, the most useful first step is identifying it accurately. A photograph that looks similar is a starting point, not proof that a replacement will fit your doll.

This hypothetical worksheet follows a request for a replacement removable wig. It is not a customer case study, an approved claim or a promise of free replacement. The same information-gathering method can help with other parts, while their technical requirements may be quite different.

## Start with the order and exact item

Find the order reference and final configuration. Record the manufacturer, body and head codes, and the item you need. For a wig, include the original selection, colour and style if known.

Explain whether the item arrived damaged, was lost later or developed a problem during ownership. Those are different situations. A support request does not need a confident diagnosis; a clear description of what happened is more useful.

If you are unsure whether the hair is a removable wig or implanted, ask before pulling or attempting removal. Do not turn an identification question into a repair attempt.

## Build the hypothetical request

In this example, an owner has damaged a removable wig while it was stored separately. The head itself appears unaffected. These circumstances are invented solely to illustrate the worksheet.

| Field | Filled illustrative request |
| --- | --- |
| Product identity | Order reference retained privately; exact head code still to retrieve from the original record |
| Requested item | Removable wig only; not a replacement head or implanted hair |
| Original appearance | Dark brown, straight, shoulder length; no readable size or part label |
| What happened | Cap seam tore while the wig was stored separately; no claim of arrival damage |
| Photographs | Overall wig and close-up of torn seam; no address label in frame |
| Desired outcome | A compatible replacement in a similar style, with price and fit confirmed before acceptance |

The colour, style and damage above are invented details, not a photographed product or customer history. The missing head code stays unresolved in the request: a detailed description is not permission to guess fit. A useful final question is, "Which head or cap measurements do you need to identify a compatible replacement, and what would the complete supplied price be?"

The request should leave room for an answer other than an exact match. A hairstyle may no longer be available, and a replacement may need a different reference or size confirmation.

## Photograph the detail without exposing your home

Take a clear overall photograph and a closer view of the issue. Use sufficient light and keep receipts, address labels and personal documents out of the background. Send the order reference through the support channel rather than displaying it in a public image.

Do not include more of the doll or room than the question requires. For a wig request, the relevant item and its fit information may be enough; support can ask for another view if necessary.

Keep the damaged item until support advises what is needed. Discarding it immediately may remove useful identification or review evidence.

## Ask about compatibility and the full cost

Before accepting a replacement, confirm what is supplied, how fit is established and whether any additional attachment or fitting instructions are needed. An accessory sharing the same brand name is not automatically the right size or version.

Ask for the replacement price, delivery charge, applicable taxes or duties and current availability. A part's availability is separate from a delivery estimate. Do not make plans around an unconfirmed dispatch date.

For head or electronic parts, the questions become more technical. Connector, power, material and version information may matter. Do not substitute a generic adapter or open a powered component while waiting for an answer.

## Understand the support path

As checked on 16 October 2026, [Care for Life](/care-for-life) describes one eligible accidental-damage rescue within the first 365 days after delivery. DollWOW reviews eligibility and selects the resolution; it is not an automatic replacement entitlement.

The policy distinguishes arrival and transit issues from later accidental damage and asks that arrival issues be reported within 24 hours of delivery. It also describes ongoing repair guidance and parts help where available after the first year. Read the current terms for your situation rather than assuming this hypothetical wig request qualifies.

## Keep a short outcome record

Once support identifies the next step, retain the confirmed part description, price or coverage decision, instructions and any delivery reference. If an alternative is proposed, approve the particular alternative rather than simply saying "anything similar."

For this hypothetical request, the decision record can end in three different ways: accept an identified compatible wig and its confirmed total; request another style if the fit works but the appearance does not; or leave the request open if fit or availability cannot be established. None of those branches assumes that a replacement has already been sourced or that coverage has been approved.

Send the completed information to [support](/support). A concise, well-identified request gives the team something practical to investigate without requiring you to diagnose the problem alone.
