# Sex Doll Maintenance: A Practical Care Checklist

By Jesse, Licensed Sexologist and DollWow Intimacy Education Editor

## Quick Answer

Sex doll maintenance is a repeatable cycle: confirm the material, clean gently after use, dry every surface and internal area fully, inspect for stains or damage, protect the doll from pressure and dark dyes, and store it in a supported position. Electronics, implanted hair, standing systems, and mixed-material builds need their own instructions. When something changes, stop and ask before experimenting.

This checklist is an owner reminder, not a replacement for the instructions supplied with the exact doll. Manufacturer formulations, finishes, skeletons, electronics, and supported storage methods vary.

The routine should reduce guesswork. It should not encourage unnecessary handling, aggressive cleaning, or products that have not been confirmed for the material.

## Before Purchase: Make Care Part Of The Buying Decision

Maintenance becomes difficult when the doll does not fit the buyer's space, strength, or privacy needs. Product photography cannot answer those practical questions by itself.

Before ordering, confirm:

- whether the body is TPE, full silicone, or a silicone-head and TPE-body hybrid
- height in feet and inches and centimeters
- listed weight in pounds and kilograms
- whether the product is a full doll, torso, hips product, or another compact form
- the route from delivery to the cleaning and storage area
- whether the chosen storage position is supported for that exact body
- whether standing feet, skeleton options, heating, powered functions, or an electronic head change care
- whether wigs, implanted hair, removable components, and accessories need separate handling
- which care items are included and which must be purchased separately
- where to ask for product-specific instructions before checkout

A listed weight that looks manageable on a screen may feel very different when the product must be lifted from a low bed, turned for drying, or moved through a narrow doorway. ## Routine Maintenance Checklist

### 1. Confirm the material before choosing a product

Do not assume a care product is suitable because the bottle says it is for dolls. Confirm whether it is compatible with the exact TPE or silicone formulation, surface finish, paint, faceup, and any mixed-material construction. A silicone head on a TPE body may require two care paths.

### 2. Clean gently after use

Use the method and products supported for the exact material. Avoid abrasive tools, harsh household cleaners, unknown solvents, and very hot water. Keep liquid away from powered components, ports, speakers, wiring, and motors.

The detailed [sex doll cleaning guide](/learn/how-to-clean-a-sex-doll) covers the cleaning sequence, internal drying, electronics boundaries, and common mistakes without assuming one method fits every doll.

### 3. Dry fully

Surface dryness is only part of the job. Check folds, seams, fingers, toes, hairline areas, removable parts, and internal areas that were cleaned. Use gentle airflow and absorbent materials that will not transfer color or leave abrasive fibers. Do not use high heat to rush the process.

Storage begins only after the doll is fully dry. Trapped moisture can create odor and complicate material care.

### 4. Inspect before putting the doll away

Use ordinary room light and look over the areas that were handled, cleaned, dressed, or under pressure. Check for:

- new dye transfer or discoloration
- tackiness or an unusual surface change
- cuts, tears, punctures, or stretched openings
- changes around seams, fingers, toes, and standing hardware
- pressure marks from clothing, furniture, straps, or storage supports
- loose components or a change in joint resistance
- moisture near electronics or connectors

Do not turn every harmless variation into a repair project. The purpose is to notice changes and ask early when something looks wrong.

### 5. Apply powder only when it is supported

Some TPE products use a compatible renewal powder after cleaning and complete drying. That does not make powder universal. Full silicone, special finishes, painted areas, electronics, hair systems, and particular manufacturer formulations may need different treatment.

Confirm the product, application area, amount, and frequency. Never apply powder over trapped moisture.

### 6. Store the doll in a supported position

Use clean, light-colored, nonabrasive material between the doll and storage surfaces. Avoid sharp edges, concentrated pressure, cramped joint positions, direct sunlight, excessive heat, and dark fabrics that may transfer dye.

The correct position depends on the body, skeleton, standing system, and manufacturer guidance. Hanging, standing, lying flat, and a storage case are not universally interchangeable. ## Clothing And Dye Transfer

Dark, saturated, or unwashed fabric can transfer color to TPE and silicone. The risk depends on the dye, material, contact time, pressure, heat, and finish.

Use these habits:

1. Wash new clothing separately until excess dye is removed.
2. Test a hidden material sample when the manufacturer provides one.
3. Use light-colored barriers under dark clothing.
4. Avoid leaving tight, dark garments on the doll during storage.
5. Check high-pressure areas such as straps, waistbands, seams, socks, and footwear.
6. Remove a suspicious garment and ask for material-specific help before trying a stain treatment.

Do not attack a stain with bleach, solvent, abrasive scrubbing, or an unverified internet remedy. An aggressive treatment can damage the finish or make the problem harder to assess.

## Joints, Skeleton, And Standing Systems

A poseable skeleton is not maintenance-free, and every joint is not meant to move through the same range. Move the body slowly, support its weight, and avoid forcing resistance.

Pay attention to:

- a sudden change in joint resistance
- grinding, clicking, or movement that was not present before
- a limb that no longer holds a previously supported position
- skin or material folding sharply around a joint
- pressure around standing bolts or supported foot systems
- repeated strain from lifting by one limb or the head

Standing feet do not make unattended standing universally safe. Surface, balance, footwear, body weight, and manufacturer guidance still matter. Read the [standing feet guide](/learn/standing-feet-sex-doll-guide) and [skeleton options guide](/learn/sex-doll-skeleton-options) before choosing or using those features.

## Hair, Eyes, Makeup, And The Face

The face often combines delicate finishes and removable parts. Use clean hands and minimal force.

- Confirm whether the wig is removable and how it should be stored.
- Keep adhesives and hair products away from the face unless approved.
- Follow specific guidance for implanted hair rather than treating it like a wig.
- Do not pull eyelashes, eyebrows, or facial hair while cleaning.
- Confirm whether eyes are removable before attempting to reposition them.
- Keep unknown cleaners away from makeup, paint, and textured finishes.

The [implanted hair versus wig guide](/learn/implanted-hair-vs-wig) explains the ownership tradeoffs and compatibility questions in more detail.

## Electronics And Powered Features

Heating, sound, powered oral functions, sensors, speakers, cameras, motors, and electronic heads require a separate maintenance boundary.

Before cleaning or storage, confirm:

- whether power should be disconnected
- whether batteries are removable
- which components can be detached
- where ports, seams, microphones, speakers, and ventilation openings are located
- whether any component is water-resistant and what that rating actually covers
- charging and long-storage instructions
- software, app, account, and update requirements
- who supplies replacement parts or repair support

Do not immerse an electronic head or assume a powered feature can be cleaned like the surrounding material. If moisture reaches a powered component, stop using it and ask for the exact next step.

## Monthly Check

A monthly reminder is a practical owner habit, not a universal manufacturer requirement. It is useful when the doll has been stored for a while or used regularly.

Review:

- the storage position and support points
- surface condition and any pressure marks
- light-colored storage fabric for moisture or transferred color
- joints, feet, fingers, toes, seams, and high-use areas
- wigs, implanted hair, eyes, and makeup
- powered components, cables, chargers, and connectors
- cleaning, drying, powder, and repair supplies
- whether the handling route still feels safe

Keep a simple dated note or private photo when you notice a change. That makes it easier to explain what happened and whether it is progressing.

## After Long Storage

Do not immediately lift, pose, power, or dress a doll that has been stored for an extended period. First inspect the storage area and product in place.

Check for moisture, odor, dye transfer, dust, pressure, pests, temperature exposure, loose supports, and cable or battery issues. Move the doll slowly and support its weight. Clean only after confirming what the material and components need.

If a powered component has been unused for a long time, follow its charging and startup guidance. Do not improvise with a replacement charger.

## When You Find A Cut, Tear, Stain, Or Mechanical Concern

The first repair step is documentation, not glue.

1. Stop stretching, posing, or loading the affected area.
2. Photograph the issue in clear light from several distances.
3. Record the product, material, location, approximate size, and when it appeared.
4. Note any recent clothing, cleaning product, impact, heat, pressure, or powered-feature use.
5. Keep the area clean, dry, and supported.
6. Ask DollWow or the manufacturer which repair path is compatible.

TPE and silicone repair materials are not interchangeable. Color, formulation, finish, location, and internal structure can change the method. Opening a powered component or forcing a joint can turn a small concern into a larger one.

## Common Maintenance Mistakes

### Using one routine for every material

TPE, silicone, painted finishes, mixed construction, hair systems, and electronics can require different care.

### Storing before the doll is fully dry

Moisture can remain in folds, seams, internal areas, hairline zones, and removable parts after the visible surface looks dry.

### Leaving dark clothing on during storage

Time and pressure can increase dye-transfer risk even when a garment caused no immediate mark.

### Forcing joints or lifting by one limb

Support the body and move slowly. Resistance is a reason to stop, not apply more force.

### Treating standing feet as permission for unattended display

Standing support depends on the exact product, surface, balance, footwear, and instructions.

### Applying an unknown repair product

The wrong glue, solvent, pigment, or heat can damage the material or complicate a professional repair.

### Waiting until a small issue grows

Early photographs and a clear description give support teams more options than a repair attempted after further stress.

## FAQs

### What maintenance does a sex doll need?

Most dolls need material-safe cleaning, complete drying, careful storage, surface inspection, stain prevention, and supported handling. Hair systems, standing features, skeletons, electronics, and mixed-material builds can add separate steps.

### How often should I maintain a sex doll?

Clean and dry the relevant areas after use and before storage. Inspect the doll whenever it is handled, dressed, cleaned, or moved. A monthly reminder can help owners review a stored doll, but the exact routine should follow the product instructions and actual use.

### Is sex doll maintenance difficult?

It does not need to be complicated, but it must fit the doll's weight, material, storage setup, and features. A product that is difficult to move or dry in your home can create more work than expected.

### Do silicone dolls need less maintenance than TPE dolls?

Not universally. Silicone and TPE have different formulations, finishes, stain risks, repair systems, and care recommendations. Compare the exact product rather than assuming one material is maintenance-free.

### Should I powder a TPE sex doll?

Some TPE products use a compatible renewal powder after complete drying, but the product and frequency vary. Confirm the manufacturer's guidance and keep powder away from moisture, electronics, hair, and areas where it is not recommended.

### Can I leave clothes on a sex doll?

Dark, tight, or unwashed clothing can transfer dye, especially during long contact or storage. Use washed garments, light-colored barriers, and regular checks, and avoid storing the doll in risky clothing.

### Can a sex doll stand for storage?

Only when the exact product, feet, skeleton, surface, balance, and manufacturer instructions support that method. Standing feet do not make unattended standing or long-term standing universally safe.

### How do I care for an electronic head?

Keep water away from motors, ports, speakers, microphones, sensors, wiring, and power components. Follow the exact detachment, cleaning, charging, ventilation, and storage instructions for that head.

### What should I do if I find a small tear?

Stop stressing the area, keep it clean and dry, take clear photographs, record the location and size, and ask for product-specific guidance. Do not apply generic glue because TPE and silicone repair systems differ.

### What should I keep in a sex doll care kit?

Keep only products confirmed for the exact doll, plus clean light-colored cloths, drying materials, approved storage barriers, instructions, and support details. Add compatible powder or specialized tools only when recommended.
