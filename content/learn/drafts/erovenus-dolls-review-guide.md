## Erovenus Brand Background

Erovenus describes its development as following the creation of LoveNestle in 2023. The current brand focuses on silicone torso products and positions detailed sculpting, handcrafted surface texture, and layered body painting as core features.

The manufacturer uses names such as Infinity Craft for its surface-detail and painting approach. Those names describe Erovenus's own production system; they are not independent material standards. A buyer should translate the marketing into visible questions: What does the exact product look like from several angles? Which areas are painted? How should the finish be cleaned, protected from pressure, and repaired?

Erovenus also presents multiple product families and sizes. Series names can help navigate the range, but they do not replace the exact dimensions, product form, material, and care sheet.

## Silicone Construction And Surface Finish

Erovenus presents its current dolls as silicone products. The brand emphasizes soft silicone, hand-finished texture, and multiple layers of body painting. Evaluate those features on the exact model rather than assuming every finish is identical.

### Surface texture

Look at close photographs under neutral light. Texture should remain coherent across the torso, limbs or cut edges, hands where included, and other detailed areas. Highly directional lighting can exaggerate or hide texture.

### Body painting

Layered color can add visual depth, but paint also needs compatible cleaning and careful pressure management. Ask which areas receive special painting and whether a chosen skin tone, freckles, tan line, or other option changes the care instructions.

### Softness

Softness is not a universal measurement. Body geometry, silicone formulation, internal support, and softer areas can all change the feel and weight. A manufacturer label such as ultra-soft should be tied to the exact product and current specification.

### Durability

No soft silicone body is immune to pressure marks, punctures, abrasion, stains, or tears. Reviews are more useful when they explain how long the product was owned, how it was stored, and which areas experienced wear.

## Dimensions, Weight, And Storage

Height is only the first measurement for an Erovenus torso. Width, depth, circumference, base shape, and weight determine whether the product can be lifted, cleaned, dried, and stored comfortably.

Plan four spaces before ordering:

1. The delivery route from the door to a private room
2. A stable cleaning surface with enough turning room
3. An open drying area where moisture can be removed completely
4. A relaxed storage position without sharp bends or hard pressure points

Ask for pounds and kilograms plus feet/inches and centimeters when the information is missing. A shorter silicone product can still be dense. Do not infer weight from photographs or height alone.

## Cleaning And Material Compatibility

Erovenus's official care guidance recommends gentle cleaning with warm water and mild soap or detergent, followed by complete drying. Its support guidance also warns that silicone-based products can damage its silicone material and recommends water-based lubricant where relevant.

For the exact model, confirm:

- Which cleaner is approved
- Whether internal areas can be flushed or removed
- How to support the product during cleaning
- How to dry seams, openings, and deeper areas
- Whether any surface treatment is recommended
- Which fabrics, dyes, oils, solvents, or silicone-based products to avoid

Do not seal the product in storage while moisture remains. Do not use a generic TPE repair solvent on silicone. Follow the instructions supplied with the product and ask support before experimenting with a cleaner or repair method.

Read the [cleaning guide](/learn/how-to-clean-a-sex-doll) for the baseline routine.

## Finish, Clothing, And Stain Prevention

Detailed body painting makes stain prevention especially important. Wash new dark clothing separately, test uncertain fabrics on a hidden or supplied sample where possible, and use light-colored barriers for long contact.

Avoid tight straps, hard seams, metal hardware, or accessories pressing into one area during storage. Photography styling is not proof that an outfit is safe for extended contact or included with the product.

If a mark appears, stop and ask for material-specific guidance. Aggressive scrubbing or an incompatible solvent can turn a small stain into finish damage.
