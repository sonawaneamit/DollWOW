# Comparing Black Friday Doll Deals: A Complete-Build Worksheet

The better deal is the one that delivers the build you want on terms you understand. A larger percentage discount does not settle that question if the body, head, included features or shipping arrangement differ.

This worksheet was prepared on October 16, 2026, ahead of the seasonal sale period. It does not announce a Black Friday offer. Use [DollWOW's promotions page](/promo) for offers actually available when you shop.

## Match the product before the percentage

Put the full body and head codes at the top of each offer. Include material, construction version, finish and any weight-reduction selection. Similar photos are not enough: a gallery can show a styled configuration while the starting price covers something else.

Then match fulfillment. A ready-to-ship unit with installed options is not identical to a factory build that still needs production. Neither is automatically better value; the right choice depends on whether the specific configuration or earlier delivery matters more to you.

The [cost guide](/learn/sex-doll-cost) covers wider budget planning. This worksheet answers a narrower question: do two sale offers actually buy the same thing?

## Use one comparison sheet

| Compare | Record for each offer |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Body, head code, material and version |
| Included build | Finish, hair, skeleton and installed features |
| Wanted extras | Only the options you would actually choose |
| Eligibility | Product, region, dates and any coupon restrictions |
| Delivery | Stock location, shipping charge and current estimate |
| Total | Confirmed payable amount, with unresolved charges noted |
| Support | Published arrival, return and aftercare terms |

Do not give an unwanted free accessory its advertised retail value in your personal budget. If you would not buy it, it should not outweigh a meaningful difference in the doll itself.

Likewise, record a random extra head differently from a specified head you chose. Their usefulness to you may be quite different even if both offers say "free head."

## Normalize two hypothetical offers

Imagine Offer A starts lower but excludes the finish and hair choice you want. Offer B starts higher and includes both. These are hypothetical offers, not current retailer listings or a completed checkout.

For A, add the confirmed charges for the two wanted choices. For B, establish that the included versions really match. Then apply only the discount confirmed as eligible for each configuration and add the applicable delivery charges.

The calculation is:

**Configured product total minus the confirmed eligible discount, plus shipping and any other confirmed customer charges.**

Do not calculate a discount on the whole basket if it applies only to the base doll. Do not stack two coupons unless the written terms allow it. If one offer has an unresolved shipping charge, leave its comparison total unresolved rather than declaring it cheaper.

Here is a numerical version of that exercise. Every price, discount and shipping charge below is invented, in USD, for the same assumed body, head, wanted finish and hair. Neither column represents a seller quotation, active promotion or completed cart. Taxes and import fees are excluded from both columns, so these are comparable subtotals, not promised delivered totals.

| Hypothetical quote field | Offer A | Offer B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting product amount | $2,000 | $2,150 |
| Wanted finish and hair | $200 extra | Included |
| Product amount with wanted choices | $2,200 | $2,150 |
| Assumed eligible discount | 10% of the $2,000 base only = $200 | 5% of the $2,150 product amount = $107.50 |
| Product amount after discount | $2,000 | $2,042.50 |
| Assumed shipping charge | $100 | $50 |
| Comparable subtotal before tax/import fees | $2,100 | $2,092.50 |

Offer B's subtotal is $7.50 lower despite its smaller percentage discount and higher starting amount. Applying A's 10% to its extras too would incorrectly reduce A by another $20 and reverse the result. That is the specific eligibility question to resolve in a real sale comparison.

If either real quote has an unknown charge or different included finish, stop the comparison there. Do not borrow the hypothetical answer or fill the gap with zero. Record the offer's timestamp and time zone beside the eligibility wording, because seasonal terms can change between making the sheet and ordering.

## Read seasonal terms literally

Check the start and end date, applicable time zone, brand and product restrictions. A custom-build promotion may exclude warehouse stock. An offer for one regional warehouse may not apply to another destination.

Keep a dated copy of the visible terms and your selected options. A September factory banner is not evidence of a November DollWOW promotion, and a manufacturer-direct offer is not automatically a retailer offer.

If the promotional wording and configured total disagree, pause and ask for clarification. There is no need to complete payment merely to find out whether an uncertain benefit applies.

## Compare support without pricing promises

Read the actual [Care for Life terms](/care-for-life), shipping information and return conditions beside the total. Separate included guidance from a guaranteed repair or replacement outcome. Support can be valuable without being represented as unlimited coverage.

DollWOW's price-match review compares the complete configuration, extras, shipping and final price rather than the headline price alone. Send the competing product link, selected build, destination and dated offer details through [Price Match](/price-match). A request is not an automatic approval.

## Make the final choice calmly

Before paying, confirm that the wanted choices remain in the order summary and that the offer is still active. Recheck any changed head, material or warehouse selection because it may change eligibility.

Choose from the complete build and the total you can verify. This worksheet contains no confirmed 2026 Black Friday prices or completed checkout comparison; its hypothetical offers should not be read as current deals.
