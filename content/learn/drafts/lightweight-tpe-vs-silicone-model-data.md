# Lightweight TPE vs Silicone: How to Read the Weight Figures

"Lightweight" tells you a design has been made lighter against some reference. It does not tell you whether it weighs less than the silicone or TPE doll next to it on your shortlist.

For that decision, start with the finished configuration and the meaning of its weight figure. Height, proportions, head, internal construction and selected options all belong in the comparison. The material label alone leaves too much unanswered.

## What a published reduction can tell you

SE's [official LSTPE explanation](https://www.sedoll.com/stpe-series-introduces-a-new-custom-option-lstpe-lightweight-upgrade/) describes a lightweight foam structure inside its STPE body construction. Its published examples give different reductions for different bodies:

| SE body designation in the factory table | Standard figure | LSTPE figure | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 157H | 41 kg | 31 kg | 10 kg |
| 161F | 35 kg | 31.6 kg | 3.4 kg |
| 163E | 37 kg | 26 kg | 11 kg |

These are manufacturer-published body figures checked on 16 October 2026, not DollWOW scale measurements. They show the change within each listed body. They do not establish a matched comparison against a particular silicone doll.

Notice that the tallest of these three lightweight bodies has the lowest listed figure. That is a useful warning against guessing weight from height, or transferring the largest advertised saving to every supported model.

## Annotate the saving for your exact body

For a filled record using SE's 161F row, write: "Standard body 35 kg; LSTPE body 31.6 kg; listed reduction 3.4 kg; selected head inclusion not established by this comparison." Do not borrow the 163E's 11 kg reduction for the 161F just because both have an LSTPE option.

To compare the size of each reduction, divide by that same body's standard figure: 10 / 41 is about 24.4% for 157H, 3.4 / 35 is about 9.7% for 161F, and 11 / 37 is about 29.7% for 163E. These are calculations from the factory table, not new measurements or a material-wide weight-saving rate.

The practical conclusion is body-specific: 161F has the smallest listed saving of these three and the highest LSTPE figure, despite having the lowest standard figure. This article has not measured a matched silicone body. A cross-material winner cannot be calculated from this SE-only table.

## Use a matching sheet, not a material ranking

For each shortlisted build, record the manufacturer, full body code, head code, material combination, height, bust, waist and hips. Add the published weight exactly as labelled and the source date.

Next, ask three short questions: Does the weight include this head? Does it include these selected upgrades? Is it net product weight or packed shipping weight?

Keep approximate figures approximate. Converting a rounded pound figure into kilograms does not make it a precise scale result. Likewise, a nominal body weight is not a guarantee that every finished unit will land on the same decimal.

The [size and weight guide](/learn/sex-doll-size-weight-guide) provides broader context. This matching sheet is for the narrower question of whether your two particular builds can fairly be compared.

## A hypothetical comparison that stays honest

Imagine two adult builds of similar height. Listing A states 30 kg body-only. Listing B states 32 kg including its selected head. These numbers are hypothetical and do not represent products in the catalog.

It is tempting to call A lighter by 2 kg. That conclusion is premature: A's complete weight is missing. The useful next step is to obtain A's complete-doll figure on the same basis, not to estimate a head weight from another brand.

If that answer is unavailable, mark A "complete weight unconfirmed." You can still compare its appearance and dimensions, but should not make handling plans around an unsupported total.

## Weight is one part of manageable ownership

A lower figure may help with movement, but shape and access still matter. A long body in a narrow room can be difficult to reposition even when another, heavier object is easy to lift. Storage supports and cases add their own load.

Also ask whether the lighter construction supports the head, finish and functions you actually want. An appealing reduction is less useful if it changes a non-negotiable part of your build.
