# WM S-TPE: What the Factory Label Does and Does Not Tell You

WM uses the name S-TPE for a material offering, but the name alone does not tell you how a particular doll compares with standard TPE. The useful comparison is the same body, equivalent head and selected options, with a specification for each material version.

Choose from those details rather than assuming the newer-sounding label is automatically lighter, safer or longer-lasting.

## Start with WM's actual terminology

WM's official site published a post titled [New Material S-TPE](https://wmdollshop.com/news/new-material-stpe/) on 6 January 2024. The accessible page presents a video rather than a written matched-body specification table. It establishes the manufacturer's name for the offering, not a numerical performance advantage.

When a listing says STPE without the hyphen, ask whether it refers to that WM offering and which exact body supports it. Do not expand the acronym into a chemical formulation from an unrelated industrial product or another manufacturer's marketing.

The broader [TPE versus silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) covers the main material decision. This article decodes WM's label and the evidence needed to support a claim made beside it; it does not report a tested material winner.

| Wording you see | What is established | What still needs evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WM S-TPE | The manufacturer's published name for its offering | Exact supported model/version and supplied construction |
| STPE without punctuation | A seller has used that spelling | Whether it refers to WM's offering rather than another brand's formulation |
| Lightweight | A weight-related claim is being made | Same-body figures with head/options and weight basis stated |
| Softer or more durable | A performance claim is being made | Defined comparison and relevant method, not the label alone |

For example, an offer labelled S-TPE with a silicone head identifies two separate selections: the body-material label and the head construction. It does not become a full-silicone doll, nor does that combination establish a weight advantage. This is a hypothetical label-reading example, not a verified configuration available on every WM body.

## Record the specification behind the label

Write down the full body code and measurements before comparing prices or weight. Two dolls at the same height may have different proportions or internal construction. A smaller body made from one material cannot establish that material's weight advantage over a larger body.

Match the head too. A TPE body with a silicone head is a different combination from a TPE head and body. If the head changes between offers, record that difference rather than attributing the whole result to the body material.

When a seller claims a difference from standard TPE, use this worksheet to request the missing specifications. No matched pair or numerical result is supplied here:

| Detail | Standard TPE offer | S-TPE offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact body and version | Copy the full designation | Confirm whether it matches |
| Head code and material | Record separately | Record separately |
| Weight and what it includes | Body, complete doll or packed | Use the same basis |
| Skeleton, hands and feet | List installed construction | Identify any differences |
| Surface finish | Name the selected finish | Name the selected finish |
| Care and repair guidance | Obtain model-specific instructions | Obtain material-specific instructions |
| Configured total | Save selected options and date | Use the same currency and date |

An unanswered cell means the comparison is unfinished. It does not mean that feature is absent or included free.

## What would support a softness or durability claim?

A descriptive video can help you understand what a manufacturer is demonstrating. It cannot, by itself, provide a repeatable softness score, tear-strength comparison or expected service life.

For a numerical claim, look for the material version, test method, sample conditions and units. For a body-weight comparison, look for the same body and installed configuration on both sides. For longevity, a short demonstration is not evidence of years of ownership.

You can still choose a material without a laboratory comparison. Just distinguish a preference for the demonstrated appearance from a measured performance conclusion. Neither requires pretending that every difference has been quantified.

## Check the care implications before the upgrade price

Ask whether the selected material changes the cleaning products, surface treatment, storage guidance or repair method you should use. Keep instructions with the order record so a later repair question starts from the right material.

Do not transfer a care routine from another brand merely because its material name looks similar. Equally, do not assume a material upgrade makes the doll stain-proof or removes the need for careful handling. Those would be separate claims needing their own evidence.

If the proposed build includes heating or another installed function, check that combination as well. The material selection and function selection must describe one supported product.

## Decide from the complete offer

The sensible upgrade is the one whose documented differences matter to you at its actual configured cost. If the differences you care about remain unconfirmed, ask for clarification before paying for the label.
