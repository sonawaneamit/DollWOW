# Holiday Doll Delivery: A Stock and Timing Checklist for 2026

If a particular arrival date matters, start with the exact unit and delivery address, not a general shipping estimate. DollWOW offers two order routes: ship from warehouse or custom-made. They have different steps before a carrier can deliver them.

This checklist was reviewed on October 16, 2026. It is a planning guide, not a Christmas-arrival promise or a list of confirmed holiday order cutoffs.

## Choose between a date and a particular build

Decide which matters more: receiving a doll by your preferred date, or having a specific body, head and custom finish. That decision makes the shortlist much simpler.

If timing comes first, begin with [US ready-to-ship listings](/shop/ready-to-ship?region=us) for a US destination, then confirm the unit's location and availability. If a particular configuration comes first, allow room for production and any applicable approval stage. Do not assume a stocked doll can receive the same changes as a new factory order.

Keep a second acceptable choice. A different available build may be more practical than asking a factory to compress an unconfirmed schedule.

## Confirm what "in stock" means

For ship-from-warehouse orders, confirm the warehouse region and that the exact completed unit is physically available. If the unit is only expected in a future shipment, do not treat that as confirmed warehouse stock. For custom-made orders, confirm the current production schedule. Check that the stock or build record matches the photographed head, body, material and installed options.

Then ask whether the unit is still available for your order and what remains before dispatch. Payment review, address clarification or a stock discrepancy can matter even when no production is required.

For the distinctions behind these labels, see [ready-to-ship versus custom dolls](/learn/ready-to-ship-vs-custom-sex-dolls). Use the actual listing's status rather than another product's delivery badge.

## Build a timeline with separate checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Useful question |
| --- | --- |
| Order review | Are the configuration and destination confirmed? |
| Production, if needed | What schedule applies to this build now? |
| Factory review, if offered | When will images be available and a reply needed? |
| Dispatch | When is the parcel expected to reach the carrier? |
| Transit | Which service and destination does the estimate cover? |
| Handoff | Who will receive the parcel, and where? |

Do not add a production estimate to a delivery estimate until you know what each already includes. Otherwise you may count a stage twice or omit it completely.

DollWOW's [shipping guide](/shipping) distinguishes warehouse orders from made-to-order builds and makes release subject to stock verification and order review. Its general estimates are a starting point for questions, not a holiday-specific commitment.

## Leave room for seasonal uncertainty

Irontech's factory FAQ warns that international logistics can slow between mid-November and February around Christmas and the Spring Festival. That is a manufacturer warning, not a prediction for every DollWOW shipment. [Irontech's shipping and holiday guidance](https://www.irontechdoll.com/help-faq/)

For your order, ask about the current route and any known closure affecting it. A factory closure and a destination carrier's non-delivery day are different constraints. A domestic stocked unit may avoid production, but it still needs order release and delivery.

An estimated date is not a reason to book an unchangeable event around an unopened parcel. If missing the date would make the purchase unsuitable, say that before committing.

## Plan a private, manageable arrival

Check the shipping address, recipient name and contact details carefully. Ask about signature requirements and the service's delivery location rather than assuming a parcel can be left unattended or carried upstairs.

Confirm packed dimensions and weight so you can prepare space and suitable help. Plain packaging protects presentation, but a large parcel still needs an arrival plan. Arrange time to inspect it instead of leaving it sealed until a holiday gathering has passed.

Keep packaging and the order reference. DollWOW's shipping guidance asks for delivery damage to be reported within 24 hours, with photographs retained for review. Follow the linked policy for the actual claim process.

## Send one clear timing request

A useful request includes the product link, exact configuration, destination postcode, preferred arrival date and whether another stocked build is acceptable. Ask for stock location, expected dispatch and the relevant transit estimate in the same reply.

Proceed only when the stock and timing information supports your plan. Otherwise, choose another available unit or allow more time; this October review does not establish a November or December order cutoff.
