## Choose the Product Path Before the Product

A large catalog becomes easier to use when you make four decisions in order.

### 1. Product form

Decide whether you want a full doll, torso, hips product, standalone head, or another format. These products solve different needs and should not share one price comparison.

A full doll offers complete body scale and posing but needs more delivery, cleaning, and storage planning. A torso or hips product can be more compact and easier to move, but it does not provide the same presentation or handling experience.

### 2. Material

Choose a starting path between TPE, full silicone, silicone-head, or hybrid construction. The material affects feel, finish, weight, care, repair, options, and price, but the exact formulation and body still matter.

### 3. Fulfillment

Choose between a confirmed existing unit and a made-to-order build. Ready to ship can reduce the production stage, while custom ordering gives more control over supported options. Neither route is automatically better.

### 4. Handling limit

Set a maximum weight you can lift, turn, clean, and store. Read pounds and kilograms together with the height and body measurements. Flexible weight is harder to manage than the same weight in a rigid box.

## TPE, Silicone, and Hybrid Options

### TPE for softness and value

TPE is often chosen for a softer feel and a lower starting price. Compare formulation, firmness, body construction, listed weight, stain precautions, powder guidance, and material-specific repair.

### Full silicone for finish and detail

Full silicone is often chosen for precise sculpt detail, controlled surface finish, and simpler surface cleaning. It can cost more and may be heavier. Confirm whether the complete body and head are silicone and whether any softness or weight-reduction options apply to the exact model.

### Silicone-head and hybrid builds

A silicone head on a TPE body or another hybrid design can combine priorities. Verify the exact materials, head-body connection, tone match, neck seam, weight, and care instructions.

Read the complete [TPE versus silicone guide](/learn/tpe-vs-silicone-sex-dolls) before treating either material as a universal winner.

## Full Doll, Torso, or Compact Build?

Product form changes more than price.

For a full doll, plan the delivery route, safe lifting, cleaning position, clothing, and supported storage. For a torso or hips product, compare width, depth, base shape, internal structure, insert design where applicable, cleaning access, and how it will be stored without pressure damage.

For a compact full doll, verify adult-only presentation, exact height, weight, shoulder and hip width, and product form. A shorter doll can still be dense or difficult to handle. Read the [mini and compact doll guide](/learn/mini-sex-dolls) for the category boundaries and practical checks.

## Ready to Ship Versus Made to Order

For a ready-to-ship listing, ask:

- Is the exact unit physically in the stated warehouse?
- Which head, body, material, tone, and options are already fixed?
- Do the images show the exact unit or a manufacturer reference?
- What is the expected dispatch stage after confirmation?
- Are signature, stairs, elevator, or delivery-handoff details important?

For a made-to-order listing, ask:

- Which options are supported on this exact body and head?
- Which choices conflict with each other?
- What changes the price or production estimate?
- When does the configuration become final?
- Are factory photographs or video available before shipment?

## Common Mistakes

### Mixing product forms in one price comparison

A torso, hips product, compact full doll, and standard full doll should not be compared as interchangeable purchases.

### Treating all TPE or silicone as identical

Material families contain different formulations and constructions. Compare the exact product and manufacturer guidance.

### Assuming ready to ship means customizable

An existing unit usually has a fixed body, head, tone, and installed options. Confirm what can still change before paying.

### Choosing a custom build from a supplier-wide option list

Not every option works with every body or head. Product-specific support and compatibility must control the configuration.

### Letting a discount replace due diligence

Verify the order, delivered total, support, and arrival process. A larger coupon does not correct an unsuitable or incomplete product match.
